# Political Landscape

## The Prince and His Court

**Marcus Whitmore** (Toreador, @ID[PNJ-02]) has ruled since 1962. He is elegant, prudent, and lazy. He prefers that problems solve themselves, and he's usually right, because he has Montgomery. He is not a good Prince in wartime, and he knows it. His greatest virtue is knowing whom to let do the work; his greatest flaw, not knowing when that work is being done by someone else.

## The Sheriff

**Ethan Montgomery** (Gangrel, @ID[PNJ-01]) is the law in Seattle. His pack, Whitmore's Wolves (@ID[COT-01]), includes mortal bounty hunters, his childe Hal, and, as an outside ally, David. He is loyal to the Prince but doesn't respect him, and the Primogen know it.

## The Primogen

| Clan | Primogen | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| Ventrue | Charles Halloway (@ID[PNJ-03]) | Wants the crown. Talks about “modernizing the Masquerade.” |
| Toreador | Solène Marchetti (@ID[PNJ-14]), also Keeper of Elysium | Loyal to the Prince out of habit. Neutral by trade. |
| Tremere | Dr. Anneliese Voight (@ID[PNJ-15]), Regent of the Chantry | Worried about FIRSTLIGHT. Trusts neither Halloway nor Whitmore. |
| Nosferatu | Abel Thornquist (@ID[PNJ-13]) | Sells to everyone. His vote is the most expensive in the city. |
| Malkavian | Wendell Fry (@ID[PNJ-16]) | Holds the seat “out of habit.” Nobody knows how he'll vote, not even him. |

*The Brujah and Gangrel seats have been empty since those clans broke with the Camarilla.*

## Outside the Camarilla

- **The Everett Anarchs** (@ID[COT-03]), led by Rosa Iturbe (@ID[PNJ-11]). About fifteen Kindred. Montgomery has been watching them since 2025, and Halloway uses them as a scapegoat.
- **The remnants of the Sabbat in Tacoma.** Few, old, and hidden. See @ID[LUG-11] and @ID[GAN-09].
- **The dockside thin-bloods.** A dozen of them, protected by Thornquist in exchange for favors. Jun Takeda (@ID[PNJ-20]) is one of them.
- **The independents.** David (@ID[PJ-01]) is the best known. Tacoma also has a Hecata family with a funeral home that minds its own business and buries whoever it's paid to.

## Outside the Blood

- **FIRSTLIGHT, Puget Cell** (@ID[LUG-14]). Special Agent Dana Okafor (@ID[PNJ-18]) and analyst S. Hale (@ID[PNJ-21]), with a six-person tactical team (@ID[MEN-02]).
- **Gale Sentinel** (@ID[LUG-07]). Home security company owned by Preston Gale (@ID[PNJ-19]), Halloway's ghoul.
- **Helen Ward** (@ID[PNJ-04]) and her podcast. More than forty thousand listeners per episode, and an archive nobody should have.
