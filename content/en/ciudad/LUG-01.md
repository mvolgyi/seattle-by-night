# LUG-01 · The Harbor Island Hangar

*Elliott Bay · David Smith's Haven · Map: yes*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
Between shipping containers stacked like giant dominoes stands a corrugated-metal hangar with the faded logo of a logistics company that no longer exists. From outside it looks abandoned. Inside it smells of gun oil, salt, and reheated coffee. The lights are work lights, hanging from cables. In one corner, a coffee maker and an army cot. In another, ammo boxes sorted by caliber. From the back dock you can hear the water slapping against the pilings.
:::

### 📍 Safe Heaven Location

- **Area:** Port of Seattle, Elliott Bay, docks/industrial hangar zone.
- **Approximate coordinates:** **47.615° N, 122.35° W** (roughly in the Interbay–West Seattle/Harbor Island sector).
- **Features:** Heavy-tonnage hangar, sea and underground access, close to Port of Seattle facilities. *(The doc cites as a reference the Northwest Seaport Alliance's port trucker guide: “Seattle Harbor Truckers Guide,” 03/31/2022.)*

**🧠 Narrative Use**

- This spot is the “hidden haven”: a hangar all but forgotten in the industrial section of the port, equipped with cargo tunnels, sea access, and armor plating.
- Ideal for tactical prep scenes or vampire ambushes: its harbor location allows for ships, containers, nighttime traffic, and the noise of cranes.
- Despite being in an active part of the city, its position on an industrial pier offers enough isolation for a clandestine Kindred.

### Differences from Docs 00–16

- **Haven 4 / Haven 3.** David's sheet gives Safe Haven 4; doc 05 gives Haven 3 with Sea Access and Armory.
- **Coordinates.** 47.615° N, 122.35° W falls on the north shore of Elliott Bay (the Belltown/Interbay area), not on Harbor Island, which lies farther south (around 47.57° N). Doc 05 places it on Harbor Island, with the tunnel coming out next to Terminal 46.
- **The safe.** Doc 17 mentions a “large safe/TACSAFE in the room”; doc 05 puts the safe in Bear's room (@ID[DOC-12]). They may be the same one.
- **Layout.** The earlier sources don't describe the floor plan (armored room, Bear's room, covered dock with motorboat, 200-meter tunnel): that comes from doc 05.
