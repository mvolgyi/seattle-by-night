# LUG-16 · The Terminal 86 Grain Elevator

*North of Downtown · Silos and conveyor gallery · Map: yes*

*Chapter 3 doesn't describe this location. What follows comes from @ID[A3-E03] and the Part 3 ID registry (doc 12); on the domain map (@ID[AYU-01]) it's point ⑯ “Terminal 86.”*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The service stairs zigzag up the outside of the silo to a long, narrow gallery of sheet metal and dirty glass. Inside, a stalled conveyor belt runs from one end to the other. It smells of old grain and the sea. At the far end, a hatch stands open over the bay, letting in the wind and the rain, and in front of it is a chair with someone tied to it. Sitting on the belt, elbows on his knees and gray coat hanging open, a man with a shaved head watches you arrive. He doesn't get up. “Come,” he says to David. “Sit down. I've wanted to ask you something for fifty years.”
:::

## Storyteller Notes {narrador}

The “Read Aloud” is the arrival sidebar from @ID[A3-E03].

The location is the Terminal 86 grain elevator, north of Downtown: a row of forty-meter concrete silos by the water, with a conveyor gallery up top linking the silos to the pier. @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] waits in the gallery. The hostage, if there is one, is tied to a chair beside an open loading hatch over the bay, thirty meters above the water.

**Access:** there are three ways in: the outside stairs (the ones David uses), the inside of the neighboring silo (Athletics, Difficulty 3, to climb), and the water (coming in by boat beneath the hatch).

**The hatch:** anyone who falls through it takes 4 points of bludgeoning damage on hitting the water, thirty meters below.

**Scene:** @ID[A3-E03] (Night 7, @reloj{7}, Wednesday, November 18, midnight).
