## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The service stairs zigzag up the outside of the silo to a long, narrow gallery of sheet metal and grimy glass. Inside, a stopped conveyor belt runs end to end. It smells of old grain and the sea. At the far end, a hatch hangs open over the bay, letting in the wind and the rain, and facing it is a chair with someone tied to it. Sitting on the belt, elbows on his knees, gray coat hanging open, a man with a shaved head watches you arrive. He doesn't get up. “Come,” he tells David. “Sit. I've wanted to ask you something for fifty years.”
:::

## Storyteller Notes {narrador}

The Read Aloud is the arrival sidebar from @ID[A3-E03].

The location is the Terminal 86 grain elevator, north of downtown: a row of forty-meter concrete silos by the water, with a conveyor gallery up top that links the silos to the pier. @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] waits in the gallery. The hostage, if there is one, is tied to a chair beside an open loading hatch over the bay, thirty meters above the water.

**Access:** there are three ways in: the outside stairs (the ones the meeting calls for, in Krell's line of sight), the inside of the neighboring silo (Athletics, Difficulty 3, to climb), and the water (bringing a boat in under the hatch).

**The hatch:** anyone who falls through it takes 4 blunt damage on hitting the water, thirty meters down.

**Scene:** @ID[A3-E03] (Night 7, @reloj{7}, Wednesday, November 18, midnight).
