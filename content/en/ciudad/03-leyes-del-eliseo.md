# The Laws of the Seattle Elysium

The Glass Gallery (@ID[LUG-03]) enforces the Traditions of the Camarilla and five customs of its own. Solène Marchetti enforces all five and logs every infraction in her register (@ID[DOC-03]).

## The Five Customs

- **The peace of the glass.** No violence and no hostile Disciplines inside the Gallery. *Infraction:* immediate expulsion, loss of 1 dot of Status, and, if there is damage, a trial before the Prince.
- **The presentation.** Every Kindred who arrives in the city presents themself to the Keeper before their third night. *Infraction:* they are considered a trespasser, and the Sheriff may hunt them.
- **Those who serve.** The mortals and ghouls on the Gallery's staff are untouchable, however appetizing they may be. *Infraction:* a major debt to the Prince.
- **No connected device crosses the threshold.** Phones, smartwatches, earbuds: everything is switched off and handed over at the entrance. Voight imposed this custom in 2024. *Infraction:* confiscation and a note in the register. On the second offense, loss of 1 dot of Status.
- **What is said among the glass.** Nothing said in the Gallery is repeated outside without the Keeper's permission. *Infraction:* Marchetti stops receiving the offender until further notice, which in practice cuts them out of the city's political life.
