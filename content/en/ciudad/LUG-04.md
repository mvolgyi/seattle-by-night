# LUG-04 · The Sealed Underground (Seattle Underground)

*Pioneer Square · Abel Thornquist's domain · Map: yes*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The guided tours cover three blocks of the Seattle Underground. This isn't that. Past an iron door that appears on no blueprint, the sidewalks of 1889 run on beneath the ground, with boarded-up storefronts, hand-painted signs, and violet glass skylights that let a dirty light down from the street above. It smells of mold, of damp, and, every so often, of server ozone. A network cable climbs along the ceiling like a vine.
:::
