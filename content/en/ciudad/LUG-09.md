# LUG-09 · Club Cinder

*Capitol Hill · The Toreador rack · Map: no*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
A black door with no sign on a street full of bars. Inside, an exposed-brick basement with a small stage and a crowd that dances with their eyes closed. The music is slow and low, with a bass line that gets inside your chest. Under the red lights, everyone looks a little paler than they should. Someone at the bar is writing on a napkin.
:::
