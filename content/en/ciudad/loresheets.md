# Loresheets

These three Loresheets are tied to Seattle and to the adventure. They are bought with experience points like any Background. A character can only have one Loresheet at level 4 or 5, unless the Storyteller says otherwise.

## LOR-01 · The Doctrine of Controlled Fury {#LOR-01}

*“Locate. Assess. Execute. Everything that comes after the first blow is the Beast asking permission.”*

In the '50s, the Brujah Marcus “Ironjaw” Vance (@ID[PNJ-06]) developed a method of combat and discipline for hunting Kindred without losing control of one's own Beast. He taught it to very few, in brutal sessions with cold water and broken bones. Today the doctrine survives in a handful of Camarilla enforcers on three continents. In Seattle, two know it: the one who finished the lesson and the one who didn't.

**Prerequisite:** trained by Vance or by someone who has this Loresheet at level 4 or higher.

| Level | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| • | **Locate** | When you pursue a specific target (a Kindred you were sent to find), gain +1 die on Awareness and Investigation tests to find them. |
| •• | **Assess** | Once per scene, after observing a Kindred for one turn, make a Wits + Insight test against their Composure + Subterfuge. On a win, the Storyteller tells you their Hunger level and whether they'll try to flee, fight, or negotiate. |
| ••• | **Execute** | Against a target you have assessed (level 2) this scene, your first attack gains +2 dice. If that first attack misses, you can't use this effect again this scene. |
| •••• | **The Full Guard** | You recognize the doctrine the moment you see it (@pista{P-03}, @pista{P-08}). A rival using the inverted guard loses 2 dice from their melee defense pools against you. In addition, gain +1 die to resist fury frenzy during combat. |
| ••••• | **Heir of the Fist** | You can teach the doctrine up to level 3. Once per story, spend 1 point of Willpower to automatically succeed on a test to resist frenzy in combat. The Camarilla's Archons know your name, for better and for worse. |

**Tests:** Assess: @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.insight|0|Wits + Insight (vs. Composure + Subterfuge)} · the target resists with @tirada{attributes.composure+skills.subterfuge|0|Composure + Subterfuge}.

*Note: David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), an NPC, has this Loresheet at level 4. Krell (@ID[PNJ-12]) has level 2 and the inverted guard, without level 4. A PC can buy it with David as mentor, since he meets the prerequisite: normally up to level 3. But David chooses whom he teaches. If Krell learns the other guard (@ID[A3-E03]), David goes up to level 5.*

## LOR-02 · The Seattle Underground {#LOR-02}

*“The city above is the one you see. The one below is the one that remembers.”*

Since 1889, there have been buried streets, storefronts, and sidewalks beneath Pioneer Square. Tourists see three blocks. The Nosferatu see the rest: tunnels connecting basements, sewers, old steam conduits, and bricked-up passages that run all the way to the port. Whoever knows the underground knows a city that appears on no map.

**Prerequisite:** none, though levels 4 and 5 require permission from Thornquist (@ID[PNJ-13]) or his successor.

| Level | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| • | **Shortcuts** | Downtown and in Pioneer Square, gain +2 dice on tests to escape a pursuit or reach a place first by going beneath the streets. |
| •• | **The Undermarket** | You have access to the Undermarket. Once per story, you can buy a specific piece of information in exchange for a minor favor to the Moles (@ID[COT-04]). |
| ••• | **Sealed Haven** | You know a bricked-up passage that only you know how to open. It works as an additional Haven 1, with the Hidden advantage, and it's less than ten minutes from any point downtown. |
| •••• | **The Mother** | You know where the Mother Below sleeps. Once per chronicle, you can wake her for a moment to ask her a question. She knows things from before 1889. She sets the price. |
| ••••• | **Architect Below** | You can open and close sections of the Sealed Underground. Once per story, you can create a new Portillon for your coterie (+1 Portillon in a downtown domain). The Nosferatu of Seattle treat you as if you were clan. |

*Location: the Sealed Underground (@ID[LUG-04]).*

## LOR-03 · The City's Eyes {#LOR-03}

*“We see what the city doesn't.” (Gale Sentinel slogan)*

Seattle has more cameras than streetlights: doorbells, traffic, public transit, stores, delivery drones. Almost all of them are connected. Kindred who understand this network can use it to their advantage. Those who don't end up in a file.

**Prerequisite:** Technology 2 or higher, or a contact who has it.

| Level | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| • | **Blind Spots** | You know which parts of the city have fewer cameras and at what hours they fail. Gain +1 die on Stealth against electronic surveillance. |
| •• | **Access** | Once per session, make an Intelligence + Technology test (Difficulty 3) to view doorbell and traffic camera footage of a specific location from the last 72 hours. |
| ••• | **Erase** | You know how to make yourself disappear from footage. Once per story, when FIRSTLIGHT's Thermometer would rise because of a camera, it doesn't. |
| •••• | **Cold Subject** | You know Gale Sentinel's ANOMALY model and how to fool it with thermal clothing, heat, or interference. ANOMALY cameras don't register the members of your coterie if you prepare beforehand (one Intelligence + Technology test, Difficulty 4, per scene). |
| ••••• | **The Eye Inside** | You have a trusted contact inside Gale Sentinel or Northline. Once per story, you can learn the current value of the Thermometer and FIRSTLIGHT's next target. |

**Tests:** Access: @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|3|Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 3)} · Cold Subject: @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|4|Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 4)}.

*Note: Ezra Lund (@ID[PJ-03]) is the natural candidate for this Loresheet. If he discovers that Gale's firmware is his (@ID[A1-E05] or @ID[A3-E02]), he can buy level 4 without needing training.*
