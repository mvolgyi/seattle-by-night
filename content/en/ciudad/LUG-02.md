# LUG-02 · The Sheriff's Farm and Boat Landing

*Snohomish Valley, 40 minutes north · Ethan Montgomery's haven · Map: yes*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The gravel road ends at a dark-timbered farmhouse surrounded by fields flooded with rain. There are dogs, a lot of them, and they don't bark: they watch. Behind the house, through the trees, the river glints. The man waiting for you on the porch doesn't get up from his rocking chair. His eyes are a color that isn't quite human.
:::

## Storyteller Notes {narrador}

**Layout:** main house, a barn converted into the Wolves' meeting hall, a basement with a tunnel roughly 300 meters long leading to a private boat landing on the Snohomish River, and kennels holding a dozen ghoul dogs.

**Backgrounds:** Haven 3 and Herd 2 (the bounty hunters, @ID[MEN-03], who live in trailers behind the barn).

**Scenes:** @ID[A1-E03] (the coterie forms) and @ID[A3-E04] (the pack wavers). It's one of the possible targets in @ID[A4-E02].

**Detail:** Montgomery receives visitors sitting on the porch, even in the rain. If he invites someone inside, the matter is serious.
