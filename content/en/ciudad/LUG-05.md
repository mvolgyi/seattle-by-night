# LUG-05 · Helen Ward's Apartment

*Ballard, Market Street · Map: yes*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
Third floor, above a closed-down hardware store. A one-bedroom apartment someone has turned into an investigation room: walls covered in maps stuck with red, blue, and yellow pins, mugs of cold coffee, an ashtray on the sill of the fire-escape window. On the desk, a stripped cable hangs plugged into nothing. The woman sitting on the floor, hood up, looks toward the door and doesn't seem surprised.
:::
