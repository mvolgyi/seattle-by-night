# LUG-07 · Gale Sentinel Campus and Data Center

*Bellevue, Eastside · Map: yes*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The campus is an office park with artificial turf and benches nobody uses. In the lobby, a giant screen shows a mosaic of thousands of live doorbell feeds: porches, hallways, rain. Below it, the company motto: “We see what the city doesn't.” The building out back has no windows. It hums.
:::
