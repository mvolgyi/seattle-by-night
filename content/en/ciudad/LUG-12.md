# LUG-12 · Emily Smith's Grave

*Cemetery north of the city · Map: no*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
A plain gray granite headstone on a hillside of cedars. EMILY ROSE SMITH · 1958–1996 · “She stayed with the ones nobody wanted.” The grass is trimmed. At the foot of the stone lies a bunch of fresh flowers, wrapped in newspaper, still beaded with rain.
:::
