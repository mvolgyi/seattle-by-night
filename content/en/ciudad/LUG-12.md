# LUG-12 · Emily Smith's Grave

*Cemetery north of the city · Map: no*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
A plain gray granite headstone on a hillside of cedars. EMILY ROSE SMITH · 1958–1996 · “She took in the ones nobody wanted.” The grass is trimmed. At the foot of the stone lies a bouquet of fresh flowers, wrapped in newspaper, still beaded with rain.
:::

## Storyteller Notes {narrador}

The fresh flowers are the heart of @ID[GAN-12]. Who leaves them is left open for the chronicle (Marcos from @ID[GAN-01], someone from Salt's, or someone who signs “M.V.”).

It appears in the Epilogue of @ID[AVE-01] as one of the three possible codas.

Use as a lost Touchstone: if the coterie brings David, the visit leaves him a little more at peace, but it also makes him ask himself a question he doesn't want to ask. He may ask it out loud, to whoever brought him.
