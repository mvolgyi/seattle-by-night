# LUG-06 · Halloway Tower

*Downtown · Charles Halloway's Haven · Map: yes (penthouse and vault)*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
Forty-two stories of dark glass in the financial district. The private elevator rises without a sound and opens onto a penthouse with soaring ceilings and furniture that looks like it has never been used. The entire west wall is a window over Elliott Bay: the port cranes, the ferries, the island where the Hound lives. From up here, the city looks like a game board.
:::

## Storyteller Notes {narrador}

**Layout:** floors 1 through 38 are Halloway Meridian Capital offices (Influence and Resources); floor 40 has ghoul security (@ID[MEN-04]); the penthouse holds the Council chamber and the study; an underground vault on level −3, reachable only by the private elevator.

**The vault** holds the original of Vance's journals, copy 1 of 3, and, from Night 6 on (@reloj{6}), the File's original drive.

**Backgrounds:** Haven 4, Resources 5, Influence 4.

**Scenes:** @ID[A4-E01]. A possible infiltration before the climax, if the coterie plans one.
