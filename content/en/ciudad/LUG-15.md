# LUG-15 · The Ferry Terminal and the Last Ferry

*Colman Dock and Puget Sound · Map: yes (terminal and ferry deck)*

## Read Aloud {jugadores}

:::leer
The terminal is almost empty. Under the fluorescents there's a handful of sleepy-faced passengers, a security guard staring at his phone, and a broken coffee machine. Outside, the last ferry of the night waits with its lights on and its engines running, like a white building floating on black water. The horn sounds once, long. Five minutes.
:::
