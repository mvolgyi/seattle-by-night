# PJ-02 · Celeste Arden · Ventrue · “The Glass Heiress”

**Concept:** investment analyst and childe of Charles Halloway, the Ventrue Primogen.

## Mortal Days

Celeste grew up in Bellevue, in a house with a view of the lake that her father, a real estate developer, lost in 2008 along with everything else. She was nineteen, and she watched the money disappear first and the friends after. She swore she would never again be on the wrong side of a spreadsheet. She studied quantitative finance, joined a small fund, and by twenty-six she could spot companies about to fall before anyone else. That caught Halloway's attention.

## Kindred Nights

Halloway Embraced her in 2011 and offered her what she wanted most: never to lose again. Today she runs the network of shell companies behind Halloway Meridian Capital. She knows her sire is ambitious. She feeds only on people who have just been ruined: the Blood of someone who has lost everything is the only kind that works for her, and it always reminds her of her father.

## Connection to the Coterie

Her folder in the File is a photo of her at the scene of a suicide in a Bellevue parking garage. She was there to feed, not to kill, but the photo doesn't prove it.

## Ambition, Desire, and Touchstone

**Ambition:** for her name to be worth more than her sire's. **Desire:** for Halloway to recognize her as his equal. **Suggested Conviction:** “Never let anyone fall alone.” **Touchstone:** her mother, who lives in a care home with dementia and still calls her by her name.

## Suggested Backgrounds

Resources 3, Contacts (finance) 2, Influence (Eastside) 1, Mawla (Halloway) 2. Flaw: level 2 Blood Bond to Halloway.
