# PJ-03 · Ezra “Static” Lund · Nosferatu · “The Ghost in the Network”

**Concept:** network security engineer, Embraced by the Underground's information broker.

## Mortal Days

Ezra worked six years for a big cloud company in South Lake Union. In 2018 he discovered that thousands of Seattle doorbell cameras were sending video to a server that didn't appear in any contract. He reported it internally. Two weeks later they fired him for a made-up “policy violation,” and no company in the city ever answered him again.

## Kindred Nights

Abel Thornquist (@ID[PNJ-13]) Embraced him in 2019. He wanted someone who could see the city's digital nervous system the way he sees the tunnels. Ezra lives in the Sealed Underground (@ID[LUG-04]), among recycled servers and cables that run down through 19th-century holes. He sells information with no face and no name. His Mask is an anonymous account on a dozen forums. He feeds on blood bags he gets by hacking a blood bank's inventory (*Bagger*).

## Connection to the Coterie

The File has a thermal camera capture showing a figure with no body heat going into a manhole in Pioneer Square. It's him.

## Ambition, Desire, and Touchstone

**Ambition:** expose whoever controls the city's cameras. **Desire:** for someone to see his face and not look away. **Suggested Conviction:** “Information belongs to whoever needs it, not whoever pays for it.” **Touchstone:** his sister Maja, a night-shift nurse, who leaves food at his door even though she knows he doesn't eat it.

## Suggested Backgrounds

Contacts (hackers) 2, Herd 1, Haven 2 (in the Underground), Mawla (Thornquist) 1. Advantage: feed from bags without penalty.

## Shines In {narrador}

@ID[A1-E04], @ID[A1-E05], and @ID[A3-E02]. He's the natural specialist for @ID[LOR-03] *The City's Eyes*.

## Secret {narrador}

In 2017, as a contractor, Ezra wrote the firmware for Gale Sentinel's cameras. The back door Preston Gale uses is his code. Ezra suspects it, but he's never confirmed it.
