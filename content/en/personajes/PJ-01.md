## Concept

*Source: doc 17, “🩸 David Smith — The Hound of the Masquerade.”*

**Clan:** Brujah · **Generation:** 9th · **Sect:** Independent (operational ally of the Camarilla) · **Coterie:** Collaborator of the Seattle Sheriff

**Ambition:** Maintain the balance between the Kindred and the Masquerade.

**Desire:** Eradicate those who break the veil, even if they're former allies.

**Humanity:** 6 · **Blood Potency:** 4 · **Willpower:** 7 (Iron Will •••). Despite the horror, he still holds a tactical view of good and evil.

*“Some must burn, so that the rest can see the light.”*

David Smith is the kind of vampire they call when other vampires become a problem. His training as a Navy commando was only the beginning.

After his Embrace in 1960, he traveled for decades perfecting his personal war against the corruption of the Blood: he learned hand-to-hand combat from the Brujah of Marseille, spiritual discipline from Assamite monks in the Maghreb, and control of the body from Japanese Gangrel on Mount Osore.

His reputation is that of an incorruptible enforcer. A hound that answers not to Princes but to a code he wrote himself in blood and fire.

**In short:** Brujah veteran turned enforcer. Former Navy Commando, Embraced in 1960 in Haiti. He has served the Masquerade as a hunter of rogue Kindred on three continents. In Seattle he acts as a free agent for the Sheriff: judge, executioner, and ghost.

**Concept (sheet):** Veteran. **Affiliation (sheet):** Independent — Camarilla Collaborator. **Sire:** Marcus “Ironjaw” Vance (@ID[PNJ-06]).

### 🩸 Potence 4 — Supernatural Strength

- **Iron Fist** (Potence 1 homebrew; *Lethal Body*): Your blows hit like a jackhammer. Superficial damage converted to lethal.
- **Prowess** (Potence 2; *Prowess*): Lets you perform impossible feats of strength or lift up to a ton. Perfect for breaching, rescuing, or opening armored entry points.
- **Shattering Blow** (Potence 3 homebrew): A single concentrated impact that can bring down walls or incapacitate in one hit. Ideal as a final execution.
- **Kinetic Bullet** (Potence ••••, Celerity 2 Amalgam – Homebrew):
  - **Cost:** 1 Rouse Check to activate. Lasts one scene. Allows up to Potence (4) shots at no extra cost; more shots require new Rouse Checks (1 per additional shot).
  - **Effect:** David can throw any small/medium object (marble, stone, nut, small stake, etc.) as if it were a heavy pistol shot.
  - **Range:** up to Potence × 10 m (40 m); no penalty within half that distance.
  - **Attack:** Dexterity + Athletics + Potence (or Strength + Athletics, at the table's choice); the target uses normal defense.
  - **Damage:** as a .45 → base damage 3 + net successes; lethal against mortals/ghouls, and against Kindred without Fortitude.
  - Once per scene you can make an “Execution Shot”: +1 Diff.; with 3+ net successes, a mortal/ghoul is left dead or unconscious; a Kindred suffers a heavy penalty (-2) to the body part hit.
  - With stakes / bolts and +2 Diff., with 4+ net successes you can stake or pin the Kindred's chest (Storyteller ruling).
  - It also works for breaking cameras, lights, weapons in mortal hands, etc., with no visible firearms and no shell casings.

💬 *Note: “Iron Fist + Shattering Blow” is a valid and brutal combination, but Potence 4 is usually used with Prowess or Superhuman Assault. In your case, Shattering Blow is better for a Masquerade hunter.*

## Last Bastion of the Masquerade

*Source: doc 17. Last Bastion of the Masquerade (Fortitude ••••). Homebrew – optional Amalgam with Presence or Auspex, if you want to fine-tune it further.*

*“I don't fall until the job is done.”*

**Theme / fiction.** When David marks someone as an enemy of the Masquerade, his body and his will refuse to fall. Broken bones, bullets, fire… everything holds together on sheer refusal. It isn't blind Brujah fury: it's the brutal calm of the executioner who won't let himself die until he's finished off the condemned.

### Mechanical Version (V5-Friendly)

**Requirements**

- Fortitude 4
- Humanity 6+ (or at least 1 Conviction tied to “protecting the Masquerade / the balance”)
- Optional as an Amalgam: Presence 2 or Auspex 2 (Presence if you want it more “imposing,” Auspex if you see it as an almost mystical focus on his mission).

**Cost**

- 1 Rouse Check on activation.
- It can only be used once per scene, and once per night for its big effect.

**Activation.** You must declare a target a “Veil-breaker”:

- It must be someone who, in David's judgment (and the Storyteller's), is actively endangering the Masquerade:
  - a Kindred using Disciplines in front of mortals,
  - a Sabbat carrying out butchery,
  - a Cainite about to expose the existence of vampires, etc.
- Quick action: David locks his gaze / pronounces the “sentence” (a whisper, a stone-cold line, whatever works for you) and activates the power.

While this sentenced target remains present in the scene (or David is directly pursuing it), the power is active.

### Effects

**1. I Won't Fall Yet.** While the sentenced target remains in the scene or in direct pursuit:

- If David would be Incapacitated by damage (superficial or aggravated) from a damage roll:
  - Instead of falling, he stays at 1 superficial Health level.
  - He automatically gains the Stunned / Staggered condition (the Storyteller can impose -2 on complex physical actions, he can't take multiple actions, etc.).
  - This effect can only happen once per scene and per night.

In short: 👉 The first time someone “would drop him” while he's executing an enemy of the Masquerade… he doesn't fall. He stays standing at 1 Health.

**2. Executioner's Flesh.** While the sentenced target remains present:

- David gains +Fortitude (4) dice on tests to:
  - Resist frenzy from damage or provocation by the target.
  - Resist Dominate / Presence / Auspex from the target (or from its direct allies in the scene, at the Storyteller's discretion).
- In addition, he ignores wound penalties (as if Deny Pain were “boosted”) against that specific target.
- Outside that hunt, Deny Pain works normally.

Narrative effect: 👉 While he's hunting a real danger to the Masquerade, he doesn't break, doesn't flee, doesn't bend mentally at that enemy's hands.

**3. Moral Limit.** Because otherwise it gets out of hand:

- If the Storyteller considers that David activated it against someone who isn't really a threat to the Masquerade, or out of pure personal grudge:
  - The power works the same, but at the end of the night David must make a Remorse test with +1 die of difficulty.
  - If he fails, he gains at least 1 additional Stain: he has twisted his Conviction, using “the Masquerade” as an excuse to kill.

This nails it to the concept of an enforcer with a code, not a thug justifying himself.

### Is It Broken or Reasonable?

If you compare it with the official Fortitude 4 powers:

- Draught of Endurance: grants Fortitude and powers to another Kindred for a night.
- Gorgon Scales: grants brutal defenses, but very situational and tied to Resonance.

Last Bastion of the Masquerade:

- It's very strong, but:
  - It only works in scenes where there's a real threat to the Masquerade.
  - The “I don't fall” is once per night, and it leaves you wrecked.
  - It doesn't increase damage or grant super attacks; it only keeps David on his feet to finish the job.
  - It has a moral cost if abused.

At the table, it's a power that:

- Reinforces his role as the “Black Dog of the Masquerade.”
- Gives a very cinematic moment: the blow that should knock him to the ground… and he gets up at 1 Health with a broken jaw and a fixed stare.
- Doesn't unbalance things as much as a constant buff of raw damage reduction against everything and everyone.

### Simpler / Faster Version

If you'd prefer something even easier to use, the same concept can be reduced to:

**Lite Version:**

- **Cost:** 1 Rouse Check.
- **Effect:** Once per night, when you take damage that would leave you Incapacitated while fighting someone who's endangering the Masquerade, you stay at 1 Health instead of falling.
- **Extra:** +2 dice to resist frenzy and mental Disciplines from that enemy until the end of the scene.

The air seems to smell of gunpowder, rust, and dried blood. It's the scent that has followed him for decades, the same one that soaks into his clothes and his soul. David Smith needs neither words nor weapons to command respect. His presence alone, still but charged with pent-up tension, makes you think he's a heartbeat away from unleashing a storm.

## Tactical Synergies Between Disciplines and Skills

*Source: doc 20.*

### 1. Celerity (Quick Reflexes, Blink, Lightning Movement)

- **Optimal use:** pursuit, ambushes, evasion, and surprise attacks.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Athletics + Celerity:** lets you move from cover to cover before your enemies can react.
  - **Brawl or Melee + Blurring Rush:** you close the distance and strike in the same action, before the opponent finishes reacting.
  - **Stealth + Celerity:** move through the shadows or close distances without being seen.
- **Usage tip:** activate *Blink* the turn before attacking and *Blurring Rush* as you strike—combining them gives you an appear-and-attack sequence that's nearly impossible to stop.

:::recuadro Editor's Note
Doc 20 used Lightning Strike (*Lightning Strike*), which was dropped from the profile (see Disciplines). Where the doc combined it with Lightning Movement, Blink and Blurring Rush now go.
:::

### 2. Potence (Iron Fist, Shattering Blow)

- **Optimal use:** melee combat, physical intimidation, breaking structures or equipment.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Brawl + Iron Fist:** extra (lethal) damage even unarmed.
  - **Melee + Shattering Blow:** multiplies the weapon's damage; you break bones or armor effortlessly.
  - **Intimidation + Potence:** you can use supernatural strength to back up threats (smashing a table or door as an intimidating gesture).
- **Tactical combination:** activate *Iron Fist* and *Shattering Blow* together on attacks against tough enemies, then use *Fortitude* (Deny Pain) to weather the counterattacks.

### 3. Fortitude (Toughness, Deny Pain)

- **Optimal use:** resisting physical damage, fire, and lethal damage, and staying on your feet when others would fall.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Stamina + Fortitude:** each level adds an automatic success to your soak or endurance tests.
  - **Prolonged Combat + Deny Pain:** you can keep fighting under severe penalties.
  - **Prowess + Fortitude:** lets you keep control even in the middle of frenzy.
- **Narrative use:** David can take bullets, keep advancing, grab the enemy by the throat, and deliver a precise blow with Potence.

### 4. Presence (Instinctive Authority, Majesty of War)

- **Optimal use:** intimidate, subdue, or control the social environment in seconds.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Intimidation + Instinctive Authority:** subdue enemies or ghouls without raising your voice.
  - **Persuasion + Majesty of War (Kindle Emotions):** impose your will in social scenes or interrogations.
  - **Intimidating Stares + Presence:** useful in social combat or to force a “halt” before the fight.
- **Key combination:** before entering combat, activate *Instinctive Authority* to intimidate or scatter weak enemies. If that fails, switch to Celerity/Potence without losing rhythm.

### 5. Auspex (Heightened Senses, Premonition)

- **Optimal use:** tracking, detecting traps, anticipating attacks.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Awareness + Heightened Senses:** detect snipers, heartbeats, or slight movements.
  - **Tailing + Premonition:** you anticipate ambushes or the target's movements.
  - **Investigation + Premonition:** ideal for tracking Masquerade violators or preparing a night hunt.
- **Strategy:** activate *Premonition* before the hunt or a confrontation; you may receive visions that guide your action or give you an edge on initiative.

### 💀 Standout Tactical Combos for David Smith

| Situation | Disciplines + Skills | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent ambush** | *Celerity + Stealth + Auspex (Premonition)* | You get ahead of the victim, predict their movement, and attack before they react. |
| **Close combat (1v1)** | *Potence + Fortitude + Brawl (Tactical Combat)* | You increase damage, ignore wounds, and land devastating blows. |
| **Surprise attack from above** | *Celerity (Lightning Movement) + Athletics (Jumping)* | You launch yourself from a rooftop and strike before you're seen. |
| **Hunting a Masquerade violator** | *Auspex (Premonition) + Stealth + Presence (Instinctive Authority)* | You follow the psychic trail, locate them, and neutralize them without open combat. |
| **Against multiple enemies** | *Fortitude (Flesh of Marble) + Celerity (Quick Reflexes) + Potence (Shattering Blow)* | You take minimal damage, act before them, and bring them down one by one. |
| **Intimidating an entire room** | *Presence (Majesty of War) + Intimidation + Iron Will* | You silence everyone and impose respect or terror even among elder vampires. |

### 🧠 General Play Strategy

1. **Before the confrontation:** activate *Auspex* → analyze the surroundings.
2. **Start of the conflict:** use *Presence (Instinctive Authority)* to control the social flow or disorient enemies.

## Differences from Docs 00–16 {narrador}

The earlier sources (docs 17–20) predate docs 00–16. Where they clash, docs 00–16 win:

- **Arrival in Seattle.** Doc 17 places David in Seattle from 2010 onward (and before that, from 1970 to 2010, between Europe, the Maghreb, and Sabbat hunts). In the adventure he's already in Seattle in 1996 (@ID[REF-01]), and doc 16 marks as provisional that he arrived in the '80s. Emily's sheet (doc 24) also says he watched her from his car outside school.
- **The Sheriff.** The file cites a “relationship of respect with Sheriff O'Connor”; in docs 00–16, O'Connor stopped being Sheriff in 1978 and the Sheriff is Montgomery (@ID[PNJ-01]).
- **Haven.** The sheet gives Secure Haven 4; @ID[LUG-01] (doc 05) gives Haven 3 with Sea Access and Arsenal.
- **Age at death.** Born 03/15/1935 and dead 07/08/1960, he would have been 25; the file says 27, and the sheet 28/30.
- **Predator Type.** The profile says Silent Hunter; the file, Hunter of Hunters.
- **Touchstones.** The sheet lists Helen and Bear; docs 00–16 give Helen and Emily, with Bear as an anchor.
- **Celerity.** The sheet and doc 20 use Lightning Strike (in V5 it's *Lightning Strike*, level 5) and Lightning Movement; doc 17 swaps it for Split Second. The sheet adds Blurring Rush, with no description. Doc 20 also names Toughness and Flesh of Marble (Fortitude) and Quick Reflexes, which aren't on the sheet. By editorial decision, this profile swaps Lightning Strike for Blink and sets Blurring Rush at level 3 (see Disciplines).
- **LOR-01.** None of the earlier sources mention the Loresheet; doc 17 calls channeling the Brujah Compulsion “Controlled Fury,” while in docs 00–16 it's Vance's doctrine (@ID[LOR-01]).
