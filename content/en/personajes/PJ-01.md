# David Smith · The Hound of the Masquerade

*“Some must burn so the rest can see the light.”*

**Clan:** Brujah · **Sect:** independent, operational ally of the Camarilla · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** ally of the coterie; the emotional center of the story

## In One Sentence

ex-Navy commando Embraced in Haiti in 1960 by Marcus Vance, turned outside enforcer for the Sheriff of Seattle.

## In This Adventure {narrador}

he's the emotional center of @ID[AVE-01] and an allied NPC, like @ID[PNJ-05|Bear]: he calls the coterie together in @ID[A0-E01], Montgomery assigns him to go with them in @ID[A1-E03], and he stays with them all the way to the last ferry. Krell is his blood brother and he doesn't know it; Helen is his Touchstone; the Larchmont and Emily's night are the two times he arrived too late.

The PCs live that plot. They find out about Krell, about Rosa, about the Larchmont, and about Hal's reports. They decide what to tell David, when, and how. And they carry whatever happens next.

## How to Run Him

**He helps; he doesn't star.** If David assists on a roll, the PC rolling adds +1 die. If he does something on his own (covering an exit, opening an armored door, recognizing a guard stance), narrate it without rolling. He never solves on his own what the coterie should be solving.

**He weighs in; he doesn't decide.** Forgive, hand over, kill, lie: the players decide that. David pushes, argues, or keeps quiet. If the coterie picks something he wouldn't, he says so once, briefly, and then goes along or leaves the scene. He doesn't undercut them in front of others.

**What he doesn't know.** That Krell is Vance's childe. That Rosa gave Ray Dorsey shelter in 1996. What folios 41 and 42 say. What Hal tells Montgomery every month. Each of those things reaches David from a PC's mouth, or it doesn't reach him.

**The voice.** Short sentences, long silences, operational language: “target,” “exit,” “time.” He treats the coterie as colleagues, not subordinates. The rest is in @ID[TONO-DAVID].

**Everyone else looks at him.** Montgomery calls him “Hound.” Halloway, “Mr. Smith.” Krell talks only to him. Use it: let it bother the PCs that the city looks at David and not at them, and that they have to speak through him or make themselves heard.

## What He Does for the Coterie

- **Haven and domain.** He lends them the Harbor Island hangar (@ID[LUG-01]) as a haven. The coterie's domain is the surrounding docks.
- **The arsenal and Bear.** Weapons, vehicles, and a ghoul who drives, repairs, and covers the door (@ID[PNJ-05]).
- **Contacts.** Police and ex-military ghouls. A word from him gets them in to see Montgomery.
- **Reading violence.** He looks at a combat scene and knows who did it and how. With @ID[LOR-01] at level 4 he recognizes the inverted guard the moment he sees it.
- **The name.** “The Black Dog of the Masquerade” opens doors in the Camarilla and closes them among the Anarchs.

## When He Breaks

David doesn't roll Remorse or gain Stains: he's an NPC. What the story costs him shows at the table.

- **When they hit him where it hurts** (Emily, the Larchmont, Rosa, Vance): he goes quiet, walks out onto the dock, and doesn't come back until dawn. That night he doesn't help: no +1 die and no advice.
- **When the Beast presses** (Bear hurt, Helen in danger, an order he thinks is unjust): the Storyteller rolls the Compulsion (see “Compulsion and Bane”) or the fury frenzy. If David goes under, stopping him is the coterie's problem.
- **When he crosses a line** (kills Krell with his own hands, Dominates Helen, makes a deal with Halloway): he stops talking about the Masquerade as something worth anything. Bear notices first (@ID[DOC-12]).

## How He Changes

He doesn't spend experience or buy Backgrounds. What grows or breaks is his relationship with the coterie.

- **He trusts.** If the PCs tell him the truth when it costs them (Krell, Rosa, the Larchmont, the reports), at the end of the adventure David is Allies 2 for the coterie.
- **He stops volunteering.** If he finds out they hid something big from him, he keeps helping, but he no longer offers what he knows. They have to ask.
- **He stops trusting the Sheriff.** If Montgomery brands him a traitor (@ID[A3-E04]), David stays with the coterie and outside the law. He never calls him “Sheriff” again.
- **Mentor.** He can train a PC in Vance's doctrine. That PC meets the prerequisite for @ID[LOR-01] and can buy it with David as mentor.
- **New Convictions.** If @ID[A2-E03] or @ID[A3-E03] end well, David can gain “Debts are paid with deeds, not with blood” or “Finish what he didn't finish.” Write it down and let it show in how he talks.
- **Heir to the Fist.** If the coterie offers Krell the other guard and David teaches it to him, David raises @ID[LOR-01] to level 5 and Krell can be Allies 2 for the coterie.

## Concept

*Source: doc 17, “🩸 David Smith — The Hound of the Masquerade.”*

**Clan:** Brujah · **Generation:** 9th · **Sect:** Independent (operational ally of the Camarilla) · **Coterie:** Collaborator of the Seattle Sheriff

**Ambition:** Maintain the balance between the Kindred and the Masquerade.

**Desire:** Wipe out those who break the veil, even if they're old allies.

**Humanity:** 6 · **Blood Potency:** 4 · **Willpower:** 7 (Iron Will •••). Despite the horror, he still holds a tactical view of good and evil.

*“Some must burn, so the rest can see the light.”*

David Smith is the kind of vampire they call when other vampires become a problem. His training as a Navy commando was only the beginning.

After his Embrace in 1960, he traveled for decades perfecting his personal war against the corruption of the Blood: he learned hand-to-hand combat from the Brujah of Marseille, spiritual discipline from Assamite monks in the Maghreb, and control of the body from Japanese Gangrel on Mount Osore.

His reputation is that of an incorruptible enforcer. A hound who answers not to Princes but to a code he wrote himself in blood and fire.

**In short:** Brujah veteran turned enforcer. Former Navy commando, Embraced in 1960 in Haiti. He has served the Masquerade as a hunter of rogue Kindred on three continents. In Seattle he acts as the Sheriff's free agent: judge, executioner, and ghost.

**Concept (sheet):** Veteran. **Affiliation (sheet):** Independent — Camarilla Collaborator. **Sire:** Marcus “Ironjaw” Vance (@ID[PNJ-06]).

### 🩸 Potence 4 — Supernatural Strength

- **Lethal Body** (Potence 1 homebrew; *Lethal Body*): Your blows hit like a jackhammer. Superficial damage converted to lethal.
- **Prowess** (Potence 2; *Prowess*): Lets you perform impossible feats of strength or lift up to a ton. Perfect for breaching, rescuing, or opening armored entrances.
- **Shattering Blow** (Potence 3 homebrew): A single concentrated impact that can bring down walls or incapacitate with one hit. Ideal as a finishing execution.
- **Kinetic Bullet** (Potence ••••, Celerity 2 amalgam – Homebrew):
  - **Cost:** 1 Rouse Check to activate. Lasts one scene. Allows up to Potence (4) shots at no extra cost; more shots require new Rouse Checks (1 per additional shot).
  - **Effect:** David can throw any small/medium object (marble, rock, nut, small stake, etc.) as if it were a heavy pistol shot.
  - **Range:** up to Potence × 10 m (40 m); no penalty within half that distance.
  - **Attack:** Dexterity + Athletics + Potence (or Strength + Athletics, table's choice); the target uses normal defense.
  - **Damage:** as a .45 → base damage 3 + net successes; lethal against mortals/ghouls, and against Kindred without Fortitude.
  - 1/scene you can make an “Execution Shot”: +1 Diff.; with 3+ net successes, a mortal/ghoul is dead or unconscious; a Kindred suffers a heavy penalty (-2) on the body part hit.
  - With stakes / bolts and +2 Diff., with 4+ net successes you can stake or pin the Kindred's chest (Storyteller ruling).
  - It also works for breaking cameras, lights, weapons in mortals' hands, etc., with no visible firearms and no shell casings.

💬 *Note: “Lethal Body + Shattering Blow” is a valid and brutal combination, but Potence 4 is usually used with Prowess or Superhuman Assault. In his case, Shattering Blow is better for a hunter of the Masquerade.*

## Last Bastion of the Masquerade

*Source: doc 17. Last Bastion of the Masquerade (Fortitude ••••). Homebrew – optional amalgam with Presence or Auspex, if you want to fine-tune it further.*

*“I don't go down until the job is done.”*

**Theme / fiction.** When David marks someone as an enemy of the Masquerade, his body and his will refuse to fall. Broken bones, bullets, fire… all of it held together by sheer refusal. It isn't blind Brujah fury: it's the brutal calm of the executioner who won't let himself die until he's finished off the condemned.

### Mechanical Version (V5-Friendly)

**Requirements**

- Fortitude 4
- Humanity 6+ (or at least 1 Conviction tied to “protecting the Masquerade / the balance”)
- Optional as an amalgam: Presence 2 or Auspex 2 (Presence if you want it more “imposing,” Auspex if you see it as an almost mystical focus on his mission).

**Cost**

- 1 Rouse Check on activation.
- Can only be used once per scene, and once per night for its big effect.

**Activation.** You must declare a target a “Veil-breaker”:

- It must be someone who, in David's judgment (and the Storyteller's), is actively endangering the Masquerade:
  - a Kindred using Disciplines in front of mortals,
  - a Sabbat carrying out massacres,
  - a Cainite about to reveal the existence of vampires, etc.
- Quick action: David locks his gaze / pronounces the “sentence” (a whisper, a stony line, whatever you like) and activates the power.

As long as this sentenced target remains present in the scene (or David is pursuing them directly), the power is active.

### Effects

**1. I Won't Fall Yet.** As long as the sentenced target remains in the scene or in direct pursuit:

- If David would be Incapacitated by damage (superficial or aggravated) on a damage roll:
  - Instead of falling, he's left at 1 superficial Health level.
  - He automatically gains the Dazed / Staggered condition (the Storyteller can impose -2 on complex physical actions, no multiple actions, etc.).
  - This effect can only happen once per scene and per night.

In short: 👉 The first time someone “would drop him” while he's executing an enemy of the Masquerade… he doesn't fall. He stays on his feet at 1 Health.

**2. Executioner's Flesh.** As long as the sentenced target remains present:

- David gains +Fortitude (4) dice on tests to:
  - Resist frenzy from damage or provocation by the target.
  - Resist Dominate / Presence / Auspex from the target (or from their direct allies in the scene, at the Storyteller's discretion).
- He also ignores wound penalties (as if Deny Pain were “boosted”) against that specific target.
- Outside that hunt, Deny Pain works normally.

Narrative effect: 👉 While he's hunting a real danger to the Masquerade, he doesn't break, doesn't flee, and doesn't bend mentally at that enemy's hands.

**3. Moral Limit.** Because otherwise it gets out of hand:

- If the Storyteller decides David has activated it against someone who isn't really a threat to the Masquerade, or out of pure personal grudge:
  - The power works the same, but at the end of the night David breaks (see “When He Breaks”): he twisted his Conviction and used “the Masquerade” as an excuse to kill.
  - If a PC saw it, they decide whether to tell him.

This nails it to the concept of an enforcer with a code, not a thug justifying himself.

### Is It Broken or Reasonable?

If you compare it with the official Fortitude 4 powers:

- Draught of Endurance: gives Fortitude and powers to another Kindred for one night.
- Gorgon Scales: gives brutal defenses, but very situational and tied to Resonance.

Last Bastion of the Masquerade:

- It's very strong, but:
  - It only works in scenes where there's a real threat to the Masquerade.
  - The “I don't go down” is once per night, and it leaves you wrecked.
  - It doesn't increase damage or give super attacks; it only keeps David on his feet to finish the job.
  - It has a moral cost if abused.

At the table it's a power that:

- Reinforces his role as “the Black Dog of the Masquerade.”
- Delivers a very cinematic moment: the blow that should put him on the ground… and he gets up at 1 Health with his jaw broken and his gaze fixed.
- Doesn't unbalance things as much as a constant brute-force damage-reduction buff against anything and everyone.

### Simpler / Quicker Version

If you'd rather have something even easier to use, the same concept can be boiled down to:

**Lite Version:**

- **Cost:** 1 Rouse Check.
- **Effect:** Once per night, when you take damage that would leave you Incapacitated while fighting someone who is endangering the Masquerade, you stay at 1 Health instead of falling.
- **Extra:** +2 dice to resist frenzy and mental Disciplines from that enemy until the end of the scene.

**Narrative description of David Smith**

Standing under dim light, David Smith looks like a statue carved from steel and shadow. His face, angular and firm, is that of a man who has seen too many battles and forgets none of them. The square jaw, clenched, seems made for biting rather than talking. His weathered skin has the pallor of someone who hasn't felt the sun in years; a jagged scar runs across his right cheek, from the cheekbone to the edge of his lip, a dry line that never quite finished healing.

His hair, short and dark, shows the first gray at the temples, witnesses to an age when reflexes still outrun thought. His eyes—dark gray, almost black—don't look: they assess, calculate, weigh every movement. There's something unsettling about them, a dull gleam, like that of a hunter who has learned to recognize prey even in total darkness.

He wears a black tactical jacket, heavy, reinforced at the shoulders and chest, with multiple pockets and Velcro closures and no insignia. It's a uniform without a flag, the uniform of someone who serves only his own cause. Every fold seems designed for combat, every buckle cinched with the precision of a soldier who leaves nothing to chance. Beneath it, the silhouette reveals a strong, contained body, muscles tense more from habit than from effort.

The air seems to smell of gunpowder, rust, and dried blood. It's the scent that has followed him for decades, the same one that soaks his clothes and his soul. David Smith needs neither words nor weapons to command respect. His presence alone, still but charged with pent-up tension, makes you think he's one heartbeat away from unleashing a storm.

## Tactical Synergies Between Disciplines and Skills

*Source: doc 20. Storyteller guide for when David fights alongside the coterie. Let him shine, but let a PC land the decisive blow.*

### 1. Celerity (Quick Reflexes, Blink, Lightning Movement)

- **Best use:** pursuit, ambushes, evasion, and surprise attacks.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Athletics + Celerity:** moves from cover to cover before enemies react.
  - **Brawl or Melee + Blurred Rush:** closes the distance and strikes in the same action, before the opponent finishes reacting.
  - **Stealth + Celerity:** moves through shadows or closes distance without being seen.
- **How he uses it:** *Blink* on the turn before attacking and *Blurred Rush* when striking: an appear-and-attack sequence that's nearly impossible to stop.

:::recuadro Editor's Note
Doc 20 used Lightning Strike (*Lightning Strike*), which was removed from the sheet (see Disciplines). Where the doc combined it with Lightning Movement, Blink and Blurred Rush now go instead.
:::

### 2. Potence (Lethal Body, Shattering Blow)

- **Best use:** melee combat, physical intimidation, breaking structures or equipment.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Brawl + Lethal Body:** lethal damage even unarmed.
  - **Melee + Shattering Blow:** multiplies the weapon's damage; breaks bones or armor effortlessly.
  - **Intimidation + Potence:** backs up a threat with supernatural strength (breaking a table or a door as a gesture).
- **Tactical combination:** *Lethal Body* and *Shattering Blow* together against tough enemies, then *Fortitude* (Deny Pain) to weather the counterattacks.

### 3. Fortitude (Toughness, Deny Pain)

- **Best use:** resisting physical, fire, and lethal damage, and staying on his feet when others would fall.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Stamina + Fortitude:** each level adds an automatic success to his soak or endurance rolls.
  - **Prolonged combat + Deny Pain:** keeps fighting with severe penalties.
  - **Prowess + Fortitude:** keeps control even in the middle of frenzy.
- **Narrative use:** David can take bullets, keep advancing, grab the enemy by the throat, and deliver a precise blow with Potence.

### 4. Presence (Instinctive Authority, Majesty of War)

- **Best use:** intimidating, subduing, or controlling the social environment in seconds.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Intimidation + Instinctive Authority:** subdues enemies or ghouls without raising his voice.
  - **Persuasion + Majesty of War (Kindle Emotions):** imposes his will in social scenes or interrogations.
  - **Intimidating stares + Presence:** forces a “halt” before the fight.
- **Key combination:** before combat, *Instinctive Authority* to intimidate or scatter weak enemies. If it fails, he switches to Celerity or Potence without losing rhythm.

### 5. Auspex (Heightened Senses, Premonition)

- **Best use:** tracking, detecting traps, anticipating attacks.
- **Synergy:**
  - **Awareness + Heightened Senses:** detects snipers, heartbeats, or slight movements.
  - **Tailing + Premonition:** anticipates ambushes or the target's movements.
  - **Investigation + Premonition:** tracks Masquerade violators or prepares a night hunt.
- **Strategy:** *Premonition* before the hunt or a confrontation. If he gets a vision, he tells the coterie in two words: it's a clue, not a solution.

### 💀 Standout Tactical Combos for David Smith

| Situation | Disciplines + Skills | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent ambush** | *Celerity + Stealth + Auspex (Premonition)* | Gets ahead of the victim, predicts their movement, and attacks before they react. |
| **Close combat (1v1)** | *Potence + Fortitude + Brawl (Tactical combat)* | More damage, ignores wounds, and lands devastating blows. |
| **Surprise attack from above** | *Celerity (Lightning Movement) + Athletics (Jumping)* | Drops from a rooftop and strikes before he's seen. |
| **Hunting a Masquerade violator** | *Auspex (Premonition) + Stealth + Presence (Instinctive Authority)* | Follows the trail, locates them, and neutralizes them without open combat. |
| **Against multiple enemies** | *Fortitude (Flesh of Marble) + Celerity (Quick Reflexes) + Potence (Shattering Blow)* | Takes minimal damage, acts before them, and takes them down one by one. |
| **Intimidating a whole room** | *Presence (Majesty of War) + Intimidation + Iron Will* | Silences everyone and commands respect or terror even among older vampires. |

### 🧠 How He Fights Alongside the Coterie

1. **Before the confrontation:** *Auspex*. He scans the surroundings and says where the exits are.
2. **Start of the conflict:** *Presence (Instinctive Authority)* to cut off the social flow or disorient the enemy.
3. **Physical combat:** *Celerity + Potence + Fortitude* in tandem. He takes the toughest enemy or covers the most exposed PC.
4. **Finish:** when the opponent falls or surrenders, he stops before frenzy. If a PC keeps hitting, he holds them back.
5. **After:** *Auspex* or *Premonition* to see whether the Masquerade held.
