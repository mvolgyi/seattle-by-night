# August Krell · The Other Son

*“He taught you to block the blow. He taught me to take it.”*

**Clan:** Brujah · **Sect:** none · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** antagonist; the false Vance

## Mortal Days: The Legionnaire of the Docks

August Krell was born in Hamburg in 1944, between bombing raids, and grew up in the port of Marseille, where his mother took him running from everything. At seventeen he joined the Foreign Legion under a false name and reached Algeria for the last year of the war. He came back with a medal he never wore and an odd calm around violence: he didn't enjoy hurting people, but it didn't cost him anything either. Afterward he worked as a longshoreman, as muscle for smugglers, and as whatever the docks needed.

## Kindred Nights: The Half-Taught Lesson

In 1972, Marcus Vance (@ID[PNJ-06]) watched him fight three men armed with iron bars in a dockside warehouse and win. He Embraced him that same night. Krell is **Vance's childe**, like David. Nobody in Seattle knows it, not even David.

Vance trained him for four years with the same doctrine, the same discipline, and the cold water. But in 1976 he decided Krell wasn't fit: the Beast asked his permission too often, and he granted it too gladly. He taught him the inverted guard, the one given to those who don't finish (@ID[DOC-04]), and left without saying goodbye.

Krell spent fifty years as a mercenary. He worked for Archons, for the Sabbat, for whoever paid. He killed a lot, and well. He never stopped asking himself what he'd done wrong. In early 2026, Mr. Pell, Halloway's archivist, found him in Lisbon: Vance's journals mention “the German” three times. Halloway offered him Vance's coat, which came in the same auction lot, a domain in Seattle, and above all the journal folios where Vance writes about him. Krell accepted before he finished the sentence.

## Plans and Schemes

**Clean up the copies.** He destroys the Kindred who buy or transport copies of the File, using Controlled Fury so it looks like Vance's work (@ID[A1-E02], @ID[A2-E02]). Jun Takeda is his next target (Night 4).

**Make his brother see him.** He doesn't want to beat David; he wants David to acknowledge him. In @ID[A3-E01] he goes after one of the coterie's Touchstones to force David to look him in the eye. In @ID[A3-E03] he offers to talk.

**The folios.** Halloway promised him the journal pages that talk about him and still hasn't handed them over. Krell is starting to suspect they don't exist, or that they say something Halloway doesn't want him to read. If the coterie shows him those folios, he switches sides (@ID[P-12]).

**The promised domain.** Halloway promised him Harbor Island, David's domain. Krell hasn't yet asked himself how Halloway plans to keep that promise.

## Domain and Haven

A rusted barge in dry dock in Georgetown, south of the port, which Halloway rents for him through a shell company (Haven 1). He sleeps in the hold, in a toolbox as big as a coffin. He feeds on night-shift longshoremen and truckers. He finds them alone, hits them once, hard, and drinks fast. He doesn't kill anyone unless he has to. He considers it unprofessional.

## Servants and Tools

**Vance's coat**: a long gray wool coat, tailored for a man broader in the shoulders. It's too big on him. It's “the coat that isn't his” from @ID[DOC-06] and @ID[DOC-10].

**Copy 2 of 3** of Vance's journals (@ID[DOC-04]), with his pencil notes.

**Halloway's support**: an electric car with no registered plates, and two security ghouls (@ID[MEN-04]) if he asks for them.

**Resources 1**: what Halloway pays him.

## Kindred Relationships

**Marcus Vance** (wounded love). He hates him and needs him. He believes he's dead and isn't sure that changes anything.

**David Smith** (envy). The one who finished. The one who got the other guard. The good son.

**Charles Halloway** (distrust). He knows Halloway is using him. He doesn't care, as long as he gets the folios.

## Mask and Appearance

He has no Mask (Mask 0); he uses a forged Portuguese passport. He looks about twenty-eight, but he moves like someone older. His head is shaved, and he has an old knife scar running through his left eyebrow, a broken nose that healed badly, and hands full of broken knuckles. He wears dark work clothes under Vance's coat. He's left-handed. He speaks little, with a French accent, and when he's serious he drops his voice so low you have to lean in. He smells of wet wool.
