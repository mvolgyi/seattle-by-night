# Danny Ruiz · The One Still Down There

**Reference** (deceased mortal, no actor) · @ID[PNJ-04|Helen Ward]'s college friend · one of the seven “unaccounted for” tenants of the @ID[LUG-11|Larchmont] in 2014.

*This page collects what docs 00–16 say about Danny. The source doc for each fact is in parentheses.*

## What Helen Remembers

- In college, Danny lent Helen his Media Law notes and laughed at her because she highlighted in three colors (doc 03, Opening Fiction @ID[CAP-00]).
- He lived on the fourth floor of the Larchmont Building, in Tacoma, until the night in 2014 when the building burned and seven tenants were “unaccounted for.” There were no bodies. There was an article, Helen's, that the paper paid her not to publish. And there was a photo of a man with his back to the fire, looking at the camera (doc 03).
- In the list Helen writes from memory (@ID[AYU-02]): “2014 · LARCHMONT. Seven ‘unaccounted for.’ Danny. Forty-one photos of the fire, the fire department report that doesn't add up, the doorman's statement he later retracted. THE PHOTO.” (doc 15).

## What Really Happened

- The Larchmont burned in the small hours of a March night in 2014. The official version blamed a short circuit (doc 05, history; locked in: “Larchmont fire in March 2014”).
- A pack of three Sabbat Kindred who didn't leave for Gehenna was using the basement as a farm: they kept seven tenants locked in the old cistern and fed on them for weeks. David arrived after the pack had already set the building on fire; the seven tenants were already dead (doc 05, LUG-11).
- @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] ordered the cistern sealed without removing the bodies: seven bloodless corpses were a Masquerade breach. “Danny Ruiz (REF-02) is still down there” (doc 05, LUG-11; doc 11, @ID[A2-E05]).
- ID registry: “REF-02 · Danny Ruiz, in the Larchmont cistern” (doc 05).
- Helen still doesn't know; David didn't get there in time (doc 03, CAP-00's Storyteller Notes).
