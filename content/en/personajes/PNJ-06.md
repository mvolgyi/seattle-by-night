## History and Motivation

Marcus Vance was a World War II veteran, an **artillery commander and military strategist** who, after his Embrace in the '40s by an exiled European Brujah, became a figure of order and discipline within the clan—a rarity among his kind.

Unlike chaotic or idealistic Brujah, Marcus believed that **revolution needed structure, and violence, purpose**. In his view, the Masquerade was the wall that held the balance between Cainite anarchy and total extinction.

During the Cold War, he operated as a roving enforcer for the Camarilla, hunting Kindred who threatened political stability. His nickname, *Ironjaw*, came as much from his iron jaw as from his unbreakable resolve: he never backed down, not from humans and not from Elders.

**Nicknames:** *Ironjaw*, *The Red Commander*, *The Hammer of Order* · **Years active:** 1940–1985 · **Place of origin:** New York, USA · **Last known location:** Port-au-Prince, Haiti—1960 · **Fate:** Presumed destroyed by the Sabbat in the '80s, though unconfirmed. · **Sect:** Camarilla (missing, possibly hunted by the Inquisition).

## The Embrace of David Smith

**Date:** July 8, 1960 · **Place:** Haiti, during a covert SEAL mission.

David, then an **American commando**, was leading an infiltration operation on the Haitian coast, investigating a string of disappearances tied to blood cults. His squad was ambushed—not by rebels, but by a Sabbat pack that had turned the place into an experimentation camp.

Marcus Vance was in the area, hunting the same group on Camarilla orders. He arrived too late to save Smith's platoon… but in time to **watch the young soldier, gravely wounded, refuse to die**, dragging himself forward with a knife in hand to take out the last enemy even as he bled out.

> “I saw something in him I hadn't seen in decades—not fear, not fury. I saw *will*. And I thought: men like that must not die. They must be used.”
> — *Excerpt from the memoirs of Marcus Vance, recovered from his haven in Marseille.*

Marcus Embraced him not out of compassion but out of **strategic principle**: David was the ideal soldier, a being with discipline, conscience, and an unbreakable moral code. He shaped him for a decade, teaching him the balance between the violence of the Beast and the structure of duty. Thus was born **“the Hound of the Masquerade,”** an enforcer deadlier and more precise than any Archon.

## The Sire's Fate

In 1985, Marcus Vance disappeared in Eastern Europe while investigating a Sabbat outbreak in Romania. Rumor among the Kindred says he was destroyed in direct combat with a **Tzimisce Elder**, though some reports link him to the Black Hand.

David never confirmed his death. But he still keeps the **military knife he carried on the day of his Embrace**, a relic of the moment he died as a human and was born as a hunter.

## Relationship with David Smith

- **Emotional bond:** absolute respect, without sentimentality.
- **Blood Bond:** dissolved decades ago, but the philosophical loyalty persists.
- **Influence:** Marcus's voice still echoes in David's mind when he faces hard moral decisions.
- **Legacy:** “Violence is not chaos. It is judgment.”

### 💀 Career and Historical Significance

Marcus “Ironjaw” Vance stood out among the Brujah for his unusual loyalty to the **Camarilla** and his devotion to the **Masquerade** as a principle of survival. While others of his clan championed revolution, Marcus championed **order**. He was an old-school soldier: he believed in hierarchy, discipline, and force as the language of justice.

> “Where others see rebellion, I see indiscipline. The Beast does not need to be free. It needs to be tamed.”
> — *Marcus Vance, Marseille, 1973.*

During the Cold War, he acted as a roving agent of the Camarilla, eliminating Sabbat cells, Anarch heresies, and uncontrolled vampires in conflict zones. His modus operandi was direct and surgical: **“Locate. Assess. Execute.”**

Vance was a mentor and, to some, an executioner. His reputation was that of an incorruptible enforcer, feared even by the Ventrue he served.

### 🩸 The Embrace of David Smith

In 1960, during a joint operation with agents of the Sheriff of New Orleans, Vance intercepted a Sabbat pack in Haiti. There he found a dying young commando: **David Smith**, sole survivor of a platoon devoured by the enemy Cainites.

According to reports recovered from his hermetic journal (Marseille, 1963):

> “Not all men deserve to die. Some only need a different war. Smith did not ask to be saved. But he did not surrender either. That is enough.”

He Embraced Smith to forge a **living weapon against vampiric indiscipline**. For nearly a decade he trained him under a martial code: obedience, precision, and restraint of the Beast. The Archons of Paris describe their relationship as “father and son in death.”

### 🕯️ Fate and Legacy

Vance's last recorded communication dates to 1985, an encrypted message addressed to Archon Duval (@ID[PNJ-07]):

> “If I do not return, burn my havens. Whatever is left of me will no longer serve order.”

No physical remains were found. He is presumed destroyed in a confrontation with a **Tzimisce Elder** near Cluj-Napoca, Romania. Black Hand files confirmed activity in the area 48 hours later.

## Differences from Docs 00–16

- **Fate.** The header of doc 23 says “presumed destroyed by the Sabbat in the '80s”; the dossier and docs 00–16 set it in 1985, near Cluj-Napoca. They agree on the essentials.
- **Journals.** Doc 23 cites “memoirs recovered from his haven in Marseille” and a “hermetic journal (Marseille, 1963)” as if they were in Camarilla archives. In docs 00–16 the journals weren't burned; they were sold in 1986 and Halloway has them; the Camarilla has had only copy 3, since 2025 (@ID[GAN-07]). The doc 23 quotes are not folio 14 of @ID[DOC-04].
- **David's training.** Doc 23 says “for a decade” (nearly a decade in the dossier); docs 00–16 give no duration. Vance trained Krell in Marseille from 1972 to 1976 (@ID[AYU-07]), which doc 23 doesn't mention.
- **Controlled Fury.** Doc 23 names it as Vance's doctrine, as does @ID[LOR-01] (which places it in the 1950s).
