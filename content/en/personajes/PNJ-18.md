# Special Agent Dana Okafor · She Who Gives Nothing Away

*“Every source wants something. Until you know what it wants, it's bait.”*

**Mortal** · FIRSTLIGHT, head of the Puget Cell · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** latent antagonist

## Before: The Woman Who Counted Bodies

Dana Okafor was born in 1981 in Houston, the daughter of a Nigerian engineer and a schoolteacher from Louisiana. She joined the FBI at twenty-six and spent eleven years in the Chicago violent crimes unit, where she made her name closing cases others had written off. In 2017 she investigated a string of disappearances on the South Side that fit no known pattern. She found the pattern. She also found the basement where it ended. Inside was something that tore two fingers off her left hand before she emptied her magazine into its head and it kept moving.

## Now: The Puget Cell

Six months later, a man from the Department of Homeland Security visited her in the hospital and told her she wasn't crazy. In 2018 she joined FIRSTLIGHT. In 2024 they sent her to Seattle to open a small cell, with a tight budget and a clear order: confirm whether the city had “an organized structure” and, if it did, leave it alone until it could be torn out by the roots.

Okafor doesn't hate the Kindred: she fears them methodically, the way you fear a fire. She's patient, meticulous, and distrustful, above all of anything that comes to her easily. ARCHER came too easily. She uses ARCHER anyway, because it's all she has, but she doesn't buy it.

## Plans and Machinations

**The full index.** ARCHER promised her the entire File. If she gets it, she requests reinforcements from national headquarters.

**Helen Ward.** At Thermometer 5 she contacts her. She offers her protection and a salary. She genuinely believes Helen is in danger, and she's right.

**The “R. A.” anomaly.** She's spent months preparing a visit to the medical examiner behind the fifteen impossible reports. If the Thermometer reaches 7, she makes it.

**Who is ARCHER?** She suspects it's an insider who wants to use FIRSTLIGHT to settle a score. If someone confirms it for her with evidence, she stops trusting the deliveries and starts investigating the source.

## Appearance

Forty-five years old. Tall, close-cropped graying hair, unbranded office clothes: gray blazer, slacks, flat shoes. Her left hand is missing the ring finger and the pinky, and she keeps it in her pocket most of the time. She speaks quietly, unhurried, and has a habit of repeating the other person's last sentence before answering. She drinks black coffee all day. She never sits with her back to a door.
