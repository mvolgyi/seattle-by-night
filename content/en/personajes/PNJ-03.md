# Charles Halloway · The Uncrowned King

*“This city doesn't need a Prince who watches. It needs one who sees.”*

**Clan:** Ventrue · **Position:** Ventrue Primogen · **Sect:** Camarilla · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** main antagonist

## Mortal Days: The Newspaper Heir

Charles Halloway was born in 1921 into a Seattle family that had made its fortune in timber and multiplied it in the press. By thirty he was running three newspapers and a radio station. He was cold, methodical, and very patient. His father had taught him that a story doesn't run when it happens, but when it's convenient. He also learned that a story that never runs can be worth even more.

## Kindred Nights: The One Who Learned by Watching

Prince Adelaide Grange Embraced him in 1958 for his press contacts. Four years later, Whitmore drove her out of the city by blackmailing her with photographs and documents, and Halloway understood two things. First, that his sire had been defeated without a drop of blood spilled. Second, that the method worked.

In 1978 he used it against O'Connor. In 1986 he bought Vance's journals, thinking they might someday serve him against the Sheriff's Hound. He has been waiting ever since. He has accumulated money (Halloway Meridian Capital), information (Thornquist), and patience. A year ago he began writing to Justicar Panhard. In 2026, with the city full of cameras and the Second Inquisition at the door, he decided he had waited long enough.

## Plans and Schemes

**The File.** His ghoul Preston Gale found Helen's archive using data from his company's doorbell cameras. The @ID[MEN-01] crew stole it on Night 1. Halloway keeps it in his vault (@ID[LUG-06]) from Night 6 on.

**The ghost.** He hired August Krell (@ID[PNJ-12]) with the promise to “give him what Vance denied him”: a name, a place, recognition. Krell destroys the buyers of copies by imitating Controlled Fury.

**ARCHER.** Through Gale, he feeds FIRSTLIGHT fragments of the File that point only to the Prince's and the Sheriff's assets (@ID[DOC-07]). When the raid comes, he'll ask for a regency “to protect the Masquerade.”

**The expendable childe.** If things go wrong, the accounts behind the leak lead to Celeste Arden (@ID[PJ-02]) or to her NPC substitute (@ID[PNJ-10]). He'll offer her up as a traitor at the Council (@ID[A4-E01]) without blinking.

## Domain and Haven

Halloway Tower downtown, with a penthouse and a vault (@ID[LUG-06]; Haven 4). He also controls the Eastside. He feeds only on mortals who have signed a contract with him (Ventrue feeding restriction): employees, partners, debtors. He believes a signature is a form of consent and has never wondered whether people knew what they were signing.

## Mask and Demeanor

To the mortal world he is Charles Halloway Jr., grandson of the founder and reclusive chairman of an investment fund, who never appears in public due to a chronic illness and takes meetings only by video call, with his camera off (Mask 3). He looks about forty. He is slim and of medium height, with black hair parted on the side and brown eyes that seem tired. He wears dark, classically cut suits, a white shirt, and a narrow tie. His hands don't move when he talks. He never interrupts. He smiles when he loses.
