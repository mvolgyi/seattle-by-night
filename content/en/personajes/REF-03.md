# Ray Dorsey · The Anarch Who Killed Emily

**Reference** (Kindred destroyed in 1996, no actor) · Seattle Anarch · killed @ID[REF-01|Emily Smith] and was destroyed by @ID[PJ-01|David].

*This page gathers what docs 00–16 say about Dorsey. The source doc for each fact is in parentheses. The docs give him no clan, sire, or stats.*

## What Is Known

- ID registry: “REF-03 · Ray Dorsey, the Anarch who killed Emily in 1996 (destroyed by David)” (doc 07).
- He used teenagers from Rainier Valley as his herd (doc 05, story: “Emily's Night (1996)”).
- In the fall of 1996 he asked @ID[PNJ-11|Rosa Iturbe] in Everett to put him up for a week: “he said the Sheriff was after him” (doc 07, Rosa's profile). Rosa gave him a bed at the shipyard (doc 11, @ID[A2-E03]).
- A week later he killed Emily Smith, a social worker, in a half-abandoned apartment in Rainier Valley (doc 05; doc 07; doc 11). The obituary dates the death to October 21, 1996 (doc 15, @ID[AYU-04]; a provisional detail according to doc 16).
- David arrived minutes too late and destroyed him with a single shot (doc 05; doc 07: “the Hound destroyed him with a single shot”).

## Emily's Night

- The backup who arrived afterward and cleaned up the scene while David was still on the ground was @ID[PJ-05|Hal Brekke], a Gangrel two years into his unlife (doc 05; doc 02: “he was the one who cleaned up the scene while David was still on his knees”).
- To the city, it was a score being settled between gangs. To Montgomery, it was the reason he started keeping an eye on his Hound (doc 05). The obituary says the police are investigating it “as a gang-related confrontation” and that “one of the juveniles was unharmed” (doc 15, AYU-04).
- That juvenile was @ID[MEN-10|Marcos Delgado], fifteen years old, the only kid who survived. Before he passed out, he saw a man with a scar on his face shoot Dorsey and kneel beside the social worker (doc 09, @ID[GAN-01]).

## What He Left Behind

- **Rosa.** She didn't know what Dorsey was going to do. She never forgave herself. Since then she has one rule in Everett: nobody who uses minors as a herd (doc 07). Her agenda in Salt's is “to settle the debt of 1996” (doc 08, @ID[COT-03]).
- **Rumor 11:** “Rosa Iturbe sheltered the Anarch who killed the Hound's daughter.” *(True. It was in 1996, a week before Emily's death. Rosa didn't know what he was going to do. She never forgave herself)* (doc 05).
- **The confession** (@ID[A2-E03], doc 11): “There's something I have to tell you. Thirty years ago. A guy named Ray Dorsey asked me to put him up for a week. Said the Sheriff was after him. I gave him a bed at the shipyard. A week later he killed a social worker in Rainier Valley. You know which one.” Doc 01 summed it up like this: “Rosa knew the Anarch who killed Emily.”
