# Jun Takeda · She Who Doesn't Know What She Carries

*“I just deliver. I don't open the packages. That's rule number one.”*

**Clan:** thin-blood · **Sect:** none (Thornquist's protégée) · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** courier, possible victim, and clue (@ID[P-05])

## Mortal Days: The Beacon Hill Courier

Jun Takeda is 22. She grew up on Beacon Hill, between her grandmother's flower shop in the International District and the hills she biked up to get to class. She dropped out of college to pay her mother's rent and started delivering for several apps at once: food by day, packages by night. She knew the city better than any map. In the spring of 2025, at four in the morning, she dropped off an order at a building in Georgetown, and the customer opened the door with a stained mouth.

## Kindred Nights: The Courier from Below

She doesn't know who Embraced her. She woke up in the doorway the next night, hungry and without a pulse, though sometimes, when she eats something, a little of it comes back. She drifted for two weeks until a thin-blood from the docks brought her down to the Underground. Thornquist offered her what she does best: delivering. Ever since, she has carried packages between Kindred without asking what's inside.

On Night 1, Thornquist had her take two USB drives to two different buyers. One was for the Nosferatu destroyed at Pier 46. The other was for a buyer who never showed up to the meet. Jun still has that second drive in her jacket pocket. Krell knows it.

## Plans and Schemes

**Survive.** Since she heard about Pier 46, she sleeps somewhere different every day. She hasn't gone back to the Underground.

**Hand it over or throw it away.** She doesn't know what to do with the USB drive. If she gives it to Thornquist, she figures he'll sell it anyway. If she throws it away, she'll have nothing to bargain with. If the coterie finds her before Krell does, she may give it to them in exchange for protection.

**Learn.** If she survives, she wants to learn to hunt for real (@ID[GAN-11]).

## Domain and Haven

Nothing fixed. She sleeps in rented storage units, in the back room of her grandmother's flower shop, or at a friend's bike shop (Haven 0). As a thin-blood, she can eat some food and stand sunlight for a while, but she needs blood. She buys expired bags from a hospital orderly and, when she's out of money, carefully bites other couriers asleep at the bus stop.

## Servants and Tools

**The bike**: electric, modified, the fastest in the Underground.

**Contacts 2**: couriers across half the city.

To mortals she is Jun Takeda, delivery courier (Mask 3: she has a legal life, accounts, and family). She looks her age. She is small and wiry, with hair dyed a faded blue, ear piercings, a yellow waterproof delivery jacket, an insulated backpack, and a helmet covered in stickers. She talks fast, cracks jokes when she's scared, and never stays still.
