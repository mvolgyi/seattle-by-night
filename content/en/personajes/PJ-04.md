# PJ-04 · Nadia Ferro · Toreador · “The Voice Behind the Mic”

**Concept:** sound producer for Helen's podcast and childe of Prince Whitmore.

## Mortal Days

Nadia played bass in Capitol Hill bands for fifteen years. She was never famous, but she was the one who made everyone else sound good. In 2015, an infection damaged her left ear and took her off the stage. She moved to the mixing board, where she found what truly obsessed her: the human voice at three in the morning, when people tell the truth.

## Kindred Nights

Prince Marcus Whitmore (@ID[PNJ-02]) heard her mixing live at a club and Embraced her in 2016. To him she was one more piece in his collection. She lives at court, but she doesn't belong to him. In 2021 she started producing “What the City Forgets,” Helen's podcast. Helen's voice hooked her. Ever since, she has been passing Helen anonymous tips to make the episodes better. She feeds at late-night shows, on audiences lost in a trance.

## Connection to the Coterie

The File has a note from Helen about her own producer: “N. never shows up in group photos. Coincidence?”

## Ambition, Desire, and Touchstone

**Ambition:** record something that outlives the Masquerade. **Desire:** for Helen to finish her big story. **Suggested Conviction:** “Never silence someone who tells the truth.” **Touchstone:** Theo, her old bandmate, who still plays Thursdays at a dive bar on Capitol Hill.
