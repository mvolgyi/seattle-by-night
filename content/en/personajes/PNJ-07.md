# Archon Duval · The Justicar's Envoy

**Background figure** (no actor sheet) · Camarilla Archon in the service of Justicar @ID[PNJ-08|Helena Panhard] · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** waits on Bainbridge for the briefcase holding the File if @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] wins the Council (@ID[A4-E03]); the lead of @ID[GAN-07].

*This page gathers what docs 00–16 say about Duval. The source doc for each fact is in parentheses. The docs give him no first name, clan, or stats.*

## Who He Is

- He appears as a background figure, like Justicar Panhard and @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] (doc 01, NPC index; ID registry v2: “PNJ-07 · Archon Duval (background)”).
- He knew Vance (doc 09, @ID[GAN-07]).
- He serves as Justicar Panhard's envoy (doc 13, @ID[A4-E03] and the ID registry: “Archon Duval appears on Bainbridge as the Justicar's envoy”).

## In the Adventure

- **Night 8.** If Halloway wins the Council (@ID[A4-E01]), at 12:30 a.m. Mr. Pell (@ID[MEN-09]) leaves Halloway Tower with the briefcase to deliver the original File “to an envoy of the Justicar on Bainbridge”: it's the proof Panhard asked for before she'll recognize the regency (doc 13, A4-E01's Storyteller Notes). Pell travels with Dawson and two ghouls (@ID[MEN-04]) and means to “deliver it to Archon Duval, who waits on Bainbridge as the Justicar's envoy, as proof of the ‘crisis’ that justifies the regency” (doc 13, A4-E03).
- **The endings.** If the File reaches Duval and Halloway lost or was exposed: @ID[FIN-A] (“Duval gives it back”). If Halloway won: @ID[FIN-B]. If Halloway won but the coterie kept the File: a partial FIN-B, because Halloway has no proof to show the Justicar (doc 13, endings table). Losing the briefcase “into Duval's hands” is one of the four possible endings (doc 13, A4-E03's Notes).

David earns an Archon's favor (temporary Mawla 2), but he's gone from Seattle for one story. If he goes alone, the coterie plays it without him and without his +1 die. *Suggested: the PC who went with him to Marseille shares Duval's favor (temporary Mawla 2).*
