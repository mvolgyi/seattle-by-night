**Background figure** (no actor sheet) · Camarilla Archon in the service of Justicar @ID[PNJ-08|Helena Panhard] · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** waits on Bainbridge for the briefcase with the File if @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] wins the Council (@ID[A4-E03]); protagonist of @ID[GAN-07].

*This page collects what docs 00–16 say about Duval. The source doc for each detail is in parentheses. The docs give him no first name, clan, or stats.*

## Who He Is

- He appears as a background figure, like Justicar Panhard and @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] (doc 01, NPC index; ID registry v2: “PNJ-07 · Archon Duval (background)”).
- He knew Vance (doc 09, @ID[GAN-07]).
- He serves as Justicar Panhard's envoy (doc 13, @ID[A4-E03] and the ID registry: “Archon Duval appears on Bainbridge as the Justicar's envoy”).

## In the Adventure

- **Night 8.** If Halloway wins the Council (@ID[A4-E01]), at 12:30 a.m. Mr. Pell (@ID[MEN-09]) leaves Halloway Tower with the briefcase to hand over the original File “to an envoy of the Justicar on Bainbridge”: it's the proof Panhard asked of him before she'll recognize the regency (doc 13, Storyteller Notes for A4-E01). Pell travels with Dawson and two ghouls (@ID[MEN-04]) and means “to deliver it to Archon Duval, who waits on Bainbridge as the Justicar's envoy, as proof of the ‘crisis’ that justifies the regency” (doc 13, A4-E03).
- **The Endings.** If the File reaches Duval and Halloway lost or was exposed: @ID[FIN-A] (“Duval sends it back”). If Halloway won: @ID[FIN-B]. If Halloway won but the coterie kept the File: a partial FIN-B, because Halloway has no proof to put before the Justicar (doc 13, endings table). Losing the briefcase “into Duval's hands” is one of the four possible endings (doc 13, Notes for A4-E03).
