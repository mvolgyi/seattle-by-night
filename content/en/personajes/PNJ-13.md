# Abel Thornquist · The Owner Below

*“Everything's for sale down below. The only thing that changes is who pays.”*

**Clan:** Nosferatu · **Position:** Primogen · **Sect:** Camarilla

## Mortal Days: The Carpenter of the New City

Abel Thornquist arrived from Sweden in 1887 with a toolbox and nothing else. Two years later the city burned, and the rebuilding meant work for everyone. He built the timber structures that held the new streets up over the old ones. He was among the first to go down into the basements left underneath. In the winter of 1890 a section collapsed in Pioneer Square, and he was trapped for three days in the dark, with a broken leg and the water rising.

## Kindred Nights: The Broker

He was found by a Nosferatu who had lived down there since before the fire, an old woman the Underground still calls “the Mother.” She gave him a choice: drown or stay. He chose to stay, and he stayed for more than a century. When the Mother fell into torpor in the '40s, Thornquist inherited the Sealed Underground and everything that moved through it: secrets, favors, contraband, people.

He isn't cruel, but he isn't kind either. He's a merchant. He has sold information to every Prince the city has had and to their enemies. He protects the thin-bloods on the docks because they bring him news from the surface, and he charges for that protection in favors. He Embraced Ezra Lund (@ID[PJ-03]) in 2019 because he understood before anyone else that the underground of the twenty-first century is made of cables, not stone.

## Plans and Schemes

**The auction of the File.** Thornquist already has two partial copies of the File, bought from Jun Takeda without her knowing what she was carrying. He is quietly organizing an auction in the Undermarket. He doesn't know that Krell is destroying the buyers.

**The most expensive vote.** He knows Halloway is going to bring a motion against the Prince. He's waiting for offers from both sides.

**The firmware.** He suspects Ezra wrote the Gale Sentinel code. He hasn't told him. He's waiting until it's worth more.

## Domain and Haven

The Sealed Underground, beneath Pioneer Square (@ID[LUG-04]; Haven 4). He feeds on homeless people who sleep in the lowest entrances to the Underground. In exchange he offers them a dry place, blankets, and the assurance that nobody else will bother them. Most of them don't remember the feeding part. The ones who do keep coming back.

**The thin-bloods** (Allies 2): a dozen young people who gather information on the surface.

**Contacts 4**: cops, doormen, night janitorial staff, and maintenance techs all over the city.

**Ezra Lund**: his childe and his best investment.

## Mask and Appearance

He has no mortal Mask (Mask 0). He's huge and stooped. His skin looks like wet wood, gray and cracked; his jaw is twisted from the 1890 fall, and his fingers are long as roots. He wears a three-piece suit from the '20s, patched a thousand times, and a pocket watch that doesn't work. He speaks with a Swedish accent, quietly, as if every word cost money. He always carries a carbide lamp, though he doesn't need one.
