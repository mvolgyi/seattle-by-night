# Rosa “Salt” Iturbe · She Who Doesn't Kneel

*“Nobody kisses rings here. Here, we weld.”*

**Clan:** Brujah · **Sect:** Anarchs · **Position:** baroness of Everett

## Mortal Days: The Shipyard Welder

Rosa Iturbe was born in 1961 in Sestao, beside the shipyards of the Bilbao estuary, and learned to weld from her father at fifteen. When the estuary's shipyards closed in the '80s, she emigrated to the United States with a letter of recommendation from a union and ended up in the Everett shipyards, welding hulls for Navy ships. She was the shipyard's first woman union steward. They called her “Salt” because she said her blood was salty.

## Kindred Nights: The Debt of 1996

In 1991, an Anarch who was hiding in the shipyard Embraced her after a night on strike. Rosa learned fast and organized the Kindred of the north the way she had organized the welders: assemblies, dues, guard shifts. In the fall of 1996, she sheltered a Seattle Anarch for a week who claimed the Sheriff was after him. His name was Ray Dorsey. A week later, Dorsey killed a social worker in Rainier Valley and the Hound destroyed him with a single shot. The social worker was Emily Smith.

Rosa didn't know what Dorsey was going to do. She never forgave herself. Since then she has had one rule in Everett: nobody uses minors as a herd. Since 2023, when the Brujah who left the Camarilla came north, Rosa has been the de facto baroness. Halloway is using her as a scapegoat and she knows it, though she doesn't know it's him.

## Plans and Machinations

**Clear Everett's name.** Rumor says the dockside killer is one of hers. If the Sheriff decides to believe it, there will be a Blood Hunt (@ID[GAN-06]). Rosa is looking for the real killer on her own.

**The debt to the Hound.** She has never told David about 1996. She doesn't know whether telling him would be courage or selfishness. If the coterie makes it to Salt's (@ID[A2-E03]), she confesses it to a PC. That PC decides whether to tell David.

**The thin-bloods.** She offers them refuge in Everett in exchange for work, without asking them for favors the way Thornquist does. If Jun survives, Rosa can take her in.

## Domain and Haven

Salt's Bar and the old shipyard in Everett (@ID[LUG-10]; Haven 2 in a ship's hull in dry dock). She feeds on the night-shift welders and longshoremen, with their consent: some know what she is and let her drink in exchange for protection and a union that actually works.

## Mask and Mien

To mortals she is Rosa Iturbe, retired welder and owner of Salt's Bar (Mask 2). She looks about thirty. She is short and compact, with short black hair, small welding burns on her forearms, and hands that are always stained with grease. She wears a blue work coverall with her name embroidered on it and steel-toed boots. She speaks with a Biscayan accent, fast and blunt, and swears in Basque when she gets angry.
