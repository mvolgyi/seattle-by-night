# Adelaide Grange · The Prince Who Left

**Background figure** (no actor sheet) · Boston Ventrue · Prince of Seattle from 1897 to 1962 · sire of @ID[PNJ-03|Charles Halloway].

*This page gathers what docs 00–16 say about Grange. The source doc for each fact is in parentheses.*

## What Is Known

- “Prince Adelaide Grange, a Boston Ventrue, ruled from 1897 with an accountant's hand” over the city's first Camarilla, small and harbor-bound (doc 05, history; timeline: “1897 · Adelaide Grange (Ventrue), Prince”).
- In 1931 she named @ID[PNJ-17|Liam O'Connor] Sheriff, “because nobody knew the port better than he did” (doc 05), “and because that kept him on her side” (doc 07, O'Connor's profile). O'Connor survived her fall and served Whitmore for sixteen more years (doc 07).
- In 1958 she Embraced Charles Halloway for his press contacts (doc 06, Halloway's profile: “**Sire:** Adelaide Grange (PNJ-22) · **Embraced:** 1958”).
- In the spring of 1962, the year of the World's Fair, she “left the city ‘for health reasons’ and was never seen again” (doc 05). @ID[PNJ-02|Marcus Whitmore] took the throne. “Whitmore understood something Grange never saw: in a city that looks forward, power isn't imposed, it's displayed” (doc 05).
- ID registry: “PNJ-22 · Adelaide Grange, former Ventrue Prince, vanished in 1962 (background)” (doc 05); “PNJ-22 · Adelaide Grange, Halloway's sire, driven out by Whitmore in 1962” (doc 06).

## The Truth of 1962

- Whitmore gathered evidence of a Masquerade breach by Grange, “some real and some exaggerated,” and showed it to her in private. She left the city without a fight. It was a clean coup, without bloodshed (doc 06, Whitmore's profile). His strategy with Halloway is the same one he used with Grange: wait for him to overreach (doc 06).
- Halloway saw it up close: his sire “had been defeated without a drop of blood,” and the method worked. In 1978 he used it against O'Connor (doc 06, Halloway's profile).

## How They Feel About Her

- **Whitmore** (unease): “He never knew whether she left or whether others made her disappear. He prefers not to know” (doc 06).
- **Halloway:** “Halloway knows where Grange is.” *(False. He'd give anything to know)* (doc 06).

## Rumors

- “Whitmore ran Grange out with blackmail.” *(True)* (doc 06).
- “Halloway is the childe of former Prince Grange.” *(True. Few people remember)* (doc 06).

## Echoes in the Adventure

- In @ID[A4-E01], if the coterie defends the Prince well, Whitmore tells Halloway: “Charles. Sixty-four years ago I watched your sire make the same mistake. She believed a good story was the same thing as the truth” (doc 13). Halloway's sire is Grange (doc 06).
- In @ID[FIN-B], Whitmore abdicates “‘for health reasons,’ like Grange in 1962, and vanishes from the city” (doc 13).
