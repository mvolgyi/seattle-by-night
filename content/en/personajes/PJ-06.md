# PJ-06 · Dr. Ruth Abernathy · Tremere · “The On-Call Medical Examiner” ***(alternate)***

**Concept:** night-shift pathologist at the county medical examiner's office and a member of Voight's chantry.

## Mortal Days

Ruth performed autopsies for twelve years. She was meticulous, dry, and had no patience for the living. In 2004 she signed the report on a body with no blood and no visible wounds. The next day the body had vanished from the morgue cooler, and her boss asked her to “revise” the report.

## Kindred Nights

Dr. Anneliese Voight (@ID[PNJ-15]) Embraced her in 2005: a medical examiner who asks questions is a danger, but a medical examiner who answers to the chantry is a weapon. Since then, Ruth is the reason Kindred deaths in Seattle end up as “overdose,” “accidental fire,” or “animal attack.” This week she's on leave: a substitute is signing the night-shift reports. She feeds in hospitals, on sedated patients.

## Connection to the Coterie

The File holds a table of Helen's listing fifteen “impossible” autopsy reports. Every one of them bears Ruth's signature.

## Ambition, Desire, and Touchstone

**Ambition:** find the body that disappeared in 2004. **Desire:** one night without corpses. **Suggested Conviction:** “The dead deserve the truth, even if the living don't.” **Touchstone:** her ex-husband, a retired homicide detective who still calls her when he can't sleep.
