# Dr. Anneliese Voight · She Who Watches the Net

*“The epidemic never begins with the first death. It begins with the first data point nobody wanted to look at.”*

**Clan:** Tremere · **Positions:** Regent of the Observatory Chantry and Primogen · **Sect:** Camarilla

## Mortal Days: The Statistician of Vienna

Anneliese Voight earned her doctorate in medical statistics at the University of Vienna in 1919, when women were barely allowed into the lecture halls. She spent the influenza pandemic counting the dead on maps of the city and was one of the first to see that the infections followed the streetcar lines. Nobody listened to her in time. Ever since, she can't stand it when someone ignores a pattern.

## Kindred Nights: The Regent of the Dome

A Regent of the Vienna Chantry Embraced her in 1924 so she would apply her methods to something more important than the flu. In 1964 she was sent to Seattle to run a small Chantry the Pyramid considered irrelevant. When the Pyramid fell, Voight kept the Chantry, its books, and her independence, and she joined none of the houses that emerged.

Since 2019 she has turned the old Queen Anne observatory (@ID[LUG-13]) into a network surveillance center. She was the first to detect FIRSTLIGHT in the city and the one who imposed the 2024 law on devices in Elysium. She wrote the Prince a detailed report that took him two nights to open. She's beginning to think the Seattle Camarilla won't survive its Prince.

## Plans and Machinations

**Watch Northline.** She intercepts part of the Puget Cell's traffic. She knows there's an inside source, but she doesn't know who ARCHER is. She'd give a lot to find out.

**Protect Ruth.** She knows FIRSTLIGHT has marked her childe (@ID[PJ-06]) or the NPC medical examiner who takes her place. She's preparing an evacuation plan.

**A Prince who listens.** She doesn't want Halloway to win, but she doesn't want Whitmore to stay either. If the coterie proves it knows how to read patterns, it could become her candidate in the shadows.

## Domain and Haven

The Observatory Chantry in Queen Anne (@ID[LUG-13]; Haven 3, with Blood Sorcery wards). She feeds on blood-bank blood the Chantry buys through a research lab, and occasionally on researchers working late on the university campus.

## Mask and Appearance

To mortals she's Dr. A. Voight, director of a private computational epidemiology lab affiliated with the university (Mask 2). She looks about thirty-three. She's petite, with very short hair in her natural color, and she wears rimless glasses, black turtlenecks, and a lab coat she forgets to take off. She speaks perfect English with a Viennese accent. She has a habit of answering questions with a percentage.
