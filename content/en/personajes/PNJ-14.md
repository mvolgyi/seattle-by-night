# Solène Marchetti · She Who Takes Notes

*“I don't judge. I record. Time does the judging, and time always reads my books.”*

**Clan:** Toreador · **Positions:** Keeper of Elysium and Toreador Primogen · **Sect:** Camarilla

## Mortal Days: The Calligrapher of Montreal

Solène was born in 1884 in Montreal, the daughter of an Italian typesetter and a seamstress from Quebec. Her handwriting was so beautiful that by fourteen she was already copying parish registers and notarial documents. She spent twenty years writing down other people's lives: births, weddings, wills, deaths. She liked the idea that nothing she wrote would ever be lost.

## Kindred Nights: The Memory of the Court

A Toreador of the Montreal court Embraced her in 1912 to handle his correspondence. She outlived him. In 1955 she arrived in Seattle with a letter of recommendation for Whitmore, who was not yet Prince. When he took the throne in 1962, he named her Keeper of Elysium. Since then she has kept fourteen registers, one for every five years, all written by hand.

She is loyal to Whitmore out of habit and affection, but above all she is loyal to the record. She believes an Elysium without memory is just a party. She knows more about Seattle's political history than anyone, because she has seen all of it and written all of it down. What she doesn't do is interpret. She leaves that to others.

## Plans and Machinations

**The record as insurance.** Without anyone having thought of it that way, her books are another File: the history of every Kindred in Seattle since 1962. If Halloway realizes this, Marchetti will be his next target.

**Neutrality.** She will keep Elysium open no matter what. If Halloway wins, she will record him as Prince in the same handwriting.

**The blank entry.** On the night of November 12, the night of the theft, she wrote nothing. She doesn't know why. It worries her.

## Domain and Haven

An apartment above the archive of the Glass Gallery, with floor-to-ceiling shelves (Haven 2). She feeds on artisans: calligraphers, bookbinders, glassblowers. She visits them in their workshops in the small hours, when they work alone, and buys a piece from them before she drinks.

## Servants and Tools

**The Gallery staff** (Allies 2): five ghouls who serve Elysium.

**The registers**: fourteen volumes of the city's Kindred history.

**Status 3** (Camarilla).

## Mask and Appearance

To mortals she is the director of the Gallery foundation's archive (Mask 2). She looks about twenty-eight. She is tall, with dark hair gathered in a low bun, and wears old-fashioned dresses in shades of gray and glasses on a chain. She writes with a fountain pen. She rarely smiles, but when she does, she means it. She never raises her voice.
