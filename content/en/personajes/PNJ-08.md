# Justicar Helena Panhard · The Missing Signature

**Background figure** (no actor sheet) · Camarilla Justicar · **Role in @ID[AVE-01]:** the authority to whom @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] wants to send the regency minutes; with her recognition, “the regency becomes a crown.”

*This page gathers what docs 00–16 say about Panhard. In parentheses, the source doc for each fact. The docs give her no explicit clan, age, or stats; the Council's order of business speaks of “the Justicar of Clan Ventrue” (doc 15, @ID[AYU-08]).*

## Who She Is

- Background figure alongside Archon @ID[PNJ-07|Duval] and @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] (doc 01, NPC index; ID registry v2: “PNJ-08 · Justicar Helena Panhard (background)”).
- Duval acts as her envoy (doc 13).
- She holds copy 3 of Vance's journals, the one Mr. Pell typed: Halloway sent it to her in 2025 “as a goodwill gift” (doc 09, @ID[GAN-07]; ID registry: “Copy 3 of 3 of Vance's journals, held by Justicar Panhard”).

## Panhard and Halloway

- The city's **Rumor 4**, “Halloway has Justicar Panhard's support”: half true. “He's been writing to her for a year. She doesn't answer him, but she doesn't tell him no either” (doc 05).
- Halloway started writing to her a year ago; to him she is his “hope”: “If she recognizes his regency, no one in Seattle will be able to oppose him” (doc 06, Halloway's sheet).
- Whisper: “Halloway has the Justicar in his pocket.” *(False. She hasn't answered him)* (doc 06).
- Halloway told her about @ID[PJ-02|Celeste]: if David turns Duval down in GAN-07, Celeste receives a parallel invitation from the Justicar (doc 09).
- At the Council (@ID[A4-E01]), if a majority of the Primogen votes for the “emergency regency,” Halloway “can send the minutes to Justicar Panhard, with whom he's been corresponding for a year. With a Justicar's blessing, the regency becomes a crown” (doc 13). The motion asks that the minutes be forwarded “to the Justicar of Clan Ventrue for her information and confirmation” (doc 15, AYU-08).
- The original of the File “is the evidence Panhard asked him for in order to recognize the regency” (doc 13, A4-E01 Notes).

:::recuadro Two Versions of the Correspondence
Docs 05 and 06 say Panhard doesn't answer Halloway. Doc 13 says she asked him for the File as evidence and, in an ambiguous phrase, that “he's been corresponding with her for a year.” If it matters at your table, decide whether the Justicar has already answered or whether Halloway only believes she will.
:::

## Panhard in the Endings

- **@ID[FIN-B]:** Halloway is Regent “with Justicar Panhard's recognition if the File reached Duval” (doc 13).
- **Partial FIN-B:** if Halloway wins the Council but the coterie keeps the File, “Halloway doesn't have his evidence before the Justicar: his regency is weak” (doc 13, endings table).
- **@ID[FIN-C]:** if Halloway won the Council and the File is destroyed, “his regency lacks the evidence the Justicar asked for: Panhard doesn't recognize it.” Seattle is left in political limbo (doc 13).

## Rumors
