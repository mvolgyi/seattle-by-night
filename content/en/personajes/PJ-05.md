# PJ-05 · Halvard “Hal” Brekke · Gangrel · “The Wolf of Ballard”

**Concept:** former crab fisherman, childe of Sheriff Montgomery, and member of his pack.

## Mortal Days

Hal is the grandson of Norwegian fishermen from Ballard. He spent his summers on the Bering Sea, on boats where someone died every season. He was the one who never got seasick, the one who went out on deck in thirty-foot waves. In 1994, a storm sank his boat. He was the only one who made it to shore alive, half frozen, on a beach where Montgomery was hunting.

## Kindred Nights

Montgomery (@ID[PNJ-01]) Embraced him that night because he saw someone who didn't know how to give up. Since then Hal has been one of Whitmore's Wolves (@ID[COT-01]): he tracks, covers up, and keeps watch. He has known David for thirty years. He was the backup who reached the apartment the night Emily died, and he was the one who cleaned the scene while David was still on his knees. They've never talked about it. He feeds on fishermen and longshoremen in the waterfront bars (*Alleycat*).

## Connection to the Coterie

The File has a 2023 cell phone video of a man running after a car, faster than the car. It's him.

## Ambition, Desire, and Touchstone

**Ambition:** earn a domain of his own. **Desire:** for Montgomery to tell him, just once, that he did well. **Suggested Conviction:** “Never leave anyone in the water.” **Touchstone:** his niece Ingrid, who now captains the family boat and saves him a seat at the Christmas table even though he never comes.
