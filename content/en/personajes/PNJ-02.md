# Marcus Whitmore · The Glass Prince

*“A Prince doesn't rule. He chooses who rules in his name, and applauds.”*

**Clan:** Toreador · **Position:** Prince of Seattle · **Sect:** Camarilla

## Mortal Days: The Impresario

Marcus Whitmore was born in Baltimore in 1838 and came to San Francisco with the Gold Rush, though not to look for gold. He opened a theater. Then another. By 1870 he owned the most elegant variety hall on the West Coast, where rich miners spent in one night what they had dug up in a year. He was charming, generous, and up to his neck in debt. His talent was making people feel part of something beautiful.

## Kindred Nights: The One Who Never Got His Hands Dirty

A Toreador soprano at the San Francisco opera Embraced him in 1871, enchanted with the man who had filled her theater. Whitmore spent eighty years at that city's court learning what he does best: being in the picture. In 1952 he moved to Seattle, and in 1962, with the World's Fair, he took the throne.

This is how he did it, though no one tells the story: Whitmore gathered evidence of a Masquerade breach by Prince Grange, some of it real and some of it exaggerated, and showed it to her in private. She left the city without a fight. It was a clean coup, bloodless, and Whitmore has ruled the same way ever since: letting others get dirty. Montgomery gets dirty for him. Halloway, who was Grange's childe, learned the technique by watching him.

## Plots and Schemes

**Voight's envelope.** He opened it two nights after Elysium. It says FIRSTLIGHT has a cell in the city and that someone at court might be feeding it. He did nothing. He expects Montgomery to handle it.

**Let him overreach.** He suspects Halloway is behind something. His strategy is to wait for him to go too far and punish him once it's obvious to everyone. It's the same strategy he used on Grange. This time he may be too late.

**The collection.** He Embraces artists the way others buy paintings. Nadia Ferro (@ID[PJ-04]) is his latest piece. If she fails him in public, he'll make her pay for it.

## Mask and Appearance

To mortals he is Marcus Whitmore III, the discreet philanthropist who chairs an arts foundation (Mask 3, with a fictitious family line that renews itself every forty years). He looks about thirty-three. He is tall, with slicked-back hair, no gray, and very pale gray eyes. He wears impeccably cut suits without a tie and a single ring set with a black stone. His voice is warm and his laugh contagious. He makes anyone feel like the most important guest in the room. He hates being rushed.
