# Ethan Montgomery · The Sentinel of Puget Sound

*“I am the guardian between the Beast and the city. If one wakes, the other falls.”*

**Clan:** Gangrel · **Position:** Sheriff of Seattle · **Sect:** Camarilla (out of personal loyalty to the Prince)

## Mortal Days: The Man Who Could Smell Footsteps

He was born in 1845 to a family of settlers on the Rogue River, in Oregon Territory. He learned to track before he learned to read. By twenty he was already working for the army and the railroad companies, running down outlaws, deserters, and horse thieves, and on the frontier they said that if Montgomery was on your trail, it didn't matter how fast you ran. In 1885 he spent three weeks hunting a killer of travelers near Eureka. When he finally caught up with him, he discovered that his quarry wasn't a man.

## Kindred Nights: Whitmore's Wolf

The nomad Gangrel Elijah Kane (@ID[PNJ-09]) spared his life because he found him interesting and Embraced him because he found him useful. Montgomery spent eighteen years roaming the coast with him. In 1903, after the San Francisco earthquake, he entered the service of the Camarilla and never left it. In 1978, Whitmore named him Sheriff of Seattle to replace O'Connor.

Since then he has kept the city clean without asking for anything in return. When his clan broke with the Camarilla, he stayed. He didn't do it for the Prince, whom he considers weak, but for the city, which he considers his. Now he is tired and afraid, though he would never say so. The theft of the File and the ashes on the docks smell to him like something he can't name.

## Plans and Schemes

**The list.** Montgomery has written down the names of eleven Kindred he will destroy the night Whitmore falls, before anyone can stop him. The first is Halloway.

**Watching the Hound.** Since 1996 he has received a monthly report on David, first from several bounty hunters and, for years now, from his childe Hal (@ID[PJ-05]). He trusts David more than anyone, and that is exactly why he never stops watching him.

**Vance?** He knows Vance only by reputation and genuinely fears him. If David's sire is alive and in the city, Montgomery believes David's loyalty will split in two. Halloway exploits exactly that fear.

**The Larchmont.** He was the one who ordered the cistern sealed with seven bodies inside (@ID[LUG-11]). If that comes out, he falls, and probably the Prince with him.

## Domain and Haven

A farm in the Snohomish Valley with a tunnel down to a boat landing on the river (@ID[LUG-02]; Haven 3). He feeds on the line between rural and urban: truckers at rest areas, hunters in the woods, the occasional deer when the Hunger bites and there are no humans around. He never feeds inside the city, because he says the Sheriff doesn't hunt where he patrols.

## Servants and Tools

**The bounty hunters** (@ID[MEN-03]; Herd 2): six mortals, ex-military and ex-cops, who know him as “the Boss.”

**The dogs**: twelve ghouled working dogs that sense and obey his will (Animalism).

**Contacts 3**: state police, the port, and security firms. They cover up deaths and steer investigations elsewhere.

**Allies 3**: David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]).

## Relationships with Kindred

**@ID[PJ-01|David Smith]** (respect). He calls him “Hound,” and he doesn't mean it as an insult. He sees in him what he himself would be if he lost his soul.

**@ID[PJ-05|Hal Brekke]** (pride). He has never told him so. He makes Hal hand over the reports on David and knows Hal hates him for it.

**@ID[PNJ-02|Marcus Whitmore]** (loyalty). He doesn't respect him. He serves him because the alternative is worse.

**@ID[PNJ-03|Charles Halloway]** (contempt). He thinks he's a snake in a suit. What he lacks is the proof to act.

**@ID[PNJ-17|Liam O'Connor]** (guilt). He took his post without asking questions. He knows he should have asked them.

### 🩸 Background and History

Ethan Montgomery was a **tracker** before he was a hunter. He was born into a family of pioneers of the American West who settled near the Rogue River.

He grew up close to nature and to the violence that survival demanded in territories contested by humans, beasts, and, unbeknownst to him, the children of Caine.

During the railroad expansion, Montgomery worked as a **tracker and outlaw hunter** for the army and private companies.

His talent for following human quarry made him a local legend:

> “If Montgomery's on your trail, it doesn't matter how fast you run, he can already smell you.”

In 1885, he hunted a killer who was slaughtering travelers near Eureka.

When he finally caught up with him, he discovered that his quarry **wasn't human**. The vampire he was hunting beat him easily, but before killing him, found him... fascinating.

He offered him “a new way to hunt.”

That was his Embrace, under the tutelage of a **nomad Gangrel named Elijah Kane**, who trained him in Cainite survival.

For decades, Ethan roamed the West Coast eliminating Sabbat, out-of-control childer, and monsters that threatened the Masquerade.

His disciplined nature caught the attention of the Prince of Seattle, **Marcus Whitmore**, who named him **Sheriff** in 1978.

### 🧠 Personality

Montgomery is a man of few words, with an almost animal presence.

His calm is as unsettling as his restrained violence.

He treats the city's vampires as **predators allowed to live under his watch**, not as citizens.

He has a professional respect for @ID[PJ-01|David Smith], whom he considers *“the younger version of what I'd be if I lost my soul.”*

To neonates, Sheriff Montgomery is a figure of fear and respect.

To the Elders, he is a **living weapon of the Camarilla** that keeps both the Sabbat and the rebel Brujah in check.
