# AYU-02 · Index of the Ward File

**WHAT WAS IN THE BASEMENT**

*(from memory, 11/12, 4:20 a.m.—I'm definitely forgetting something)*

**Structure:** twelve years, 2014–2026. One folder per year. Inside, one subfolder per case. 214 cases total, I think. Plus the unnamed folder. Plus the maps.

**The heaviest cases** (the ones with the most material):

- **2014 · LARCHMONT.** Seven “unaccounted for.” Danny. Forty-one photos of the fire, the fire department report that doesn't add up, the doorman's statement that he later retracted. THE PHOTO.
- **2015 · GEORGETOWN FOUNDRY.** Two night watchmen “missing.” One came back three weeks later remembering nothing.
- **2016–2018 · HARBOR ISLAND.** “Animal” attacks at the port. Bodies with almost no blood. The case where I met him.
- **2017 · BELLEVUE PARKING GARAGE.** A “suicide.” A young woman, very well dressed, crouched next to the body. She's not the one who jumped.
- **2018 · PIONEER SQUARE.** Thermal capture of a figure going into a sewer. The figure has no temperature.
- **2019–2022 · THE RAINIER DOSES.** “Overdose” deaths in a two-mile pattern. Nobody with a history.
- **2021 · THE IMPOSSIBLE REPORTS.** Fifteen county medical examiner's reports with details that don't fit. All with the same signature: R. A.
- **2023 · THE RUNNING MAN.** Cell phone video, Ballard: a guy runs after a car faster than the car.
- **2024–2026 · CROWN HILL.** The three kids. Kyle Brandt. The mother from Lake City.
- **Several years · N.** My own producer. She's never in the group photos. Coincidence? *[Crossed out. Next to it:]* Don't be paranoid, Helen.

**The unnamed folder:** fourteen photos. 2014–2025. The same man. The same scar. *[Underlined twice.]*

**The maps:** one map per year with digital pins. If you overlay them, you see the pattern: zones where nothing ever happens and zones where everything happens. Borders. Like territories.

**Recordings:** 300-some interviews. The voice memos (7 so far on this case). W.'s messages.

*[At the bottom of the last page, smaller, pressing the pen differently:]*

If someone has all of this, they can find them all. All of them, whatever they are. And I did this. Me.
