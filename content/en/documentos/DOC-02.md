# DOC-02 · “What the City Forgets,” Episode 112: “The Ones Who Don't Age”

**WHAT THE CITY FORGETS**

Episode 112—“The Ones Who Don't Age”

Aired 10/03/2026 · Recording script v4 · Producer: N. Ferro

*[THEME. 12 s. Duck under voice.]*

**HELEN:** There's a photo I'm not going to show you. Not because I don't have it. I do. I've had it for eleven years, and I look at it more than I should. It's a photo of a fire in Tacoma, and in the photo there's a man with his back to the flames. And there's another photo, from last year, down on the docks. And it's the same man.

*[N.F. margin note: 2 s pause here. Let it breathe.]*

**HELEN:** Not the same kind of man. The same man. Same scar, same way of standing. Eleven years and he hasn't aged a day. You're going to say it's a brother, a son, a lookalike. I said that too. Then I found others.

**HELEN:** A source I can't name, who always writes to me at the same hour, sent me a map last week. On the map were spots where, according to this source, the city “loses its memory.” Buildings where the cameras always fail in the same time window. Bars where nobody remembers seeing certain customers. A stretch of the port where incident reports disappear on their own.

**HELEN:** I don't know what this is. I know what it isn't. It isn't coincidence.

*[CUT TO LISTENER CALL. Male voice, older. Identified on screen as “W.”]*

**LISTENER (W.):** Hi, Helen. I've been listening since the beginning. I wanted to tell you you're not crazy. And to be careful, because the ones who don't age don't forget either. And that the rain has ears. That's all. Oh, and I like the new music.

**HELEN:** Thanks, W. It's always you who calls.

*[N.F. note: we're keeping the call. It's perfect.]*

**HELEN:** Next week: three deaths in Crown Hill the county is calling overdoses. And a mother who says her son was afraid of needles.

*[THEME. End.]*
