# AYU-04 · Emily Smith's Obituary

**THE PUGET COURIER** · Obituaries · Thursday, October 24, 1996

**EMILY ROSE SMITH**

February 14, 1958 – October 21, 1996

Emily Smith, a caseworker with the King County Department of Youth Services, died Monday in Rainier Valley while attending to minors in her care. She was 38.

Born in San Diego, she was raised in Seattle by her mother, Anne Smith, who died in 1989. Her father, Marine Sgt. David Smith, died on active duty in 1960, when Emily was two.

She earned her degree in social work from the University of Washington in 1981 and spent fifteen years working with at-risk youth in the south end of the city. Colleagues remember her as “the one who never hung up the phone” and “the one who took the cases nobody else wanted.”

Police are investigating the incident as a confrontation between gangs. One of the minors was unharmed.

She is not survived by any immediate family. A private service will be held Saturday at the cemetery in the cedars. In lieu of flowers, the family asks that donations be made to the Rainier Valley Youth Center.
