# DOC-12 · Note Kept in Bear's Safe

If you're reading this, either I'm dead or I had to do it.

If I'm dead: the truck key is in the coffee can. The guns that aren't his go to the bottom of the canal, don't sell them. Send the blue envelope to my sister in Philadelphia and don't tell her anything. Nothing.

If I had to do it, then this is for you, David, even though you won't read it, because you won't be here.

I promised myself three things when I drank the first time. No kids. No people who didn't do anything. No killing for the hell of it. You told me your code was a lot like mine. I believed you. I still believe you. But I'm writing this because one night I stopped being sure, and I promised myself that if you ever crossed that line all the way, I'd be the one to stop you. Not another vampire. Not somebody's orders. Me. Because I owe you that.

If I did it, forgive me. If I didn't and you're reading this because I died, then it was never needed, and that's the best thing that happened to me in this strange life.

Cover the door, Hound. I can't anymore.

— T. W.
