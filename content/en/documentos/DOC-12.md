# DOC-12 · Note Kept in Bear's Safe

If you're reading this, either I'm dead or I had to do it.

If I'm dead: the truck key is in the coffee can. The guns that aren't his go to the bottom of the canal, don't sell them. Send my sister in Philadelphia the blue envelope and don't tell her anything. Nothing.

If I had to do it, then this is for you, David, even though you won't read it because you won't be around.

I made myself three promises the first time I drank. No kids. No people who didn't do anything. No killing just because. You told me your code was a lot like mine. I believed you. I still believe you. But I'm writing this because one night I stopped being sure, and I promised myself that if you ever crossed it all the way, the one who stopped you was going to be me. Not some other vampire. Not anybody's orders. Me. Because I owe you that.

If I did it, forgive me. If I didn't and you're reading this because I died, then it was never needed, and that's the best thing that happened to me in this strange life.

Cover the door, Hound. I can't anymore.

— T. W.

## For the Storyteller {narrador}

**Medium:** a sheet torn from a notepad, folded in quarters, kept under a pistol in Tom Whitman's personal safe at the hangar (@ID[LUG-01]). **When:** not handed out during the adventure. Use it if Bear dies, if FIRSTLIGHT raids the hangar (@ID[A4-E02]), or as the close of @ID[GAN-04]. Give it privately to the player whose PC has had the most to do with Bear (@ID[A3-E01]). That PC decides whether to show it to David. The horror is holding it in your hand.

**Foreshadows:** Bear's moral line and what he would do if David crosses it.

**Truth:** Bear wrote it two years ago, after a night he doesn't talk about. He hasn't read it since.
