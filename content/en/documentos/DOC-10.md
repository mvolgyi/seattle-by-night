# DOC-10 · Wendell Fry: “Song for the Son of the Fist”

*Song for the Son of the Fist*

There is a father who isn't here

and two sons who wait for him,

one in the water, one in the rain,

both with the same guard of stone.

One finished the lesson.

The other learned half,

and the half, when it rots,

weighs more than the truth.

Don't look for the father on the pier.

The coat isn't his who wears it.

Look for the one who signs with his left,

the one who asks to be seen.

The city has blue eyes

that never blink.

The glass king smiles.

The queen of the rain takes notes.

And the last boat of the night

carries off what no one wanted to read.

If you come late, Hound,

come this time.

*— W.*
