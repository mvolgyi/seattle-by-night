# DOC-03 · Register of the Keeper of Elysium, November Folio

**REGISTER OF THE ELYSIUM OF SEATTLE**

*Glass Gallery · Book XIV · Folio 211*

**Night of October 22.** Regular session. Present: H.H. Prince Whitmore, the full Primogen, and the Sheriff. No incidents. Primogen Halloway requests a private audience with H.H. Denied owing to the schedule.

**Night of October 29.** Sheriff Montgomery withdraws before midnight without taking leave of H.H. No one remarks on it aloud.

**Night of November 5.** Regent Voight delivers to H.H. an envelope sealed with black wax. H.H. does not open it in the hall. Primogen Halloway again requests a private audience. Denied. This Keeper notes that the Primogen smiled upon receiving the answer.

**Night of November 5 (cont.).** A guest is relieved of a phone he was carrying, switched on, in his inside pocket. He is reminded of the fourth tradition of this Elysium: *no connected device crosses the threshold*. He apologizes. The device is returned to him on his way out, switched off.

**Night of November 5 (cont.).** Mr. Wendell Fry, of Clan Malkavian, asks that the following be entered into the record: “The fist has two sons, and neither of them knows it.” So entered.

**Night of November 12.** *[Entry left blank. Only the date.]*

*S. M., Keeper*
