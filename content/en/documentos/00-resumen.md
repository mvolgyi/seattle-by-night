# Chapter 2 · In-World Documents

Seattle leaves traces. The twelve fragments in this chapter are some of them: voice memos, messages saved on an old phone, a handwritten log, an email nobody was supposed to read. Each one is written as the object itself and can be printed or uploaded to Foundry as is, to hand out at the table.

Before each document there's a sidebar for the Storyteller: when to hand it out, what it foreshadows, and what's true in it. That sidebar isn't shown to the players.

*Reference dates:* Night 1 of the adventure (the theft) is Thursday, November 12, 2026.
