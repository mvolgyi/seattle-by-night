# Book Index and ID Registry

## 0 · Opening Fiction—“What the Water Doesn't Take”

A 2,000–2,500-word story. It's the night of the theft seen through the eyes of Helen Ward (@ID[PNJ-04]): the podcast, the rain in Ballard, the flip phone with a single number, and someone who's already inside the apartment. It ends when Helen dials SAFE. David appears only as a voice on the phone. *(There are two other possible points of view; see open decisions.)*

## 1 · Introduction

- What this book is about
- How to use this book: a chronicle for a coterie of 4–5 players, with David and Bear as allied NPCs *(the doc 01 index said “for one player, with the coterie variant in a sidebar”)*
- Themes and mood: the city that records everything
- Safety Tools: Lines and Veils, the X-Card, and the content warnings in @ID[AVE-01]
- Quick Seattle glossary (neighborhoods, local slang, how the names are pronounced)
