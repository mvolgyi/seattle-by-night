# From the Other Side

Three playable interludes with the hunters of the Puget Cell (@ID[CEL-01]). For how and when to use them, see @ID[FIRSTLIGHT-JUGABLE]. For what they leave behind for the raid, see @ID[INT-00]. For the character sheets, see @ID[FIRSTLIGHT-PREGENERADOS].

This entire journal is new content. The schedules, the van, and what appears on the recordings are **Proposed** except where a doc is cited.

## INT-01 · The White Van {#INT-01}

### Storyteller Context

Play this when the Thermometer reaches 3 (👁 *Someone Is Watching the Journalist*). FIRSTLIGHT puts @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] under passive surveillance: a van outside her building on Market Street (@ID[LUG-05]), occasional drones, and an analyst who listens closely to her podcast. That much is in the docs. What follows is that night, from inside the van.

*Proposed:* the van is white, no rear windows, with the logo of a fiber-optic company that doesn't exist. It arrives at dusk the night after the threshold is crossed and parks half a block down, across from the closed hardware store. It stays until seven.

The interlude has two parts. **The footage:** Hale pulls from Northline what the Gale Sentinel doorbell cameras recorded on Market Street in the early hours of the theft, and the players watch, from the outside, the scene their vampires played in @ID[A0-E01]. **The street:** the stakeout itself, with whatever Helen does and whatever the coterie does.

**Who's there:** @ID[CAZ-02|Pak] on the drone and the screens, @ID[PNJ-21|Hale] on the headphones, @ID[CAZ-01|Navarro] on the window camera, @ID[MEN-11|Ransom] behind the wheel. @ID[CAZ-03|Sorensen], if someone's playing her, rides shotgun because she knows the street: she grew up four blocks away. @ID[PNJ-18|Okafor] is at Northline, on the radio.

:::leer
The rain sounds like gravel on the roof of the van. Inside, it smells of cold coffee and hot plastic. Three screens: the street in green, the street in gray, the street as a heat map where everything alive is orange. Across the way, above a closed hardware store, there's a third-floor window. Hale has one earphone in and the other dangling. In the one she's wearing, a woman is talking about a man who doesn't age.
:::

### What the Hunters Want

- **Hale:** cross-reference the voice from episode 112 with what she sees on the screens. If there's a pattern, she wants to be the first to see it.
- **Pak:** prove the drone sees more than the van. Always.
- **Navarro:** see one. Prove Tucson wasn't made up.
- **Ransom:** work the shift, ask no questions, go back to the warehouse. And yet he asks.
- **Sorensen:** get paid for the night. And not be recognized by anyone from the street.
- **Okafor (by radio):** a clean report. “Facts. Theories tomorrow.”

### The Footage

Hale requests from Northline the ANOMALY logs for Market Street between 2:30 and 4:30 a.m. on Night 1. What they see depends on what the coterie did in @ID[A0-E01]:

| In A0-E01 the coterie… | What's on the footage |
|---|---|
| Left down the hallway without touching the 3B camera | Three COLD_SUBJECT alerts in the third-floor hallway, one after another. Gray silhouettes in an orange hallway. Among them, a warm figure: Helen. The faces are only half visible |
| Smashed the camera | The last image: a hand moving faster than it should. Before that, nothing useful |
| Disconnected the camera carefully, or left by the fire escape | No alerts. Northline only receives alerts, not video. The only odd thing is the disconnection log for the whole block, from 2:52 to 3:03 (@pista{P-01}): eleven clean minutes. Hale circles it |
| Used Obfuscate in front of the camera | The camera records the others, not whoever used it. A door in the hallway that opens by itself. Hale watches it four times |

In every case, a doorbell camera on the opposite sidewalk records two things without triggering an alert, because they're warm: **a pickup truck** parked half a block down, with a big man at the wheel who never gets out (@ID[PNJ-05|Bear]), and **a gray electric car** with someone inside, which leaves at 4:10 (the watcher from @ID[MEN-04]). The hunters don't know who either of them is.

### The Street

After the footage, the night. What happens depends on where Helen is:

- **Helen is at home.** At 1:20 a.m. she goes down to buy cigarettes at the gas station on the corner. If any of the coterie's vampires is with her that night, ask the player what they do: the hunters see it from the van. On the thermal screen, whoever walks beside Helen isn't orange.
- **Helen is with the coterie.** The third-floor window is dark. Ask the table whether any of their vampires went back that night for clothes, papers, or the cat. If someone did, the hunters see them go in. If no one did, it's a stakeout: rain, coffee, the podcast, and at 4:10 a.m., the same time as the night of the theft, the gray electric car rolls slowly past the front door and leaves.

If nothing happens on the street, the interlude is the footage and a conversation. Don't drag it out: stakeouts are boring inside the fiction, not at the table.

### Tests

- **Pull something from the footage:** @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|3|Hale: Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Inquisitive)*. With the Perk *Close Listening*, +2 dice if she cross-references the footage with episode 112. On a success, the full timeline. **With 3 or more successes**, and only if there were alerts, a usable frame of one of the faces.
- **Watch the street:** @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.awareness|3|Navarro or Ransom: Wits + Awareness (Difficulty 3)}. With the drone up, +2 dice (@ID[dron-termico]).
- **Fly the drone without being heard:** @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.technology|3|Pak: Dexterity + Technology (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Underground)*. In heavy rain, Difficulty 4.
- **Keep the van from drawing attention:** if a vampire passes nearby, @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.stealth|3|Ransom: Dexterity + Stealth (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Underground, if Pak is driving)* against the @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.awareness|0|PC's Wits + Awareness} the Storyteller calls for. At Danger 3 or higher, Difficulty 4.
- **Recognize the man in the truck:** only Ransom, and only if Bear shows up on the footage. @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.insight|2|Ransom: Wits + Insight (Difficulty 2)}. On a success, it's Tom Whitman. They served together. He hasn't heard from him in years.

### Decisions

| **Ransom recognizes Bear and tells Okafor** | That's @ID[GAN-04], “Ransom Reports”: @termometro{+2|GAN-04: Ransom tells Okafor about Bear}. Advantage **V2** (the truck). Bear is marked |

### How It Affects A4-E02

It can leave **V1**, **V2**, **F1**, and **F2** (@ID[INT-00]). Nothing else: what's seen on Market Street doesn't reveal where the coterie is hiding.

### Closing

At 6:40 a.m. the sky turns a dirty gray. Pak brings the drone down with the battery at four percent. Hale keeps one earphone in and writes in her notebook: “Why him?” Ransom starts the engine. Return to the coterie's scene where you left it.

### Storyteller Notes

- **Don't show faces the coterie didn't let the camera record.** If they dealt with the camera in @ID[A0-E01], the footage has nothing. Let that be the reward for doing it right.
- **Bear always shows up on the footage**, because he stayed in the truck watching the street. Whether Ransom recognizes him depends on someone playing Ransom; if no one is, no one recognizes him.
- **The gray car** is a loose end for the hunters, not for the coterie: don't resolve it. Hale can note the time. Nothing more.

## INT-02 · SEA-0411 {#INT-02}

### Storyteller Context

@reloj{5}, Monday, November 16, from 8:00 p.m. on. Northline (@ID[LUG-14]), 11th floor: three lights on and the conference room with the blinds down. ARCHER makes the second delivery through the agreed channel. It's the memo Hale signs that night: @ID[DOC-07].

*Proposed:* the channel is an encrypted drop box. At 8:12 p.m. a package with no sender comes in, reference SEA-0411. Inside, what DOC-07 says: three locations of interest (a rural property in the Snohomish Valley with a boat landing, a hangar on Harbor Island, a penthouse in Capitol Hill held by a cultural foundation), the claim that the source has access “to the local structure,” and the promise of the “complete index.”

At the start, apply the delivery if you haven't already: @termometro{+1|ARCHER's second delivery (Night 5)}.

**What's already written and doesn't change:** Hale recommends treating the deliveries as possible bait (DOC-07, point 6). Okafor uses the delivery anyway, because it's all she has. What's in play is **how**.

**The night's schedule** (from the docs; it doesn't move):

- At 11:00 p.m., *nrthln_research* meets a seller at a Denny's on Aurora Avenue (@ID[A2-E06]). Okafor goes in person, with two operators in a van. She has to leave Northline at 10:15 p.m.
- If @ID[PNJ-13|Thornquist]'s auction is still on (@ID[A1-E04]), Hale goes to buy a copy later that night.
- If the Thermometer is at 5 or higher, Okafor calls Helen tonight (☎ *Someone Calls the Journalist*).

There's time for **two things** before Okafor heads down to the garage.

**Who's there:** Okafor and Hale, who are the interlude. Ransom comes up to pick Okafor up for the Denny's and can stay on as escort. Navarro and Pak, if anyone is playing them, are at the port warehouse, a phone call away.

:::leer
The corkboard covers the whole wall. Photos of Seattle at night, clippings, the transcript of episode 112 with lines underlined in three colors. Hale is standing in front of her laptop, which beeps once. A package. No sender. Reference: SEA-0411. Okafor sets her coffee on the table without looking at it and repeats, slowly: “No sender.”

### What the NPCs Want

- **Okafor:** something solid. She distrusts what comes easy, and this came on its own. If the source gives her the complete index, she requests reinforcements from national headquarters.
- **Hale:** to know why. “Every source wants something.”
- **ARCHER** (run by the Storyteller, never appearing): for the Cell to act on the list and not ask questions.
- **Ransom** (if present): for someone to tell him why those three addresses and not others.

### Tests

- **Read the sample:** @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.investigation|3|Hale or Okafor: Intelligence + Investigation (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Inquisitive)*. On a success, the three locations match areas from episode 112 (DOC-07, point 3). With *Close Listening*, automatic.
- **Read the source:** @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.insight|4|Okafor: Wits + Insight (Difficulty 4)} *(Desperation: Inquisitive)*. On a success, Okafor notices what isn't there: a source that gives up three addresses and none of its own. “Whoever wrote this hates somebody.”
- **Trace the channel:** @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|5|Hale: Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 5)} *(Desperation: Inquisitive)*. On a success, the package bounced through a data center on the Eastside. No name: just an area on the map. This is one of the night's two things.
- **Verify a target tonight:** Pak puts the drone up over one of the three, @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.technology|3|Pak: Dexterity + Technology (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Underground)*, or Navarro drives by, @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.awareness|3|Navarro: Wits + Awareness (Difficulty 3)}. If no one is playing them, Hale rolls from Northline using the city cameras, at Difficulty 4. This is the other of the two things.
- **Talk to Helen** (Thermometer 5 or higher): no roll. Okafor offers her protection and a salary, and means it. Helen decides like Helen: the Storyteller handles that in the coterie's scenes.

### Decisions

| If the Cell… | Then |
|---|---|
| **Plays along** (logs the delivery as reliable and asks for the complete index) | The three addresses go onto the target list as they arrived. Nothing else moves tonight. It's what Okafor does if no one stops her |
| **Verifies a target** | On a success at the hangar or the farm: there's activity at night. @termometro{+1|INT-02: the Cell confirms activity at one of ARCHER's targets}. Advantage **V3** for that target. Desperation −1. With a 1 on the Desperation dice and Overreach, Danger +1: Bear hears the drone, or the farm dogs bark at something they can't see |
| **Traces the channel** | On a success, Okafor writes in her notebook: “ARCHER: inside? Eastside.” Crack **F3**. Desperation +1: their only gift may be poisoned |
| **Files the delivery as bait** (they don't use it) | Nothing gets verified and the list goes in a drawer. The Cell is left with nothing: Desperation +1. If there's a raid, it arrives without **V3** |

The delivery happened regardless: the +1 from the start isn't undone. No outcome of this interlude lowers the Thermometer.

### How It Affects A4-E02

It can leave **V3** (one target) and **F3** (@ID[INT-00]). And a question in Okafor's notebook that comes back in @ID[FIRSTLIGHT-MAREA-ROJA].

### Closing

At 10:15 p.m. Okafor grabs her coat and looks at Hale's unfinished memo on the screen. “Point six,” she says. “Leave it as it is.” In the elevator her phone buzzes: *nrthln_research: confirmed, 11:00 PM*. If the Thermometer is at 5 or higher, before the doors close she dials a Ballard number. “Helen Ward? My name is Dana Okafor. Don't hang up.”

Cut. The next scene, if the coterie goes after Lonnie, is @ID[A2-E06], and Okafor is already sitting by the window.

### Storyteller Notes

- **The players see the list.** They know whose each address is; Okafor and Hale don't. Play with that: make Okafor's player decide what to do with the penthouse “of a cultural foundation” knowing whose it is.
- **The Truth of DOC-07 doesn't come out at the table.** Hale can suspect someone on the inside. She can't get to Halloway: there's nothing to get it from tonight.
- **If FIRSTLIGHT ends up with Lonnie** later (@ID[A2-E06]) and the Cell traced the channel, Okafor has two things pointing east of the lake. It isn't evidence. It's a better question.

## INT-03 · Artificial Daylight {#INT-03}

### Storyteller Context

@reloj{8}, Thursday, November 19. The Thermometer hit 9 at the end of @ID[A3-E04]: 🚨 *Knocking at the Door*. The raid (@ID[A4-E02]) is at 9:40 p.m. This interlude is what comes before: the 8:30 p.m. briefing in the port warehouse where the tactical team sleeps, the drive, and the entry, up to the exact moment the scene switches to being played with the coterie.

The Storyteller chooses the target before starting, using the table in A4-E02 (the hangar, the farm, or the penthouse). In the briefing it's on the whiteboard, circled in red.

**What's non-negotiable** (doc 13): FIRSTLIGHT arrives at 9:40 p.m. with all six operators, light body armor, incendiary ammunition, UV flashlights, the thermal drone, and the generator van. Okafor directs from a car parked a block away. The objective is to capture a Kindred alive or, failing that, destroy it and take all the material. Okafor doesn't abort: the order comes from above, and the Cell's existence is on the line.

**Who's there:** everyone. Okafor and Hale in the car, with the body cameras on a tablet. Ransom, Navarro, Sorensen, and two operators from @ID[MEN-02] on the entry team, always in pairs. Pak in the van, with the drone.

**Before starting, check the Cell's Desperation** (@ID[CEL-01]): at 3 or higher, the generator arrives already running; at 4 or higher, there's no bullhorn; at 5, Okafor goes in.

:::leer
The port warehouse has broken heating and a whiteboard on wheels. Six cots, six lockers, a coffee maker nobody cleans. The rain pounds the sheet-metal roof like it wants in. There are three photos on the whiteboard. One is circled in red. Okafor waits for the last one to sit down, puts her left hand in her pocket, and says: “Tonight isn't surveillance.”
:::

### What the Hunters Want

- **Okafor:** a Kindred alive, or the evidence that justifies the three cells. No dead operators. If she loses two, she orders a withdrawal and waits for dawn (@ID[MEN-02] tactics).
- **Hale:** the material. Phones, papers, drives. “What they leave behind is worth more than what they take.”
- **Ransom:** bring everyone home. If there's a photo of Tom Whitman on the whiteboard, that changes the night.
- **Navarro:** to be up front when one shows. Take point, with the medal and the flashlight.
- **Pak:** a perfect flight, the kind people tell stories about.
- **Sorensen:** for the door to go down on the first try and the generator to start. She's paid for both.

### The Briefing

Okafor presents the target. The players propose the plan: where they go in, where the van goes, who covers what, when the drone goes up. Then, the plan test:

- **The plan:** @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.leadership|3|Okafor: Intelligence + Leadership (Difficulty 3)}. On a success, choose **one** plan advantage: **V4** *Generator in Position*, **V5** *Radio Silence*, or **V6** *Cordon* (@ID[INT-00]). With a margin of 2 or more, **two**. On a failure, the plan is the usual one and leaves nothing.
- **V4 needs Sorensen:** @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.craft|3|Sorensen: Intelligence + Craft (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Entrepreneurial)* to have the generator warmed up without the light showing. On a failure, V4 is lost.

**Ransom and the photo.** If the target is the hangar, or if @ID[GAN-04] has already happened, there's a photo of @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] on the whiteboard with a label: *Night watchman. Thomas Whitman.* Ransom's player decides right then:

| Ransom… | Then |
|---|---|
| **Says he knows him** | Okafor looks at him for a long while. She pulls him off the door and puts him on the perimeter. If F1 was in play, it's lost: during the raid, Ransom isn't where Bear can see him |
| **Keeps quiet** | Crack **F1**, if it wasn't already in play. He goes in third with the pair at the big door |
| **Asks to go first** | F1, and something more: when Bear comes out with his hands up, Ransom is the one who cuffs him. Whatever they say to each other, they say quietly |

### The Approach

- **Arrive quietly:** @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.stealth|3|Entry team: Dexterity + Stealth (Difficulty 3)}, one per pair (Ransom, Navarro, and Sorensen roll for theirs). At Danger 3 or higher, Difficulty 4. If any fails, Crack **F4**.
- **Drone up:** @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.technology|3|Pak: Dexterity + Technology (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Underground)*. In heavy rain, Difficulty 4. On a failure, Crack **F5**. With *Low Flight* and 3 successes, the drone looks inside before anyone goes in: the players know how many are inside. Their vampires don't.
- **The body camera:** any operator can tape over theirs before going in. No roll needed. Crack **F6**, and Okafor sees it on the tablet: she goes quiet and makes a note. After the raid, that operator is out of the Cell (@ID[FIRSTLIGHT-MAREA-ROJA]).
- **The door:** if the plan is a forced entry, @tirada{attributes.dexterity+skills.craft|3|Sorensen: Dexterity + Craft (Difficulty 3)} *(Desperation: Entrepreneurial)* for the breaching charge. On a success, it goes down in one turn. On a failure, it goes down in two and someone hears: Crack **F4**.

### The Cut

When the first operator touches the door, or when the generator light starts creeping in under it, **cut**. Ask the players to turn their hunter sheets face down and pick up their vampires' sheets. Read the “Read Aloud” from @ID[A4-E02] that matches the target. From then on, the Storyteller runs the hunters, with their actors and whatever was decided in this interlude.

**If the coterie isn't at the target** (the Prince's penthouse, for example), the interlude doesn't cut at the door: the hunters go in. What they find is what A4-E02 says: @ID[PNJ-02|Whitmore] escapes alone, using Presence (Majesty), and reaches the Council late and humiliated. Okafor's Faith takes 2 dice off that Majesty if she went in (Desperation 5); the outcome is the same, but Okafor notices, and notices it up close. What the hunters can gain is material: papers, phones. It's a hook for @ID[FIRSTLIGHT-MAREA-ROJA], not evidence for the Council.

### Consequences

This interlude **doesn't move the Thermometer**: it's already at 9, and what happens at the door is decided by A4-E02. What's in play is what each side brings to it:

- The plan advantages **V4**, **V5**, **V6**.
- The approach cracks **F1**, **F4**, **F5**, **F6**.
- The Cell's Desperation as it stood at the start.

If the Cell comes out of the raid with two operators down or with nothing, Desperation +1 for whatever comes next.

### Storyteller Notes

- **The irony is the point.** The players are going to play the raid against the plan they just made. Don't ask them to forget it: ask them to play their vampires, who don't know it. The A4-E02 test to detect the raid is rolled as normal.
- **Don't let the plan eat the session.** Ten minutes of briefing, one roll, and out the door. If the players want to plan more, have them do it out loud while they drive.
- **Okafor on the radio** is the voice of the raid. If her player wants to say the lines from the A4-E02 dialogue guide, let that player say them: it's their scene until the cut.
