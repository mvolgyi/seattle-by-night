# The Puget Cell · Pregenerated Characters

Six FIRSTLIGHT hunters for playing the “From the Other Side” interludes (@ID[FIRSTLIGHT-INTERLUDIOS]). Each page is meant to be handed to one player: it contains nothing about the vampire plot that their hunter doesn't know. The journal is generated hidden and its pages visible: to hand one out, give the player Observer permission on the journal or show it to them.

Each hunter has a hunter-type actor with the same name in the FIRSTLIGHT/Puget Cell folder of the character compendium, and the Puget Cell (@ID[CEL-01]) as a group. The numbers on these pages come from those sheets.

*For the Storyteller:* anything not in the docs (the Creed, the Drive, the Edges, the lives of Navarro, Pak, and Sorensen, the Touchstones) is **Proposed**. The breakdown of what comes from the doc and what's ours is in each actor's private notes.

## Dana Okafor · Special Agent {#PRE-01} {jugadores}

*“Every source wants something. Until you know what it wants, it's bait.”*

Forty-five, head of FIRSTLIGHT's Puget Cell. You were born in Houston in 1981, the daughter of a Nigerian engineer and a Louisiana schoolteacher. Eleven years in the FBI's violent crimes unit in Chicago, known for closing the cases others had written off.

In 2017 you found the pattern in a string of South Side disappearances that didn't follow any pattern. You also found the basement where they ended. What was inside tore two fingers off your left hand and kept moving after you emptied your magazine into its head. Six months later, a man from Homeland Security visited you in the hospital and told you that you weren't crazy. In 2018 you joined FIRSTLIGHT. In 2024 they sent you to Seattle.

### What You Know

- **The orders:** confirm whether Seattle has “an organized structure” and, if it does, don't touch it until you can tear it out by the roots. Tight budget. A single cell.
- **ARCHER:** an anonymous source who started feeding you things recently. Claims to have access “to the local structure.” It came to you too easily. You use it anyway, because it's all you've got. You don't buy it.
- **Helen Ward:** a podcast journalist who did alone, in twelve years, what took FIRSTLIGHT two with a federal budget. You respect her. You think she's in danger.
- **“R. A.”:** a signature from the King County medical examiner's office on fifteen impossible reports. You've been preparing a visit for months.
- **ANOMALY:** Gale Sentinel's doorbell cameras flag anyone who gives off no heat as a “cold subject.” Northline has been receiving the alerts since September.

### In the Cell

- **Sasha Hale:** the only person in Seattle you listen to without reservations.
- **Cole Ransom:** a good operator. Asks questions that aren't his to ask.
- **The tactical team:** six contractors hired as a counterterrorism unit. Most of them have never seen what they hunt. Navarro has.

### How to Play

- You speak low, unhurried. You repeat the other person's last sentence before you answer.
- Black coffee all day. You never sit with your back to a door. Your left hand is almost always in your pocket.
- You don't enter combat if you can avoid it. You direct by radio from outside. If you're cornered, you shoot for the head and fall back toward a light source.

## Cole Ransom · Operator {#PRE-03} {jugadores}

*“Nobody gets left behind. Not even here.”*

Forty-nine, mustache, wrecked knees. Former SEAL. Now you're a private contractor on the Puget Cell's tactical team. You think you work for Homeland Security, in a special counterterrorism unit. That's what they told you, and the paychecks clear.

You served on the same team as Tom “Bear” Whitman, whose knees were like yours. You haven't heard from him in years.

### What You Know

- **The job:** surveillance, support, and, if needed, entry. Never alone: always in pairs, with a drone overhead and a light source behind you. If two go down, you pull back and wait for dawn.
- **What you hunt:** they told you “subjects.” The weapons are strange for terrorists: incendiary ammunition, ultraviolet flashlights, thermal goggles. You noticed. You asked. They didn't quite answer.
- **The armory:** you run it, in the port warehouse where the team sleeps.

### In the Cell

- **Dana Okafor:** uses you because you're good. Knows you ask questions.
- **The team:** your teammates. Absolute loyalty. It's the one thing that's not up for debate.

### How to Play

- You look people in the eyes and at their hands, in that order. You can tell when someone moves differently.
- You don't say much, and you say it in radio phrases.

## Lena Sorensen · Breacher {#PRE-06} {jugadores}

*“One and a half. Count with me.”*

### What You Know

- **The job:** when the team goes in, it goes in where you said. Never alone. Always with light behind you.

### How to Play
