# Prologue · Rain over Ballard

## Preludes

Before @ID[A0-E01], each PC lives through a brief moment on the same early morning as the break-in. These are one- or two-minute scenes, with no tests, so that every player starts the session with something in hand. Read them separately or together.

:::recuadro Storyteller Notes · Preludes
**Celeste (@ID[PJ-02|PJ-02]).** At 3:30 a.m., @ID[MEN-09|Mr. Pell] messages her asking her to authorize “an urgent payment to a security vendor” from one of Halloway Meridian's companies. It's routine. She signs it. *(It's the payment to the @ID[MEN-01|MEN-01] crew. If she checks the transfer later, it's a clue: @pista{P-14}, early.)*

**Ezra (@ID[PJ-03|PJ-03]).** On his monitors down in the Underground, at 2:52 a.m., a block of doorbell cameras in Ballard goes dark. All Gale Sentinel, all at once, for eleven minutes. He's never seen a failure that clean. *(@pista{P-01}.)*

**Nadia (@ID[PJ-04|PJ-04]).** At 3:20 a.m. she gets a voicemail from @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] on her regular phone. Four seconds of breathing, then it cuts off. *(Helen started to call her and hung up. Nadia can go to the apartment on her own.)*

**Hal (@ID[PJ-05|PJ-05]).** He's watching David's hangar on @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery]'s orders, as he does every few weeks. At 3:21 a.m. he sees David and @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] tear out in the truck. *(Hal can follow them or report in.)*

**Ruth (@ID[PJ-06|PJ-06]).** She's on leave, at home, unable to sleep. At 3:40 a.m. her ex-husband, the retired detective, calls her: “Word is there's a weird call in Ballard nobody wants to take. You know anything?” *(She doesn't know anything yet. But she will.)*

## A0-E01 · The Call from SAFE {#A0-E01}

### Storyteller Context

It's 3:19 a.m. @ID[PJ-01|David]'s flip phone rings for the first time in four years. The conversation is the one told in @ID[CAP-00|CAP-00]. Fifteen minutes later, David and @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] reach @ID[LUG-05|Helen's apartment]. David doesn't go alone: from the truck, he calls in the coterie. He calls whoever's at hand and tells them where, not why.

- **Nadia** arrives on her own if she followed up on her prelude: she knows the apartment.
- **Hal** arrives behind the truck, or because David calls him.
- **Ezra and Ruth** already have Ballard on their minds from their preludes. If David calls them, they come.
- **Celeste** arrives only if the table has agreed on how she knows David (see “Character Advice” in @ID[AVE-01]). If not, she joins in @ID[A1-E01] or in @ID[A1-E03].

Those who don't arrive tonight join up in @ID[A1-E03].

The goal of the scene is simple: secure @ID[PNJ-04|Helen], understand what happened, and get out before things get worse. What the coterie doesn't know is that the doorbell camera on 3B, across from Helen's door, is still streaming.

*With a single player: this is the way in. David picks up the PC on the way and they arrive together, with Bear. David and Bear stay with the PC for the whole adventure. Let the PC do the talking: David opens the door and hangs back.*

:::leer
Market Street gleams in the rain. The hardware store is closed, its shutter rolled down, and above it, on the third floor, a single window is lit. The street door is open. The stairwell smells of damp, of wet wool, and of something else, metallic and sweet, like coins squeezed in a fist. In the third-floor hallway every door has a doorbell camera, each with its little blue light. Helen's door is closed. From the other side, someone is counting under their breath: “...one hundred forty-one, one hundred forty-two...”
:::

### Tests

- @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.awareness|3|Wits + Awareness (Difficulty 3)}: the doorbell camera on 3B, the only one in the hallway pointed at Helen's door, has its blue light steady and a second, tiny green light blinking. It's streaming live.
- @tirada{attributes.resolve+skills.awareness|2|Resolve + Awareness (Difficulty 2)}, or automatic with Auspex 1: the coin smell in the hallway is Vitae, but watered down, with no Beast behind it. It belongs to a ghoul (@pista{P-02}).
- @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|3|Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 3)}: the cameras on her door and on 3B are the same Gale Sentinel model. Helen's has a disconnection log from 2:52 to 3:03 a.m. (@pista{P-01}).

### Dialogue Guide: Helen Ward

*Helen is exhausted and scared, but she's still a journalist. She talks fast, smokes, and asks questions the moment she can. With David she's more direct than with anyone.*

- **“Are you okay?”**—“I'm in one piece. Okay's something else.”
- **“What did they take?”**—“Everything that matters. The drive, the copy in the closet. And somebody wiped the cloud at three-oh-one. This isn't some random burglar: they didn't even take the TV.”
- **“Who knew the file existed?”**—“Nadia, my producer. My source at the M.E.'s office. You, I guess.” She looks at him. “Did you know?”
- **“Did you see their faces?”**—“Caps, gloves. One of them stopped in the hallway and sniffed the stairwell. Like a dog. He smelled like wet wool and coins.”
- **(Asked by David, or by a PC who has already seen @ID[DOC-05|DOC-05]) “Why didn't you change the lock?”**—She looks at David. She's quiet for a moment. “Because you never told me why. And I don't do things without knowing why.”
- **“Who are they?”** (to David, about the PCs who came with him)—“Don't take me for an idiot. I call one number and three of you show up. None of you blink enough.”
