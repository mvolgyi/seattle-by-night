# Prologue · Rain over Ballard

## Preludes

Before @ID[A0-E01], every character other than @ID[PJ-01|David] lives through a brief moment in the small hours of the same night as the break-in. These are one- or two-minute scenes, with no tests, so each player starts the session with something in hand. Read them separately or together.

:::recuadro Storyteller Notes · Preludes
**Celeste (@ID[PJ-02|PJ-02]).** At 3:30 a.m., @ID[MEN-09|Mr. Pell] texts her asking her to authorize “an urgent payment to a security vendor” from one of Halloway Meridian's companies. It's routine. She signs off on it. *(It's the payment to the @ID[MEN-01|MEN-01] team. If she reviews the transfer later, it's a clue: an early @pista{P-14}.)*

**Ezra (@ID[PJ-03|PJ-03]).** On his monitors down in the underground, at 2:52 a.m., a block of doorbell cameras in Ballard goes dark. All Gale Sentinel, all at once, for eleven minutes. He's never seen a failure that clean. *(@pista{P-01}.)*

**Nadia (@ID[PJ-04|PJ-04]).** At 3:20 a.m. she gets a voicemail from @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] on her regular phone. All she hears is four seconds of breathing, and then it cuts off. *(Helen started to call her and hung up. Nadia can go to the apartment on her own.)*

**Hal (@ID[PJ-05|PJ-05]).** He's watching David's hangar on @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery]'s orders, as he does every few weeks. At 3:21 a.m. he sees David and @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] tear out in the truck. *(Hal can follow them or report in.)*

**Ruth (@ID[PJ-06|PJ-06]).** She's on leave, at home, unable to sleep. At 3:40 a.m. her ex-husband, the retired detective, calls her: “They're telling me there's a weird call in Ballard nobody wants to take. You know anything?” *(She doesn't know anything yet. But she will.)*
:::

## A0-E01 · The Call from SAFE {#A0-E01}

### Storyteller Context

It's 3:19 a.m. @ID[PJ-01|David]'s flip phone rings for the first time in four years. The conversation is the one told in @ID[CAP-00|CAP-00]. Fifteen minutes later, David and @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] arrive at @ID[LUG-05|Helen's apartment]. Nadia and Hal can arrive too if they followed their preludes. The rest of the coterie isn't there yet: they come together in @ID[A1-E03].

The goal of the scene is simple: secure @ID[PNJ-04|Helen], figure out what happened, and get out before things get worse. What the coterie doesn't know is that the doorbell camera on 3B, across from Helen's door, is still streaming.

*If you're playing with a single player, this scene is the way in, and David arrives alone with Bear.*

:::leer
Market Street gleams in the rain. The hardware store is closed, its shutter pulled down, and above it, on the third floor, a single window is lit. The street door is open. The stairwell smells of damp, of wet wool, and of something else, metallic and sweet, like coins squeezed in a fist. In the third-floor hallway every door has a camera doorbell, each with its little blue light. Helen's door is shut. On the other side, someone is counting under their breath: “...one hundred forty-one, one hundred forty-two...”
:::

### Tests

- @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.awareness|3|Wits + Awareness (Difficulty 3)}: the doorbell camera on 3B, the only one in the hallway pointed at Helen's door, has a steady blue light and a second, tiny green light, blinking. It's streaming live.
- @tirada{attributes.resolve+skills.awareness|2|Resolve + Awareness (Difficulty 2)}, or automatic with Auspex 1: the coin smell in the hallway is Vitae, but watered-down and with no Beast behind it. It belongs to a ghoul (@pista{P-02}).
- @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|3|Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 3)}: the camera on her door and the one on 3B are the same Gale Sentinel model. Helen's has a disconnection log from 2:52 to 3:03 (@pista{P-01}).

### Dialogue Guide: Helen Ward

*Helen is exhausted and scared, but she's still a journalist. She talks fast, smokes, and asks questions whenever she can. She's more direct with David than with anyone.*

- **“Are you okay?”** — “I'm in one piece. Okay is something else.”
- **“What did they take?”** — “Everything that matters. The drive, the copy in the closet. And somebody wiped the cloud at 3:01. This isn't some random burglar: they didn't even take the TV.”
- **“Who knew the file existed?”** — “Nadia, my producer. My source at the M.E.'s office. You, I guess.” She looks at him. “Did you know?”
- **“Did you see their faces?”** — “Caps, gloves. One of them stopped in the hallway and sniffed the stairs. Like a dog. He smelled like wet wool and coins.”
- **(To David) “Why didn't you change the lock?”** — She goes quiet for a moment. “Because you never told me why. And I don't do things without knowing why.”
- **“Who are they?”** (if Nadia or Hal are present) — “Don't take me for an idiot. I called one number and three people showed up. None of you blinks enough.”
