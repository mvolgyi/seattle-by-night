# Endings of the Climax

The ending depends on two things: how the Council ended (@ID[A4-E01]) and what happened to the File (@ID[A4-E03]). The Storyteller cross-references the two on this table.

|  | The coterie keeps the File | The coterie destroys the File | The File reaches Duval | The File reaches FIRSTLIGHT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Halloway loses or is exposed** | @ID[FIN-A] | @ID[FIN-A] + @ID[FIN-C] | @ID[FIN-A] (Duval returns it) | @ID[FIN-D] |
| **Halloway wins** | Partial @ID[FIN-B] (Halloway doesn't have his proof for the Justicar: his regency is weak) | @ID[FIN-C] | @ID[FIN-B] | @ID[FIN-D] |
| **The coterie made a deal with Halloway** | @ID[FIN-B] |  |  |  |

## FIN-A · The Truth Before the Throne {#FIN-A}

@ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] falls. Prince @ID[PNJ-02|Whitmore] declares him a traitor before the Primogen and gives him a choice between exile and a Blood Hunt. If the coterie captured him, the Sheriff destroys him that same night at the farm (@ID[LUG-02]), with no witnesses. If he fled, he becomes an enemy outside the city with resources, patience, and a grudge.

**Consequences:**

- Each member of the coterie gains Status (Camarilla) +1; +2 for whoever presented the case. @ID[PJ-01|David] is cleared before the court, and the court now knows who he works with.
- @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] owes the coterie a major favor. He crosses the first name off his list.
- @termometro{-2|Halloway falls as ARCHER (FIN-A)} (FIRSTLIGHT loses its source).
- @ID[PNJ-15|Voight] offers the coterie a standing alliance against the Inquisition: Contacts 1 or Mawla (Voight) 1 for whoever accepts.
- Halloway Meridian Capital is left headless. @ID[PJ-02|Celeste], if she survived and sided with the coterie, can take a piece of it: Resources +2. Her Blood Bond to Halloway breaks.
- Whitmore stays on the throne, but everyone knows someone else saved him. His Status drops and the city starts talking about succession.
- **If the File was also destroyed** (FIN-A + FIN-C): the consequences of @ID[FIN-C] for Helen apply as well.

## FIN-B · The Hound Changes Masters {#FIN-B}

@ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] becomes Regent of Seattle, with the recognition of Justicar @ID[PNJ-08|Panhard] if the File reached @ID[PNJ-07|Duval]. @ID[PNJ-02|Whitmore] abdicates “for health reasons,” like @ID[PNJ-22|Grange] in 1962, and vanishes from the city. If the coterie made a deal with Halloway, @ID[PJ-01|David] is named Sheriff and the coterie receives the domain of Harbor Island.

**Consequences:**

- @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] becomes a declared enemy. That same night he starts working through his list. The chronicle becomes a cold war between the new court and the old Sheriff and his Wolves (@ID[COT-01]).
- If the coterie made the deal: each PC who accepted gains Status +2, but takes 2 Stains for selling out the ones who protected them, and makes the Remorse test at the end of the session. David accepts the post and stops being the same man: play him as someone who changed masters because his own people asked him to. @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] asks him for a talk that night, at the hangar (see Coda 3 of the Epilogue).
- FIRSTLIGHT still has ARCHER inside the court, now as Prince. The Inquisition and the Seattle Camarilla are, without knowing it, on the same side. The Thermometer doesn't change, but it doesn't rise again while Halloway reigns.
- @ID[PJ-02|Celeste], if she was handed over as a traitor and survived, is placed in the custody of the new Sheriff... who is David, and with him the coterie.
- @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] stays alive, but the new Prince knows who she is. The chronicle has a permanent problem.

## FIN-C · Cleansing Fire {#FIN-C}

The File disappears: dumped in the Sound, burned, or wiped. The coterie also takes care of the thumb drives, @ID[PNJ-13|Thornquist]'s duplicates, and the cold subject database. No one can use it anymore: not @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway], not the Inquisition, not the Justicar.

**Consequences:**

- @termometro{-3|The File is destroyed (FIN-C)}. The Seattle Masquerade is safer than ever.
- Twelve years of @ID[PNJ-04|Helen]'s work are gone. The Lake City mothers, Danny Ruiz, all the cases nobody else looked at. If they tell her, Helen has to decide what to do with what's left: her memory. Remorse test for @ID[PJ-04|Nadia], if she's in play. Helen is @ID[PJ-01|David]'s Touchstone, and the coterie destroyed what she made: David is shaken, and his relationship with Helen is damaged. Whether it can be repaired depends on what the coterie does next.
- If Halloway won the Council, his regency lacks the proof the Justicar asked for: @ID[PNJ-08|Panhard] doesn't recognize it. Seattle is left in political limbo, with two Kindred who both claim to be the authority. Chronicle hook.
- The coterie gains +1 Humanity if one of them offers to tell Helen in person, without Disciplines.

## FIN-D · Red Tide {#FIN-D}

The File, or enough of it, ends up in the hands of @ID[PNJ-18|Dana Okafor]. FIRSTLIGHT now has twelve years of evidence, hunting maps, photos, and names. The Puget Cell asks national headquarters for reinforcements. Within two weeks there are three cells in the city.

**Consequences:**

- Thermometer 10. Purge. In the first week at least one Camarilla haven falls (the Storyteller chooses; if the coterie doesn't move, theirs).
- Every PC who appears in the File loses Mask −1 and Haven −1 until they relocate.
- @ID[PNJ-02|Whitmore], if he's still Prince, closes Elysium. The court scatters. @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway], if he survived, has nothing left to gain: he vanishes from the city.
- @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] gets a visit from Okafor. They offer her a job. What she decides is the first hook of the new chronicle.
