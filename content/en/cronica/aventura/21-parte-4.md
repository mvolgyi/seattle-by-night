## A4-E01 · Primogen Council {#A4-E01}

### How the Scene Works

The Council has three stages: **the motion** (Halloway presents his case), **the debate** (the coterie and the others respond), and **the vote**.

#### 1 · The Motion

Halloway speaks for five minutes without interruption. His case:

- The Second Inquisition has a source inside the court (true: it's him).
- That source has leaked three havens, all from the Prince's and the Sheriff's circles (true).
- There's a killer on the docks who fights like the Hound's sire, and the Hound has met with him in secret (half true). He shows the drone footage.
- The Prince received a warning from Regent Voight and didn't read it for two nights (true).
- The Inquisition's source has been identified: the deliveries were paid for by Cascade Holdings. And then he opens the folder marked “C. Arden.”

**Celeste's sacrifice.** Halloway accuses his own childe of being ARCHER, with her signature on the accounts as proof. He does it with rehearsed sorrow: “This hurts me more than anyone.” He asks the Primogen to hand her over to the Sheriff. That way he comes out clean and shows he's capable of sacrificing his own “for the Masquerade.”

*If Celeste is a PC, give the player a moment before moving on. See Notes.*

#### 2 · The Debate

The coterie responds. Whoever speaks for it makes the **case roll**:

**@tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.politics|4|Manipulation + Politics (Difficulty 4)} or @tirada{attributes.charisma+skills.politics|4|Charisma + Politics (Difficulty 4)}**, +1 die for each piece of evidence on the checklist. Halloway answers with his own pool (@tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.politics|0|Halloway: Manipulation + Politics (10 dice)}). It's a contested test. The margin decides:

| Coterie's margin | Result |
|---|---|
| Halloway wins | The room believes him. Celeste is handed over. The votes lean toward the regency (see the vote, no changes). |
| Tie or 1–2 successes | Doubt. Nobody fully believes anyone. The vote decides. |
| 3–4 successes | Halloway is exposed. The vote almost certainly turns against him. |
| 5 or more | **Total exposure.** The Prince orders the Sheriff to arrest Halloway on the spot. Halloway flees: see Notes and @ID[A4-E03]. |

#### 3 · The Vote

Halloway needs 3 votes in favor out of 5. Each Primogen has a default vote and a condition that changes it:

| Primogen | Default vote | Changes to “no” if... |
|---|---|---|
| Halloway | Yes | Never. |
| Voight | Yes. She believes Whitmore failed. | The coterie proves to her that ARCHER is Halloway (@ID[P-10], the C.H. email, or Gale's or Dawson's testimony). |
| Thornquist | Yes. Halloway paid him. | The coterie proves to him that Krell, Halloway's man, destroyed Pike, his errand boy (@ID[P-03] plus @ID[P-12] or Krell's testimony). Or offers him something better: a bigger favor from the whole coterie. |
| Marchetti | Abstains. | She sees @ID[DOC-08], with Whitmore's note (“C.H. — watch”), or the coterie cites her record from Night 3 (@ID[DOC-03]). Then she votes “no”: the record is what she respects most. |
| Fry | Unpredictable: roll 1 die; even, yes; odd, no. | Someone speaks a hard truth out loud during the debate. For example, David acknowledging Krell as his brother, Hal admitting to his reports, or Celeste admitting she signed without looking. Then Fry votes with whoever spoke that truth. |

**By default, if the coterie does nothing, Halloway wins 3 to 0**, with two abstentions or a vote against from Fry.

### Dialogue Guide: Charles Halloway

- **“You're ARCHER.”** — “A serious accusation, without evidence, in my own house.” He barely smiles. “Go on, please.”
- **(Faced with P-10 or the C.H. email)** — Silence. Then, very calm: “Documents illegally obtained from a mortal company. Since when does the Camarilla pass judgment with the Inquisition's methods?”
- **(Faced with Krell's testimony)** — “A mercenary with no sect, no acknowledged sire, who killed three of our own. That's your witness?” He looks at David. “Or is he your brother?” *(If @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] testifies, Halloway loses his composure for the first time: one of his hands moves.)*
- **(To Celeste)** — “I'm sorry, my dear. The Masquerade comes before both of us.”
- **(If he loses)** — He smiles. This time he means it. “Your Highness, it's been a pleasure. As always.” And he heads for the elevator.

### Dialogue Guide: Marcus Whitmore

- **(If the coterie defends him well)** — He rises slowly. His voice fills the room without effort (Presence 5). “Charles. Sixty-four years ago I watched your sire make the same mistake. He thought a good story was the same thing as the truth.” A pause. “Sheriff.”
- **(If the coterie fails)** — He looks at the table for a long time. “Very well. The Primogen have spoken.” To David: “Thank you, Mr. Smith. I believe you arrived late. Again.”

- **(If Halloway wins and Montgomery was with the coterie)** — He takes the list out of his pocket. Looks at it. Puts it away. “Not tonight.” To David: “But tonight ends sometime.”
