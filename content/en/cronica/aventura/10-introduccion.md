# Chapter 7 · The Ward File

*An adventure for a coterie of 4 or 5 characters, with @ID[PJ-01|David Smith] and @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] as NPC allies. Estimated length: 6–8 sessions. ID: AVE-01.*

## Introduction

A Seattle journalist spent ten years documenting deaths that didn't add up. Without knowing it, she built the most complete map in existence of the city's Kindred: where they hunt, where they slip up, who doesn't age. In the early hours of Thursday, November 12, 2026, someone stole it from her.

From that night on, the characters have to recover the File before it falls into the hands of the Second Inquisition or of someone at court who wants to use it as a weapon. Along the way they'll discover that someone on the docks is destroying Kindred with the technique of David's sire, that the Ventrue Primogen has spent sixty years preparing a coup, and that the whole city is watching them through 412,000 doorbells.

### The Truth, in One Paragraph

@ID[PNJ-03|Charles Halloway] wants the crown. His ghoul @ID[PNJ-19|Preston Gale] located @ID[PNJ-04|Helen Ward]'s archive using data from his company's cameras, and a team of mortals (@ID[MEN-01|MEN-01]) stole it. @ID[PNJ-12|August Krell], the discarded childe of @ID[PNJ-06|Marcus Vance], destroys whoever buys copies using Vance's doctrine, so that David chases a ghost and @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] stops trusting him. Meanwhile, Halloway feeds fragments of the archive to FIRSTLIGHT as the source ARCHER. When the Inquisition strikes the Prince's and the Sheriff's assets, Halloway will petition the Primogen for the regency.

## Character Advice

The adventure is written for “The Archived” (@ID[COT-00|COT-00]): 4 or 5 characters who appear in the File. The concepts @ID[PJ-02|PJ-02] through @ID[PJ-06|PJ-06] fit directly; with 4 players, one goes unused. David isn't part of the coterie: he's their ally, the Sheriff's Hound, who stays with them for the whole story and lends them the hangar as a haven. The Storyteller runs him, just like Bear. If the players create their own characters, keep the following in mind:

- **Clans that work best:** Ventrue (access to @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway]'s circle), Nosferatu (information and surveillance), Toreador (the court and @ID[PNJ-04|Helen]'s world), Gangrel (the Sheriff's pack), Tremere (forensics and Blood Sorcery). Malkavian and Banu Haqim also fit. Avoid more Brujah: the city already has four.
- **Useful Backgrounds:** Contacts (police, press, port, tech), Influence, Herd, Camarilla Status, and any Mawla at court. A solid Mask (2 or more) helps: in this adventure, surveillance is the main danger.
- **Key Skills:** Investigation, Technology, Insight, Stealth, Persuasion, Politics, and Brawl. At least one character with Technology 2 or more makes things much easier.
- **Motivations:** every character has something in the File. Before you start, have each player decide what their folder contains (@ID[AYU-09|AYU-09]) and one Touchstone who could be in danger in Part 3.
- **How to run @ID[PJ-01|David]:** he's the emotional center of the plot, but the PCs are the ones who live it. He knows two things the coterie doesn't: why he sent the November 9 message (@ID[DOC-05|DOC-05]) and what really happened at the Larchmont (@ID[LUG-11|LUG-11]). He doesn't volunteer them: the PCs uncover them and decide what to do with them. When he helps, he adds +1 die to whoever is rolling or does something you narrate without a roll. He never single-handedly solves what the players have to solve, and the big decisions (forgive, hand over, kill, lie) are theirs. David weighs in, pushes, or reacts.
- **How they know David:** at session zero, have each player say how their PC knows the Hound, even if only from a distance. Hal has known him for thirty years (@ID[PJ-05|PJ-05]); the others, whatever the table agrees on. This lets David summon them in @ID[A0-E01].
