# Chapter 7 · The Ward File

*Adventure for a coterie of 4 or 5 characters, including @ID[PJ-01|David Smith]. Estimated length: 6–8 sessions. ID: AVE-01.*

## Introduction

A Seattle journalist spent ten years documenting deaths that didn't add up. Without knowing it, she built the most complete map in existence of the city's Kindred: where they hunt, where they slip, who doesn't age. In the early hours of Thursday, November 12, 2026, someone stole it.

From that night on, the characters have to recover the File before it falls into the hands of the Second Inquisition or of someone at court who wants to use it as a weapon. Along the way they'll discover that someone on the docks is destroying Kindred with the technique of David's sire, that the Ventrue Primogen has spent sixty years preparing a coup, and that the entire city is watching them through 412,000 doorbells.

### The Truth, in One Paragraph

@ID[PNJ-03|Charles Halloway] wants the crown. His ghoul @ID[PNJ-19|Preston Gale] located @ID[PNJ-04|Helen Ward]'s archive using data from his company's cameras, and a crew of mortals (@ID[MEN-01|MEN-01]) stole it. @ID[PNJ-12|August Krell], the discarded childe of @ID[PNJ-06|Marcus Vance], destroys whoever buys copies using Vance's doctrine, so that David chases a ghost and @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] stops trusting him. Meanwhile, Halloway feeds fragments of the archive to FIRSTLIGHT as the source ARCHER. When the Inquisition hits the Prince's and the Sheriff's assets, Halloway will petition the Primogen for the regency.

## Character Advice

The adventure is written for “The Archived” (@ID[COT-00|COT-00]): David and 3 or 4 new characters who appear in the File. Concepts @ID[PJ-02|PJ-02] through @ID[PJ-06|PJ-06] fit right in. If the players create their own characters, keep the following in mind:

- **Clans that work best:** Ventrue (access to @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway]'s circle), Nosferatu (information and surveillance), Toreador (the court and @ID[PNJ-04|Helen]'s world), Gangrel (the Sheriff's pack), Tremere (forensics and Blood Sorcery). Malkavian and Banu Haqim also fit. Better to avoid more Brujah: the city already has four.
- **Useful Backgrounds:** Contacts (police, press, port, tech), Influence, Herd, Camarilla Status, and any Mawla at court. A solid Mask (2 or more) helps: in this adventure, surveillance is the main danger.
- **Key Skills:** Investigation, Technology, Insight, Stealth, Persuasion, Politics, and Brawl. Having at least one character with Technology 2 or higher makes things much easier.
- **Motivations:** every character has something in the File. Before starting, have each player decide what their folder contains (@ID[AYU-09|AYU-09]) and one Touchstone who could be in danger in Part 3.
- **For @ID[PJ-01|David]'s player:** before session 1, agree in private on two things his character knows and the others don't: the November 9 message (@ID[DOC-05|DOC-05]) and what really happened at the Larchmont (@ID[LUG-11|LUG-11]).
