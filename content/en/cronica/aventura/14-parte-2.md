# Part 2 · The Ghost of Marseille

Nights 3 to 5. The coterie enters court politics, discovers that the dockside killer isn't exactly who he seems, and starts assembling the pieces that point to @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway]: the 1986 journals, the Cascade Holdings accounts, and a thief who kept something. The scenes in this part can be played in any order, except @ID[A2-E01], which has a fixed date.

## A2-E01 · Elysium Night {#A2-E01}

### Storyteller Context

Night 3, Saturday, November 14. A regular gathering of Elysium at the @ID[LUG-03|Glass Gallery]. This is the central social scene of Part 2. In attendance: @ID[PNJ-02|Prince Whitmore], the entire Primogen (@ID[PNJ-03|Halloway], @ID[PNJ-14|Marchetti], @ID[PNJ-15|Voight], @ID[PNJ-13|Thornquist], and @ID[PNJ-16|Fry]), and some twenty lesser Kindred. @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] arrives late and leaves early.

Halloway has three goals tonight: sow doubt about the Sheriff, rekindle the rumor that @ID[PJ-01|David] destroyed his sire, and take the coterie's measure. He makes one mistake: he mentions a detail about @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] that appears only in the journals (P-07).

The scene plays as a round of conversations. The Storyteller decides the order based on who approaches whom. At the end, the Prince takes the floor.

*For a single player: David arrives with @ID[PNJ-05|Bear], who stays in the vestibule with the phones. The conversations are the same.*

:::leer
At the entrance, a woman in gray holds out a velvet tray: “Your phones, please.” Inside, hundreds of pieces of blown glass hang from the ceiling like jellyfish frozen in place, red, amber, and black, and the light passing through them shifts across the floor whenever someone walks by. A string quartet nobody can see is playing. Everyone has a glass in hand, and nobody drinks from it. At the far end, by the terrace, a silver-haired man laughs at something, and the whole room seems to turn slightly toward him.
:::

### Dialogue Guide: Charles Halloway

*Courteous, calm, never interrupts. His hands don't move. He calls David “Mr. Smith,” never “Hound.”*

- **(He approaches David) “What do you want?”** — “To offer my condolences, Mr. Smith. I'm told things have turned up on the docks that remind you of someone. It must be difficult.”
- **“What do you mean?”** — “Your sire. A remarkable man. ‘Locate, assess, execute.’ And the cold water in the mornings, wasn't it? A severe method.” *(The cold water appears only in the journals: @pista{P-07}.)*
- **“How do you know about the cold water?”** — The briefest pause. “We Ventrue read a great deal, Mr. Smith. The Archons' reports from the seventies were very thorough.” *(A lie. @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.insight|4|Wits + Insight (Difficulty 4)} to notice.)*
- **“What do you think of the Sheriff?”** — “That he's done excellent work for fifty years. And that fifty years is a long time for anyone.”
- **(To @ID[PJ-02|Celeste], if present) “What do you need from me?”** — Quietly, in front of the others: “Go over the Cascade accounts this week. There's a payment I don't remember authorizing.” *(It's a trap: he wants Celeste to touch the payment to the thieves and leave her signature a second time.)*
- **“Do you have the File?”** — He smiles for the first time. “I have many things. But I don't collect podcasts.”

### Dialogue Guide: Dr. Anneliese Voight

*Precise, dry, speaks in percentages. Prefers the terrace because from there she can see who comes in.*

- **“Are you worried?”** — “I have been for fourteen months. Now I'm worried with data.”

- **“Who?”** — “If I knew, I wouldn't be out on the terrace. I'd be talking to the Sheriff.”

- **(To @ID[PJ-06|Ruth], if present) “What do I do?”** — “Nothing with your name on it. For two weeks, nothing.”

### Dialogue Guide: Rosa Iturbe

*Direct, quick, Biscayan accent. Swears in Basque when she's angry. Never looks away.*

- **“We're not here to accuse anyone.”** — “That's what everyone says. Then a Blood Hunt shows up with our name on it.”
- **“The killer fights like David's sire.”** — She looks at David. “And that surprises you? You dogs all look alike.”

## A2-E04 · The Sheriff That Was {#A2-E04}

### Storyteller Context

Any night of Part 2. The coterie drives to Tacoma, about forty minutes south, to talk with @ID[PNJ-17|Liam O'Connor] at his gym, the Shamrock. O'Connor knows two things nobody has asked him about: that @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] brought down the Sheriff in 1978 with fabricated photos, and that in 1986 Halloway bought @ID[PNJ-06|Vance]'s journals.

O'Connor gives nothing away, but he doesn't want payment either. He wants to see whether the Hound “is a dog or a man.” He offers @ID[PJ-01|David] three rounds in the ring. He doesn't care who wins. He cares how David fights.

:::leer

In the ring, a huge man with red hair and cauliflower ears holds the mitts for a girl who hits with fury.
