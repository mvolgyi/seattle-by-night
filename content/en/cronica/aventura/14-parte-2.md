# Part 2 · The Ghost of Marseille

Nights 3 to 5. The coterie enters the politics of the court, discovers that the dockside killer isn't exactly who he seems, and begins gathering the pieces that point to @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway]: the 1986 journals, the Cascade Holdings accounts, and a thief who kept something. The scenes in this part can be played in any order, except @ID[A2-E01], which has a fixed date.

## A2-E01 · Elysium Night {#A2-E01}

### Storyteller Context

Night 3, Saturday, November 14. A regular gathering of Elysium at @ID[LUG-03|the Glass Gallery]. It's the central social scene of Part 2. In attendance: @ID[PNJ-02|Prince Whitmore], the full Primogen (@ID[PNJ-03|Halloway], @ID[PNJ-14|Marchetti], @ID[PNJ-15|Voight], @ID[PNJ-13|Thornquist], and @ID[PNJ-16|Fry]), and twenty or so lesser Kindred. @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] arrives late and leaves early.

Halloway has three goals tonight: sow doubt about the Sheriff, rekindle the rumor that @ID[PJ-01|David] destroyed his sire, and take the measure of the coterie. He makes one mistake: he mentions a detail about @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] that only appears in the journals (P-07).

The scene works as a round of conversations. The Storyteller decides the order based on who approaches whom. At the end, the Prince takes the floor.

:::leer
At the entrance, a woman in gray holds out a velvet tray: “Your phones, please.” Inside, hundreds of pieces of blown glass hang from the ceiling like frozen jellyfish, red, amber, and black, and the light passing through them shifts across the floor whenever someone walks by. A string quartet plays, and no one can see it. Everyone has a glass in hand, and no one drinks from it. At the far end, by the terrace, a silver-haired man laughs at something, and the whole room seems to turn slightly toward him.
:::

### Dialogue Guide: Charles Halloway

*Courteous, calm, never interrupts. His hands don't move. He calls David “Mr. Smith,” never “Hound.” He speaks to David, but he's measuring the coterie: he wants to see who jumps.*

- **(He approaches David and the coterie) “What do you want?”**—“To offer my condolences, Mr. Smith. I'm told things have turned up on the docks that remind you of someone. It must be difficult.”
- **“What do you mean?”**—“Your sire. A remarkable man. ‘Locate, evaluate, execute.’ And the cold water in the mornings, yes? A severe method.” *(The cold water only appears in the journals: @pista{P-07}.)*
- **“How do you know about the cold water?”**—The briefest pause. “We Ventrue read a great deal, Mr. Smith. The Archons' reports from the seventies were very detailed.” *(A lie. @tirada{attributes.wits+skills.insight|4|Wits + Insight (Difficulty 4)} to notice it.)*
- **“What do you think of the Sheriff?”**—“That he's done excellent work for fifty years. And that fifty years is a long time for anyone.”
- **(To @ID[PJ-02|Celeste], if present) “What do you need from me?”**—Quietly, in front of the others: “Go over the Cascade accounts this week. There's a payment I don't remember authorizing.” *(It's a trap: he wants Celeste to touch the payment to the thieves and leave her signature a second time.)*
- **“Do you have the File?”**—He smiles for the first time. “I have many things. But I don't collect podcasts.”

## A2-E04 · The Sheriff That Was {#A2-E04}

### Storyteller Context

Any night of Part 2. The coterie travels to Tacoma, about forty minutes south, to talk to @ID[PNJ-17|Liam O'Connor] at his gym, the Shamrock. O'Connor knows two things no one has asked him about: that @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] brought the Sheriff down in 1978 with fabricated photos, and that in 1986 Halloway bought @ID[PNJ-06|Vance]'s journals.

O'Connor gives nothing away, but he doesn't want payment either. He wants to see whether the Hound “is a dog or a man.” He offers @ID[PJ-01|David] three rounds in the ring. He doesn't care who wins. He cares how he fights. Who steps in, and who works the corner, is up to the coterie.

:::leer

### Dialogue Guide: Liam O'Connor

*Talks loud, laughs loud, Cork accent. When he's being serious, he sits on the edge of the ring and lowers his voice. If David didn't come, whatever is meant for him he tells the coterie: “Tell the Hound that…”*

- **“We want to know about Vance.”**—“Everybody wants to know about Vance. Nobody wants to know about O'Connor.” He laughs. “Get in the ring, Hound. Three rounds. Then we talk.”
- **(After the fight) “What do you know?”**—“That in '86 an old friend from the port saw a shipment come in from Lyon. Crates of books, addressed to one of Charles Halloway's companies.
