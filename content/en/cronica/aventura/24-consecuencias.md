## Experience

At the end of each session, each player receives 1 to 3 experience points, per the usual rules. When the adventure ends, add:

- **+2** to everyone for surviving *The Ward File*.
- **+1** to anyone who resolved a scene centered on their character (@ID[PJ-02|Celeste] at the Council, @ID[PJ-05|Hal] in @ID[A3-E04], @ID[PJ-03|Ezra] with the firmware, @ID[PJ-04|Nadia] facing Helen, @ID[PJ-06|Ruth] with the “R. A.” anomaly).
- **+1** to each PC who was at the silos (@ID[A3-E03]) if the conversation with Krell ended without destroying him.
- **+1** to the whole coterie if the Thermometer ended at 4 or lower.
