# Revision to Part 1 · The Chain of Copies

*Chapter 7 · The Ward File. Installment 2: revision of the chain of copies and Part 2.*

*This section replaces what installment 1 and Thornquist's profile (@ID[PNJ-13|PNJ-13]) say about the origin of the copies. Where they contradict, this text wins.*

## What Really Happened

@ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] wanted the whole File, and all to himself. But the crew @ID[PNJ-19|Gale] hired (@ID[MEN-01|MEN-01]) wasn't as loyal as the check. One of the thieves, **@ID[MEN-01|Lonnie Barras]**, knew that a drive someone pays that much for is worth more in pieces. During the car ride, while his partner was at the wheel, he plugged the drive into a laptop and copied three blocks of folders onto three thumb drives. Then he handed the original drive to @ID[MEN-04|Dawson], Gale's ghoul head of security (MEN-04), as if nothing had happened.

The next night, Lonnie sold two of the thumb drives to **@ID[MEN-13|Sal Medeiros]** (MEN-13), a SoDo fence who buys and sells “strange things” to clients who only come out at night. He kept the third as insurance. Sal, in turn, offered the two drives to @ID[PNJ-13|Thornquist], who bought them, made a copy of each for his own vault (it's his habit: he duplicates everything he sells), and had @ID[PNJ-20|Jun Takeda] deliver them to two buyers.

On Night 2, Gale found in the drive's logs that someone had opened the folders at 3:40 a.m., in the middle of the ride, and told Halloway. Halloway gave @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] a list: recover or destroy every copy, and every Kindred who has touched them. So the hunt begins.
