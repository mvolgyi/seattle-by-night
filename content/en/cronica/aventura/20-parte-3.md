# Part 3 · The Two Hands

Nights 6 and 7. The plot closes in on the coterie. @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] stops hiding and goes after someone they care about. In the @ID[LUG-07|Gale Sentinel] servers, the hand of @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] shows itself. The two sons of @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] come face to face. And the Sheriff, who has been wavering the whole adventure, gets the proof he's been waiting for, even if it's fake.

By the end of Part 3, the coterie should know that Halloway is behind everything. What they don't need to have yet is a way to prove it before the Primogen. That gets decided in Part 4.

### What Changes in This Part

- **Krell disobeys.** Halloway orders him to eliminate whoever follows the trail. Krell decides otherwise: he wants @ID[PJ-01|David] face to face, and for that he needs a hostage, not a corpse. It's the first decision of his own he makes in the whole adventure.
- **The original moves.** On @reloj{6}, @ID[PNJ-19|Gale] moves the original drive from the data center to the vault in Halloway Tower (@ID[LUG-06]). If the coterie reaches Gale Sentinel on Night 6 or earlier, the original is still there.
- **A new clue:** @ID[P-15], the folios from Vance's journal that talk about Krell, scanned by @ID[MEN-09|Mr. Pell] and stored on Gale's servers. Halloway never gave them to Krell. The coterie can find them in @ID[A3-E02] and use them in @ID[A3-E03].

## The Folios on the German {jugadores}

*Two folios from M. Vance's journal, written in Marseille in 1976, in French, with a typewritten translation by Mr. Pell alongside.*

**Folio 41 — Marseille, September 1976**

I left this morning before he woke. I left him the inverted guard and nothing more. He will hate me. That is only fair.

I am not leaving because he is weak. I am leaving because he is better than the American. Faster, colder, cleaner. And he has nothing on the other side of the sea. The American holds on because he has a daughter he never names. The German has no one. A perfect weapon with nothing to hold it back is not a weapon. It is a catastrophe that has not happened yet.

If I teach him the full guard, no one will be able to stop him. Not even I.

May God forgive me, if there is any left who listens to our kind.

**Folio 42**

If the American and the German should ever meet, let the American teach him the other guard. I could not. Perhaps he can. He knows what it is to have something to lose.

— M. V.

*[At the foot of the translation, in Mr. Pell's hand:]* *Mr. Halloway: are these folios to be delivered to subject K., as promised? — P.*

*[Below, in another hand, thin and upright:]* *No. — C.H.*
