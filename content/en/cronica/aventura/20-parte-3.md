# Part 3 · The Two Hands

Nights 6 and 7. The plot closes in on the coterie. @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] stops hiding and goes after someone they care about. In the @ID[LUG-07|Gale Sentinel] servers, the hand of @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] appears. The two sons of @ID[PNJ-06|Vance] come face to face. And the Sheriff, who has spent the whole adventure in doubt, receives the proof he's been waiting for, even if it's fake.

By the end of Part 3, the coterie should know that Halloway is behind everything. What they don't necessarily have yet is the means to prove it before the Primogen. That gets decided in Part 4.

### What Changes in This Part

- **Krell disobeys.** Halloway orders him to eliminate whoever follows the trail. Krell decides otherwise: he wants @ID[PJ-01|David] face to face, and for that he needs a hostage, not a corpse. It's the first decision of his own he makes in the entire adventure.
- **The original moves.** On @reloj{6}, @ID[PNJ-19|Gale] moves the original drive from the data center to the vault in Halloway Tower (@ID[LUG-06]). If the coterie reaches Gale Sentinel on Night 6 or earlier, the original is still there.
- **A new clue:** @ID[P-15], the folios from Vance's journal that talk about Krell, scanned by @ID[MEN-09|Mr. Pell] and stored on Gale's servers. Halloway never gave them to Krell. The coterie can find them in @ID[A3-E02] and use them in @ID[A3-E03].

## The German Folios {jugadores}

*Two folios from the journal of M. Vance, written in Marseille in 1976, in French, with a typewritten translation by Mr. Pell alongside.*

**Folio 41 — Marseille, September 1976**

I left this morning before he woke. I left him the inverted guard and nothing more. He will hate me. It is only fair.

I am not leaving because he is weak. I am leaving because he is better than the American. Faster, colder, cleaner. And he has nothing on the other side of the sea. The American holds on because he has a daughter he does not name. The German has no one. A perfect weapon with nothing to hold it back is not a weapon. It is a catastrophe that has not happened yet.

If I teach him the complete guard, no one will be able to stop him. Not even me.

May God forgive me, if there is any left who listens to our kind.

**Folio 42**

If the American and the German ever meet, let the American teach him the other guard. I could not. Perhaps he can. He knows what it is to have something to lose.

— M. V.

*[At the foot of the translation, in Mr. Pell's handwriting:]* *Mr. Halloway: are these folios to be delivered to subject K., as he was promised? — P.*

*[Below, in another hand, thin and upright:]* *No. — C.H.*

@reloj{7}, at the end, or the start of @reloj{8}. @ID[PNJ-03|Halloway] hands @ID[PNJ-01|Montgomery] what he's spent a week preparing: the drone footage of @ID[PJ-01|David] with @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] at the silos, a photo of @ID[PNJ-06|Vance]'s coat on Krell's shoulders, a forged letter signed “M.V.” and addressed to David, found “in the hangar” by one of Gale's ghouls (a lie), and the Elysium rumor that the Hound destroyed his sire. Halloway doesn't accuse. He only asks: “Isn't that curious?”

Montgomery summons the coterie to the farm (@ID[LUG-02]). This time he invites them inside. That, as @ID[PJ-05|Hal] knows, is a bad sign.

The scene is a trial without judges. Montgomery wants to believe David. But he has evidence on the table and forty-eight years on the job telling him to believe no one.

*If the coterie has already told Montgomery about Krell and shown him @ID[P-15] or the C.H. email, skip the first half: Montgomery is already convinced and the scene becomes a planning session for the climax.*

This time, the farmhouse door is open. Vance wrote his journals in French. And there are traces of toner on the paper: it's a printed tracing, gone over by hand.

### Dialogue Guide: Ethan Montgomery

- **(He opens, pointing at the tablet)** — “Explain this to me, Hound.”
- **“It's a trap.”** — “Maybe. Traps are true sometimes too.” Pause. “You sat down to talk with the one who killed three of ours. I saw you talk to him the way you talk to me.”
- **“He's my blood brother.”** (if David knows and says so) — Montgomery goes still. “Vance had another?” Long pause. “That's why he fights the same. That's why you hesitated.” *(+1 die.)*
- **“Halloway's behind all of it.”** — “I know that.” He looks at them. “I've known Halloway's behind all of it for forty-eight years. What I don't know is whether this time you're with him.”
- **(About the “M.V.” letter)** — “They found it in your hangar, they say.” If someone proves the forgery: Montgomery picks up the letter, looks at it closely, smells it. “Toner.” He tears it up. *(+2 dice.)*
- **(If the central test succeeds)** — He sits down for the first time. “Good. Then we do it my way. Tomorrow's the Council. Halloway's going to ask for the regency.” He takes a folded paper from his pocket: the list of eleven names. “If tomorrow goes wrong, tonight won't matter anymore. If it goes right, this stays in my pocket.”
- **(If the central test fails)** — “I don't believe you, Hound. And that's the worst thing that's happened to me in fifty years. Go. And don't come back to the hangar.”
