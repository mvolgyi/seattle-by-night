# Epilogue · What the Water Doesn't Take

One week later. Choose one of these three codas based on the ending (@ID[AVE-01-FIN]), or read all three if the session allows. Each one plays as a short scene, five or ten minutes, with no tests.

## Coda 1 · The Grave

*For @ID[FIN-A] or @ID[FIN-C], or if @ID[PJ-01|David]'s story with @ID[REF-01|Emily] was the heart of the adventure.* Location: Emily Smith's grave (@ID[LUG-12]). Play it with the PCs who go with David.

:::leer
The cedar hillside is wet and silent. The grass around the gray granite headstone has just been cut. EMILY ROSE SMITH · 1958–1996 · “She stayed with the ones nobody wanted.” At its foot, a bouquet of fresh flowers wrapped in newspaper. There are still raindrops on them. Someone was here less than an hour ago.
:::

**Notes:** the coterie decides whether to wait with David to see who brings the flowers next time (@ID[GAN-12]) or to let it be. David doesn't ask. If @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] survived and is an ally, he may be some distance off, among the trees, not coming closer. If @ID[PNJ-11|Rosa] confessed in @ID[A2-E03], the flowers are hers. Ask the players what they do. This is the last image of David's story in this adventure.

## Coda 2 · Episode 113

*For any ending in which @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] is still alive.*

:::leer
*[THEME MUSIC. 12 seconds. Fades under the voice.]*

“This episode isn't the one I promised you. The one I promised you was about three kids from Crown Hill and a mother who says her son was afraid of needles. That episode exists. I'm going to put it out. But not this week.

This week I want to tell you about something I learned. I learned there are files you can't keep on a drive. That there are people who watch and people who look after, and sometimes they're the same people, and sometimes they're not. That the city has blue eyes that don't blink.

*[pause]*

And I learned that someone who always shows up late does, sometimes, show up.

This is ‘What the City Forgets.’ I'm Helen Ward. I'm still watching.”

*[THEME MUSIC. End.]*
:::

**Notes:** the episode's content changes with the ending. In @ID[FIN-C], Helen talks about what she lost. In @ID[FIN-D], the episode never airs: the recording cuts off when someone knocks at the door. In @ID[FIN-B], Helen says nothing at all: she airs the Crown Hill episode. @ID[PJ-01|David], listening with the coterie, turns off the radio: he knows someone asked her not to talk. If @ID[PJ-04|Nadia] is a PC, she's at the mixing board.

## Coda 3 · The Hangar

*For any ending. Essential if @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] was taken hostage, if there was a raid, or if the coterie made a deal with Halloway.* Location: the Harbor Island hangar (@ID[LUG-01]).

:::leer
The hangar smells of gun oil, salt, and reheated coffee. Bear is at his workbench, cleaning a pistol that's already clean. He doesn't look up. On the bench, next to the parts, is a notebook page folded in quarters, worn thin along the creases. Bear looks at it, then at David, then goes back to the pistol. “There's coffee,” he says. “If you want some.”
:::

**Notes:** the page is @ID[DOC-12]. Bear took it out of the safe. What happens depends on the adventure: if the coterie, with David at its side, crossed a line (@ID[FIN-B], a massacre, Dominate used on Helen), Bear gives it to David to read, in front of them. If not, he puts it away again without a word and pours him a coffee. Both options are the right ending.

## The Letter

*To close the final session, after whichever coda you choose.*

:::leer
Three weeks later, an envelope with no return address arrives at the hangar, postmarked Cluj-Napoca, Romania. Heavy paper, yellowed. Inside, a single handwritten line, in a hand David knows better than his own: *“There are two. You already know. Finish the lesson.”* And beneath it, two initials: *M. V.*
:::

End of *The Ward File*. The chronicle continues in @ID[GAN-10].
