# Epilogue · What the Water Doesn't Take

One week later. Choose one of these three codas based on the ending (@ID[AVE-01-FIN]), or read all three if the session allows. Each one plays as a short scene, five or ten minutes long, with no rolls.

## Coda 1 · The Grave

*For @ID[FIN-A] or @ID[FIN-C], or if the story of @ID[PJ-01|David] and @ID[REF-01|Emily] was at the heart of the adventure.* Location: Emily Smith's grave (@ID[LUG-12]).

:::leer
The cedar slope is wet and silent. The grass around the gray granite headstone is freshly cut. EMILY ROSE SMITH · 1958–1996 · “She stayed with the ones nobody wanted.” At its foot, a bunch of fresh flowers wrapped in newspaper. Raindrops still cling to them. Someone was here less than an hour ago.
:::

**Notes:** David can wait to see who brings the flowers next time (@ID[GAN-12]) or let it go. If @ID[PNJ-12|Krell] survived and is an ally, he may be some distance off, among the trees, not coming any closer. If @ID[PNJ-11|Rosa] confessed in @ID[A2-E03], the flowers are hers. Ask David's player what he does. It's the last image of his story in this adventure.

## Coda 2 · Episode 113

*For any ending in which @ID[PNJ-04|Helen] is still alive.*

:::leer
*[THEME. 12 seconds. Fades under the voice.]*

“This isn't the episode I promised you. The one I promised you was about three kids from Crown Hill and a mother who says her son was afraid of needles. That episode exists. I'm going to put it out. But not this week.

This week I want to tell you about something I learned. I learned there are files you can't keep on a drive. That there are people who watch and people who look after you, and that sometimes they're the same people, and sometimes they're not. That the city has blue eyes that don't blink.

*[pause]*

And I learned that someone who's always late, sometimes, shows up.

This is ‘What the City Forgets.’ I'm Helen Ward. I'm still looking.”

*[THEME. End.]*
:::

**Notes:** the content of the episode changes depending on the ending. In @ID[FIN-C], Helen talks about what she lost. In @ID[FIN-D], the episode never airs: the recording cuts off when someone knocks at the door. In @ID[FIN-B], Helen says nothing at all: she puts out the Crown Hill episode, and @ID[PJ-01|David] knows someone has asked her not to talk. If @ID[PJ-04|Nadia] is a PC, she's at the mixing board.

## Coda 3 · The Hangar

*For any ending. Essential if @ID[PNJ-05|Bear] was taken hostage, if there was a raid, or if @ID[PJ-01|David] made a deal with Halloway.* Location: the Harbor Island hangar (@ID[LUG-01]).

:::leer
The hangar smells of gun oil, salt, and reheated coffee. Bear is at his workbench, cleaning a pistol that's already clean. He doesn't look up. On the bench, next to the parts, lies a notebook page folded in quarters, worn thin at the creases. Bear looks at it, then at David, then goes back to the pistol. “There's coffee,” he says. “If you want.”
:::

**Notes:** the page is @ID[DOC-12]. Bear took it out of the safe. What happens depends on the adventure: if David crossed a line (@ID[FIN-B], a massacre, Dominate on Helen), Bear hands it to him to read. If not, he puts it away again without a word and pours him the coffee. Both options are the right ending.

## The Letter

*To close the final session, after the coda you choose.*

:::leer
Three weeks later, an envelope with no return address arrives at the hangar, postmarked Cluj-Napoca, Romania. Heavy, yellowed paper. Inside, a single handwritten line, in handwriting David knows better than his own: *“There are two. You already know. Finish the lesson.”* And below it, two initials: *M. V.*
:::

End of *The Ward File*. The chronicle continues in @ID[GAN-10].
