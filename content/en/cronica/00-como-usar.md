# How to Use This Module

**Core adventure:** *The Ward File* (@ID[AVE-01])

**System:** Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition. The Second Inquisition (FIRSTLIGHT) is a latent presence in the plot.

**City:** Seattle and Puget Sound, November 2026. Main areas: Elliott Bay and Harbor Island, Pioneer Square and the Seattle Underground, Capitol Hill, Ballard, the Eastside, Tacoma, and Everett.

**Play format:** a coterie of 4–5 players with the five PCs (@ID[PJ-02] to @ID[PJ-06]). David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]) and Tom “Bear” Whitman (@ID[PNJ-05]) are allied NPCs who travel with the coterie.

## Premise

Seattle is the city that knows the most about its residents: every phone, every doorbell camera, and every delivery algorithm leaves a trail. Prince Marcus Whitmore's Camarilla survives thanks to the rain, to indifference, and to Sheriff Montgomery and his Hound, David Smith, erasing everything that shouldn't be there. One night, Helen Ward's hard drive is stolen from an apartment in Ballard: ten years of notes, blurry photos, and geographic patterns. Without anyone meaning for it to be, it is the most complete map in existence of the city's hunting grounds and Masquerade breaches. At the same time, Kindred start turning up destroyed on the docks, killed with the technique of *Controlled Fury*, the doctrine David learned from a sire presumed dead since 1985. If the Ward File reaches the Second Inquisition, the city burns. If it reaches the Primogen, someone gains a weapon to bring down the Prince. And if the coterie recovers it, they'll have to read what the file says. And decide what to tell David.

## Themes

- **The Masquerade as debt:** every secret that gets protected is paid for by a mortal.
- **Fathers and sons in the Blood:** Vance and David, David and Tom, David and Emily.
- **Surveillance:** in the tech city, the predator is also prey to the data.
- **Order versus conscience:** does the enforcer serve the law or his own code?

## Tone

Rainy, tactical *noir*, with personal horror. Few words, long silences, and brief violence that has consequences. The threat is cold and procedural: drones, reports, and databases.

## Estimated Length

Core adventure: 6–8 sessions. Chronicle hooks: 12 more, to extend the campaign to 15–25 sessions.

| 9 · Appendix | Handouts (AYU-xx), image prompts, and the Foundry VTT manifest | Storyteller |

## Conventions {jugadores}

All cross-references use stable identifiers, which don't change between versions of the book and which become links in Foundry VTT:

- **PJ-xx** player characters · **PNJ-xx** Storyteller characters · **MEN-xx** minor NPCs
- **LUG-xx** locations · **COT-xx** coteries · **LOR-xx** Loresheets · **GAN-xx** hooks
- **DOC-xx** in-world documents · **AYU-xx** handouts
- **A1-E03** scene 3 of Part 1 · **P-xx** clues · **FIN-x** endings

Within each scene, the text is always split into three types of block, which become separate elements in Foundry:

| Block | What it is | In Foundry |
|---|---|---|
| 📢 **Read Aloud** | Short text to read or paraphrase to the players | Journal page visible to players |
| 🕯️ **Storyteller Notes** | Context, secrets, tests, options, and consequences | Storyteller's private journal page |
| 🩸 **Stats** | Dice pools, Attributes, Disciplines | Actor |

Tests are always written the same way: **Wits + Awareness (Difficulty 3)**, followed by what a success reveals.
