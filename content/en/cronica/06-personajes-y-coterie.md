# Player Characters and Coterie

*For 4–5 players using the five new concepts (@ID[PJ-02] through @ID[PJ-06]); with four, one goes unused. David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]) and Bear (@ID[PNJ-05]) are NPC allies of the coterie. These are concepts with background; the players build the mechanical character sheet with the core rulebook. Sections marked “Secret” are for the Storyteller only.*

## Which Clans I Recommend and Why

The coterie has no Brujah: the city already has four, all of them NPCs (David, who travels with the coterie, Rosa, Krell, and O'Connor), so it's best not to repeat the clan. Each clan below opens a different front of the plot, so everyone gets their moment.

| Clan | Plot Front | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Ventrue** | Politics and infiltration of Halloway's inner circle | Puts the coterie inside the antagonist's house. Creates the strongest loyalty dilemma in the adventure. |
| **Nosferatu** | Information, the underground, and the surveillance network | Seattle is the city of data, and a Nosferatu hacker is the natural counterweight to Gale Sentinel and FIRSTLIGHT. |
| **Toreador** | The Prince's court and Helen's circle | With a Toreador Prince, this character lives at the center of the coup. They can also be tied to the podcast. |
| **Gangrel** | The Sheriff's pack, tracking, and combat | Connects with Montgomery and with David's past. Gives weight to the scene where the Sheriff hesitates (@ID[A3-E04]). |
| **Tremere** *(alternative)* | Forensics, blood sorcery, and the crack in the Masquerade | With five players, they join the table. With four, they can replace any of the others. |

**Recommendation for 4 players:** Ventrue, Nosferatu, Toreador, and Gangrel. The Tremere stays in reserve. **For 5:** all five.

## The Coterie: “The Archived” (COT-00) {jugadores}

Every character appears in the Ward File. When the drive is stolen, they're all exposed at once, and Sheriff Montgomery wastes no time telling them: “You're all on the same drive. You get it back together or you go down together.” It's a *Commando* coterie, with its domain on the Harbor Island docks, around the hangar David lends them as a haven, and each player receives their own folder (@ID[AYU-09]).

The full coterie page (concept, alternatives, members and agendas, customs, type, domain, and shared Backgrounds, with what doc 02 says and what doc 08 settles) is at @ID[COT-00|The Archived].

## Adjusting the Outline for a Coterie

- **David and Bear are NPCs.** They help: +1 die to the PC who rolls, or an action you narrate without a roll. They don't solve on their own what falls to the coterie, and they don't make its decisions; they give opinions, push, react. David's story (Vance, Emily, Helen, the Larchmont, Krell) is uncovered by the PCs, and they're the ones who decide what to tell him.
- **@ID[A0-E01]:** Helen calls SAFE; David summons the coterie. Nadia, if she's in play, arrives on her own: she knows the apartment.
- **@ID[A1-E03]:** becomes the coterie's formation scene. Montgomery gathers them all at his farm and assigns David to go with them; David lends them the hangar.
- **@ID[A3-E01]:** Krell goes after the most exposed Touchstone of any character (Helen, Bear, Maja, Theo, Ingrid…), not just Bear or Helen.
- **@ID[A3-E03]:** Krell demands David. The coterie decides whether to bring him and how. The steps toward Krell's turn are earned by the PCs through what they tell him; David confirms, breaks down, or gets angry.
- **@ID[A3-E04]:** if Hal is in play, the scene turns against him: Montgomery asks for his reports in front of everyone.
- **@ID[A4-E01]:** if Celeste is in play, Halloway offers her up as a traitor. If Nadia is in play, Whitmore uses her as his voice before the Council.
- **Scaling the tests:** the outline's Difficulties stay the same; scale the enemies by adding one minor NPC for each player beyond two.
