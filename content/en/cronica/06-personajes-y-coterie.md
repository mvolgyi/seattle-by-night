# Player Characters and Coterie

*For 4–5 players: David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]) and 3 or 4 new characters. Bear (@ID[PNJ-05]) remains an NPC. These are concepts with background; the players build the mechanical sheet using the core rulebook. Sections marked “Secret” are for the Storyteller only.*

## Which Clans I Recommend and Why

David is already the group's Brujah, and the city has three more Brujah NPCs (Rosa, Krell, and O'Connor), so it's best not to repeat the clan. Each clan below opens a different front of the plot, so everyone gets their moment.

| Clan | Plot front | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Ventrue** | Politics and infiltration of Halloway's circle | Puts the coterie inside the antagonist's house. Creates the strongest loyalty dilemma in the adventure. |
| **Nosferatu** | Information, the underground, and the surveillance network | Seattle is the city of data, and a Nosferatu hacker is the natural counterweight to Gale Sentinel and FIRSTLIGHT. |
| **Toreador** | The Prince's court and Helen's circle | With a Toreador Prince, this character lives at the center of the coup. They can also be tied to the podcast. |
| **Gangrel** | The Sheriff's pack, tracking, and combat | Connects to Montgomery and to David's past. Gives weight to the scene where the Sheriff hesitates (@ID[A3-E04]). |
| **Tremere** *(alternative)* | Forensics, Blood Sorcery, and the crack in the Masquerade | The fifth option if there are five players, or a substitute for any of the above. |

**Recommendation for 4 players:** David, Ventrue, Nosferatu, and Toreador. **For 5:** add the Gangrel. The Tremere stays in reserve.

## The Coterie: “The Archived” (COT-00) {jugadores}

Every character appears in the Ward File. When the drive is stolen, they're all exposed at once, and Sheriff Montgomery wastes no time telling them: “You're all on the same drive. Either you get it back together or you fall together.” It's a *Commando* coterie, with its domain on the Harbor Island docks, around David's hangar, and each player receives their folder (@ID[AYU-09]).

The full coterie page (concept, alternatives, members and agendas, customs, type, domain, and shared Backgrounds, with what doc 02 says and what doc 08 settles) is at @ID[COT-00|The Archived].

## Adjusting the Outline for a Coterie

- **@ID[A0-E01]:** Helen calls SAFE; David summons the coterie. Nadia, if present, arrives on her own: she knows the apartment.
- **@ID[A1-E03]:** becomes the coterie's formation scene. Montgomery gathers them all at his farm.
- **@ID[A3-E01]:** Krell goes after the most exposed Touchstone of any character (Helen, Bear, Maja, Theo, Ingrid…), not just Bear or Helen.
- **@ID[A3-E04]:** if Hal is in play, the scene turns on him: Montgomery demands his reports in front of everyone.
- **@ID[A4-E01]:** if Celeste is in play, Halloway offers her up as the traitor. If Nadia is in play, Whitmore uses her as his voice before the Council.
- **Scaling the tests:** the outline's Difficulties stay the same; scale the enemies by adding one minor NPC for each player beyond two.
