# The Truth and the Adventure Outline

*Phase 2. Working document for the Storyteller. All cross-references go by ID.*

## The Truth Behind the Adventure

**Charles Halloway** (@ID[PNJ-03]), the Ventrue Primogen, wants the crown of Seattle. He can't bring down Prince Whitmore (@ID[PNJ-02]) while Sheriff Montgomery (@ID[PNJ-01]) and his Hound, David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), keep the city clean. His plan has three pieces:

- **The File.** His ghoul Preston Gale (@ID[PNJ-19]), owner of a home camera and security company, locates the archive of Helen Ward (@ID[PNJ-04]) through his own customers' data. A team of mortals steals it.
- **The Ghost.** In 1986, Halloway bought the journals of Marcus Vance (@ID[PNJ-06]), which were never burned. With them he hires August Krell (@ID[PNJ-12]), a Brujah whom Vance trained and abandoned in the '70s. Krell destroys the Kindred who could expose the theft, imitating *Controlled Fury*. That gets Halloway two things: David chasing a dead man, and Montgomery starting to distrust him.
- **The Leak.** Halloway hands the Second Inquisition (FIRSTLIGHT) selected fragments of the File. They only point at Whitmore's and Montgomery's assets. When the raid comes, he'll convene the Primogen to demand a regent “capable of protecting the Masquerade”: himself.

**Vance is not in Seattle.** Every sign of him is false. What is real is Krell, the other son, the one who wasn't chosen.

## FIRSTLIGHT's Thermometer (AYU-03)

It's a 0-to-10 tracker that measures how much the Second Inquisition knows. It starts at 2. It rises with Masquerade breaches, public violence, contact between Helen and FIRSTLIGHT, and the fragments Halloway leaks each night the plot advances unanswered. It drops when the coterie cleans up traces, recovers copies of the File, or neutralizes the mole.

| Threshold | What happens in the city |
|---|---|
| 3 | FIRSTLIGHT puts Helen under surveillance: vans, drones, and an analyst who listens closely to her podcast. |
| 5 | Agent Dana Okafor (@ID[PNJ-18]) contacts Helen and offers her protection in exchange for the archive. |
| 7 | They investigate one of David's mortal assets (Bear's contacts or the hangar's cover). Resources or Haven at risk. |
| 9 | The raid, @ID[A4-E02], triggers. |
| 10 | Purge. At least one Camarilla haven falls and the city enters a Masquerade crisis for the rest of the chronicle. |

## Adventure Skeleton

**Length:** 6–8 sessions. **Format:** a coterie of 4–5 players with the five PCs; David and Bear accompany it as allied NPCs.

**Structure:** Prologue and four parts. Parts 1 and 2 can be played in any order. The city moves on its own: each unanswered night, Halloway leaks a fragment (Thermometer +1) and Krell takes another step.

## Prologue—“Rain over Ballard”

- **@ID[A0-E01] · The Call from SAFE.** It's 3:19 a.m. Helen calls the only number on her flip phone: someone has broken into her apartment (@ID[LUG-05]). The coterie arrives before the police, with David and Bear. Tests: tracking, cleaning up the scene, and handling Helen. *Exits:* @ID[A1-E01].

## Part 1—“A Trail of Water”

- **@ID[A1-E01] · What They Took.** Helen's apartment. The thieves were mortal professionals, and the building's cameras were put on a loop (P-01). Helen makes a list of what was stolen (@ID[AYU-02]). There's a smell of foreign Vitae on the doorknob: it belongs to a ghoul (P-02). *Exits:* @ID[A1-E02], @ID[A1-E04], @ID[A1-E05].
- **@ID[A1-E02] · Ash on Pier 46.** A minor Nosferatu turns up destroyed on Harbor Island, half a mile from David's haven. The blows follow the doctrine of *Controlled Fury* (P-03). On the body there's a USB drive with a partial copy of the File (P-04). Witness: @ID[DOC-06]. *Exits:* @ID[A1-E03].
- **@ID[A1-E03] · The Sheriff Asks.** At Montgomery's farm (@ID[LUG-02]). He recognizes the technique and asks what nobody wants to hear: “Is your sire alive?” He gives David and the coterie three nights, and assigns David to go with it. If the Thermometer is at 4 or higher, Montgomery is in a worse mood. *Exits:* @ID[A2-E01], @ID[A2-E04].
- **@ID[A1-E04] · Everything Sells Below.** In the Underground (@ID[LUG-04]). Thornquist confirms there are copies of the File on the market and that the courier is a thin-blood, Jun (P-05). *Branch:* rescuing Jun opens @ID[GAN-11]; if the coterie doesn't, Krell destroys her on Night 4. *Exits:* @ID[A2-E02], @ID[A3-E02].
- **@ID[A1-E05] · The City's Eyes** *(optional; skip it if the coterie doesn't follow the tech trail).* The doorbell cameras in Helen's building are all Gale Sentinel. The company received a data request that appears in no record (P-06). *Exits:* @ID[A3-E02].

## Part 2—“The Ghost of Marseille”

- **@ID[A2-E01] · Elysium Night.** At the Glass Gallery (@ID[LUG-03]). It's a social scene: Halloway, Marchetti, Voight, and Fry. Halloway mentions Vance with a detail he shouldn't know (P-07). Fry recites @ID[DOC-10]. Voight warns privately that FIRSTLIGHT has opened a file. *Exits:* @ID[A2-E02], @ID[A2-E04].
- **@ID[A2-E02] · “Locate. Assess. Execute.”** The second victim has Vance's motto written on the wall. A roll of @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.brawl|4|Intelligence + Brawl (Difficulty 4)} reveals that the technique is wrong: Krell strikes from the inverted guard, the way Vance taught the students who never finished their training (P-08). *Exits:* @ID[A2-E04], @ID[A3-E01].
- **@ID[A2-E03] · Salt's** *(optional).* In Everett (@ID[LUG-10]). Rosa Iturbe receives the coterie and David with hostility: Halloway has spread word that the Anarchs are behind the deaths. Rosa knew the Anarch who killed Emily. If David hears it, he breaks or explodes: the Storyteller plays him, and the coterie has to hold him together. *Branch:* an alliance with Rosa (allies in the climax) or a confrontation (@ID[GAN-06]). *Exits:* @ID[A2-E04].
- **@ID[A2-E04] · The Sheriff That Was.** In Tacoma, where O'Connor lives in exile. He says Vance's journals weren't burned: in 1986 they were sold to “a patient Seattle Ventrue” (P-09). He hands over @ID[DOC-08]. If the coterie approaches the Larchmont Building (@ID[LUG-11]), go to @ID[A2-E05]. *Exits:* @ID[A3-E01], @ID[A3-E02].
- **@ID[A2-E05] · The Ashes of the Larchmont** *(optional; depends on decision 5).* The burned building that marked Helen's career. The File has a folder on it. What the coterie finds depends on the truth about the fire; what it tells David depends on the players.

## Part 3—“The Two Hands”

- **@ID[A3-E01] · The Other Son.** Krell ambushes Bear or Helen (whoever is more exposed), wearing Vance's coat. It's a combat and chase scene. If the coterie arrives in time, Krell flees and leaves a message. If it doesn't, Bear is badly wounded or Helen is kidnapped. *Exits:* @ID[A3-E03].
- **@ID[A3-E02] · The Data Center.** Infiltration of Gale Sentinel (@ID[LUG-07]). The coterie finds the master copy of the File, the shipping log to “SEA-0411” (P-10), and the digital signature of Preston Gale, Halloway's ghoul (P-11). *Options:* stealth, Dominate on the guards, or an assault, which raises the Thermometer +2. @termometro{+2|A3-E02: assault on Gale Sentinel}
- **@ID[A3-E03] · Face to Face with Krell.** At the port's cargo terminal or wherever Krell chooses. Krell demands David; the coterie decides whether to bring him and how. Krell doesn't want to win: he wants David to see him. He offers to trade information for recognition. What softens him are the truths the PCs tell him, with David beside them. *Branch (the players choose):* destroy him, hand him over to the Sheriff, or turn him into a witness against Halloway (P-12).
- **@ID[A3-E04] · The Pack Doubts.** Halloway hands Montgomery “evidence” that David is protecting his sire. Montgomery summons the coterie and David. *Branch:* convince him (the PC who speaks for the coterie rolls Manipulation + Persuasion, Difficulty 4, +1 die for each clue from P-07 to P-15: @tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.persuasion|4|Manipulation + Persuasion (Difficulty 4)}), confront him, or flee. If it goes wrong, the coterie reaches the climax as fugitives, and David with them.

:::recuadro Editor's Note · Clues for A3-E04
The outline (doc 01) gave +1 die for each clue from P-07 to P-12. Part 3 (doc 12) expands the list to P-07 through P-15 and adds other modifiers (Krell as a witness, the Sheriff's favor, the lie from A1-E03): doc 12 wins. See @ID[A3-E04].
:::

## Part 4—“High Tide”

- **@ID[A4-E01] · Primogen Council.** In the penthouse of Halloway Tower (@ID[LUG-06]). Halloway presents his motion against the Prince. The coterie can interrupt it with evidence, stay silent, or negotiate privately beforehand.
- **@ID[A4-E02] · The Raid** *(triggers if the Thermometer reaches 9).* FIRSTLIGHT assaults a target from the File: the Sheriff's farm (@ID[LUG-02]) or David's hangar (@ID[LUG-01]). The coterie has to escape, save Bear, and destroy evidence.
- **@ID[A4-E03] · The Last Ferry.** At the terminal (@ID[LUG-15]). Halloway tries to get the original copy out of the city, to hand it to the Justicar or to FIRSTLIGHT. This is the final showdown.
