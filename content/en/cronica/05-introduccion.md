# Chapter 1 · Introduction

## What This Book Is About

Seattle is a city that watches itself all the time. It has cameras in its doorbells, phones in every pocket, apps that know what time each neighbor leaves the house, companies that turn that information into money, and agencies that turn it into files. For decades, the Seattle Camarilla survived thanks to three things: the rain, which makes people indifferent; a Toreador Prince who prefers appearances to war; and a Gangrel Sheriff who erases whatever's left over without asking too many questions.

This book describes that city at the moment it stops working. It presents the Kindred who rule it and those who want to rule it, the places where people hunt and deal, the rumors that circulate through Elysium, and a complete adventure, *The Ward File* (@ID[AVE-01]), in which the characters discover that their existence is recorded on a mortal journalist's hard drive, and that someone stole it to use it.

The book is designed for a coterie of 3 to 4 new characters alongside David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), the Sheriff's Brujah enforcer. It also works for a single player with Bear (@ID[PNJ-05]) as a companion, and the sidebars explain how to adapt each part.

## Themes and Mood

### The Masquerade as Debt

Every secret the Kindred keep is paid for by a mortal: the mother who will never know what her son died of, the journalist whose story got killed, the medical examiner who signs false reports. In this chronicle, protecting the Masquerade has a price, and someone collects it. When the characters make a decision “for the Masquerade,” ask yourself who pays for it, and show it.

### Parents and Children in the Blood

Vance and David. David and Bear. Halloway and his childe, Montgomery and his, the Prince and his. And Emily, David's daughter, who died in the '90s. The adventure revolves around what gets inherited: techniques, loyalties, guilt.

:::secreto
August Krell (@ID[PNJ-12]) exists so that David can see what he might have become if Vance hadn't chosen him.
:::

### The City That Records Everything

In this Seattle, the predator is also prey. Not to hunters with stakes, at least not at first, but to data: a light that goes out on a doorbell, a bill paid by card, an eleven-second video. The Second Inquisition (FIRSTLIGHT) is present throughout the adventure, but almost never in plain sight. It's measured with the Thermometer (@ID[AYU-03]), which rises with every mistake. Use it to make the players feel the air closing in without anyone firing a shot.

### Order Versus Conscience

The characters work, one way or another, for Camarilla law. The adventure asks them what they do when the law is used for something their code won't accept. There's no right answer, and the four possible endings reflect that.

### Seattle in Three Senses

To describe the city without repeating yourself, keep these images handy:

- **Sight:** glistening asphalt, cranes with red lights over the port, office windows lit at three in the morning, the little blue light of a doorbell camera.
- **Sound:** rain on sheet metal, the hum of an electric car, the foghorns on the Sound, the silence of an empty bus.
- **Smell:** salt and diesel on the docks, wet wool, burnt coffee, the copper-coin smell of Vitae.

## Quick Seattle Glossary

| Term | What It Is |
|---|---|
| Ballard | Northwest neighborhood, an old Scandinavian fishing village. Bars, shipyards, new apartments over old shops. Helen lives there. |
| Harbor Island | Artificial industrial island in Elliott Bay. Containers, cranes, and David's hangar. |
| Pioneer Square | The oldest neighborhood. Beneath it lie the buried 19th-century streets: the Seattle Underground. |
| Capitol Hill | Nightlife, music, clubs. The Toreador *rack*. |
| South Lake Union | Glass towers of the tech companies. At night, empty offices with the lights on. |
| The Eastside | Bellevue and Redmond, across Lake Washington. New money and data centers. |
| The Sound | Puget Sound, the arm of the sea that surrounds the city. Ferries, fog, and cold water. |
| Everett / Tacoma | The cities to the north and south. One for the Anarchs, the other for the exiles and what's left of the Sabbat. |
| FIRSTLIGHT | The American branch of the Second Inquisition. In Elysium they say it has an office in Seattle; nobody knows where. |
| The File | Helen Ward's archive. Ten years of deaths that don't add up. |
