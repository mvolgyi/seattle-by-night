# Chapter 6 · Chronicle Hooks

The city doesn't stop when *The Ward File* ends. These twelve hooks let you continue the chronicle after the adventure, fill the nights between parts, or give each character a story of their own. They're grouped by theme.

Every hook uses the same format: **Cast** (who appears), **What Happened** (the starting situation), **What Could Happen** (two or three outcomes with mechanical consequences), and **Focus** (which kind of Attributes it tests).

## GAN-01 · The Boy Who Survived {#GAN-01}

**Theme:** Humanity

**Cast:** Marcos Delgado (mortal, 45), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Hal Brekke (@ID[PJ-05]), analyst S. Hale (@ID[PNJ-21]).

**What Happened:** in 1996, Marcos was fifteen and in the Rainier Valley apartment the night Ray Dorsey killed Emily Smith. He was the only kid who survived. Before he passed out, he saw a man with a scar on his face shoot Dorsey and kneel beside the social worker. Today Marcos is a youth counselor in the same neighborhood. He's spent thirty years looking for that man to thank him.

Now someone else is looking for him. Analyst Hale, cross-referencing fragments of the File, found the 1996 folder: an underage witness who talked about “a man who didn't bleed.” Hale is going to visit Marcos next week.

**What Could Happen:**

- **David gets there first and lets himself be seen.** Marcos recognizes him thirty years later, unchanged. David can make him a new Touchstone, and the loss of Emily hurts a little less (+1 die on the next Remorse test). But Marcos now knows something, and FIRSTLIGHT is going to ask him about it.
- **Hal wipes the memory.** Hal already cleaned up that scene once. He can do it again, this time with Dominate or Presence. The Masquerade stays safe, but taking from Marcos the one memory that made him who he is costs 1 Stain.
- **Hale gets there first.** Marcos tells her everything. FIRSTLIGHT's Thermometer rises 2, and the Inquisition has a composite sketch of David. @termometro{+2|GAN-01: Hale gets there first}

**Focus:** Social.

## GAN-02 · The Night She Beholds the Beast {#GAN-02}

**Theme:** Humanity

**Cast:** Helen Ward (@ID[PNJ-04]), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Nadia Ferro (@ID[PJ-04]).

**What Happened:** sooner or later, Helen sees David do something no man can do. It can happen during @ID[A3-E01], if Krell goes after her, or on any later night: a steel door torn off its frame, a three-story fall he gets up from, eyes that shine in the dark. She doesn't see everything, but she sees enough.

**What Could Happen:**

- **She pulls away.** Helen stops answering, moves, turns off the flip phone. She's still alive, but David loses his Touchstone. Remorse test at the end of the story.
- **She doubles down.** She's finally found something real. Helen demands answers and threatens to publish if she doesn't get them. Someone has to negotiate with her: @tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.persuasion|4|Manipulation + Persuasion (Difficulty 4)} to keep her quiet without using Disciplines. Using Dominate costs 1 Stain.
- **She starts investigating who she's been helping.** Helen begins cross-referencing the dates of SAFE's messages with her cases and finds the Larchmont. If Nadia feeds her information, the discovery comes sooner. If she gets to the bottom of it, the relationship changes forever (see @ID[LUG-11]).

**Focus:** Social and Mental.

## GAN-12 · The Visited Grave {#GAN-12}

**Theme:** Humanity

**Cast:** David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]) and a person of the Storyteller's choosing.

**What Happened:** for the past few months, someone has been leaving fresh flowers every Sunday on Emily Smith's grave (@ID[LUG-12]), wrapped in newspaper. David never leaves flowers. Nobody else should know that grave matters.

**Who Leaves the Flowers?** The Storyteller picks one option, depending on where they want to take the chronicle:

- **Marcos Delgado** (@ID[GAN-01]). The most human option: a tie to the past that threatens no one.
- **Rosa Iturbe** (@ID[PNJ-11]). It's her way of paying a debt she's never confessed. She's been doing it since 1996, but only recently started going in person.
- **Someone who signs “M. V.”** The flowers come with a card bearing those initials. See @ID[GAN-10].

**What Could Happen:**

- **David watches the grave and finds whoever leaves the flowers.** The conversation that follows can earn a new Conviction or break an old one.
- **David lets it go on.** He doesn't ask. He gains +1 die on Remorse tests as long as the flowers keep coming.
- **Someone uses it against him.** If FIRSTLIGHT discovers the grave, they have a place to wait for the Hound.

**Focus:** Social.

## GAN-03 · Marked Blood {#GAN-03}

**Theme:** Hunger

**Cast:** Dana Okafor (@ID[PNJ-18]), Ezra Lund (@ID[PJ-03]), Ruth Abernathy (@ID[PJ-06]), the dockside thin-bloods.

**What Happened:** FIRSTLIGHT has put a batch of blood bags into circulation on the port's black market. The blood carries a chemical marker that makes the Vitae of whoever drinks it glow under ultraviolet light for 48 hours. The Puget Cell installs discreet UV lamps at three known hunting grounds and waits.

**What Could Happen:**

- **The coterie catches it in time.** Ruth can analyze a bag with @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.science|4|Intelligence + Science (Difficulty 4)}. Ezra can trace the purchase of the lamps. If they pull the batch, the Thermometer drops 1 @termometro{-1|GAN-03: the marked blood is pulled} and the coterie earns a favor from Thornquist for protecting the thin-bloods.
- **Someone drinks.** If a PC drank from a bag in the batch, any UV lamp gives them away for two nights. Thermometer +1 for each scene in which they're seen. @termometro{+1|GAN-03: a marked PC seen under UV light}
- **The thin-bloods fall.** Three thin-bloods are captured in a single night. Thornquist demands revenge or compensation, and Rosa offers them refuge in Everett.

**Focus:** Mental.

## GAN-11 · Jun's Fast {#GAN-11}

**Theme:** Hunger

**Cast:** Jun Takeda (@ID[PNJ-20]), any PC who rescued her, Rosa Iturbe (@ID[PNJ-11]), Abel Thornquist (@ID[PNJ-13]).

**What Happened:** if Jun survived the adventure, she doesn't want to deliver packages anymore. She wants to learn to hunt without leaving a trace and without hurting anyone. She asks the PC who saved her to teach her. She's been feeding badly for weeks: expired bags and bites from sleeping strangers. She's at Hunger 3 almost all the time.

**What Could Happen:**

- **Mentor.** The PC teaches her. Jun becomes a loyal Allies 1 (or Retainers 1). But if she makes a mistake in public during the first story, the Masquerade suffers and the Thermometer rises 1. @termometro{+1|GAN-11: Jun's public mistake}
- **Everett.** The PC sends her to Rosa. Jun joins Salt's, the coterie gains an ally in Everett (+1 to relations with @ID[COT-03]) and loses a contact in the Underground.
- **Nobody helps her.** Three weeks later, Jun turns up destroyed in a doorway, just like when she was Embraced. Every PC who knew her makes a Remorse test.

**Focus:** Social and Physical.

## GAN-04 · The Old Teammate {#GAN-04}

**Theme:** Beast

**Cast:** Tom “Bear” Whitman (@ID[PNJ-05]), Cole Ransom (mortal, former SEAL), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Dana Okafor (@ID[PNJ-18]).

**What Happened:** Cole Ransom served with Bear on the same SEAL team. Now he works as a private contractor on the FIRSTLIGHT Tactical Team (@ID[MEN-02]), though he believes it's a Department of Homeland Security counterterrorism unit. He runs into Bear at a bar by the port. He recognizes him. And he notices something odd: Bear moves like he did at twenty-five, and the wrecked knee doesn't limp.

*Note: this hook doesn't use the rationing of Bear's Vitae, which is excluded.*

**What Could Happen:**

- **Double agent.** Bear keeps up the friendship with Ransom and pumps him for information about the Puget Cell. The coterie gains a source inside FIRSTLIGHT (temporary Contacts 2). Bear has to lie to a brother-in-arms every week. If Ransom finds out, it ends all at once.
- **The Beast decides.** Ransom gets too close to the hangar. David has to decide what to do with him. If he kills him, 2 Stains, and Bear never looks at him the same way again. If he makes him a ghoul, Bear knows exactly what's in store for his friend.
- **Ransom reports.** Ransom tells Okafor about Bear. Thermometer +2. @termometro{+2|GAN-04: Ransom reports} Bear is marked and can no longer move around the port by day without being followed.

**Focus:** Social and Physical.

## GAN-05 · Moon over the Snohomish {#GAN-05}

**Theme:** Frenzy

**Cast:** Ethan Montgomery (@ID[PNJ-01]), Hal Brekke (@ID[PJ-05]), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Dr. Lucy Harlan (Montgomery's Touchstone).

**What Happened:** after the events of @ID[AVE-01], Montgomery is at his limit. He's gone weeks without feeding properly, under a kind of pressure he hasn't known in a century. One night under a full moon, a group of hikers gets lost near the farm. One of them cuts himself on a barbed-wire fence. The Sheriff smells the blood.

**What Could Happen:**

- **The coterie contains him.** They have to restrain a Blood Potency 5 Gangrel in full frenzy without destroying him. It's a long, dangerous fight. If they pull it off, Montgomery owes them a major favor, and he won't forget it.
- **Hal faces his sire.** Hal is the only one Montgomery's Beast might recognize. A @tirada{attributes.charisma+skills.animalken|5|Charisma + Animal Ken (Difficulty 5)} roll can calm him. If it works, Hal earns the recognition he's been looking for. If it fails, Hal ends up badly hurt.
- **Someone dies.** If the hikers die, Montgomery loses 1 point of Humanity and the farm's cover is compromised. If the victim is Dr. Harlan, who came to help, he also loses his Touchstone.

**Focus:** Physical.

## GAN-06 · Blood Hunt in Everett {#GAN-06}

**Theme:** Hierarchy

**Cast:** Tavi Mercer (@ID[MEN-05]), Rosa Iturbe (@ID[PNJ-11]), Marcus Whitmore (@ID[PNJ-02]), Ethan Montgomery (@ID[PNJ-01]), the coterie.

**What Happened:** Tavi Mercer, Salt's impatient Brujah, attacked Club Cinder (@ID[LUG-09]) or the area around Elysium. It may have happened during Part 4 of @ID[AVE-01] or afterward. Mortals were hurt. The Prince calls a Blood Hunt on her and, if he's feeling strong, on “anyone who shelters her.” Rosa refuses to hand her over.

**What Could Happen:**

- **The coterie hunts Tavi.** They carry out the Prince's order. They gain +1 Status (Camarilla) and lose any relationship with Salt's. If it was Halloway who pushed Tavi, the coterie has done his dirty work.
- **The coterie negotiates.** Rosa hands Tavi over in exchange for the Hunt not reaching Everett. The Prince has to be convinced with @tirada{attributes.charisma+skills.politics|4|Charisma + Politics (Difficulty 4)} in Elysium, and Rosa with @tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.persuasion|4|Manipulation + Persuasion (Difficulty 4)}. If they succeed, nobody's happy, but nobody dies.
- **The coterie protects Tavi.** If they prove Halloway manipulated her, the Hunt is called off. If they can't prove it, the PCs are branded as accessories: −1 Status for everyone.

**Focus:** Social.

## GAN-07 · The Archon Calls {#GAN-07}

**Theme:** Hierarchy

**Cast:** Archon Duval (@ID[PNJ-07]), Justicar Panhard (@ID[PNJ-08]), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Celeste Arden (@ID[PJ-02]).

**What Happened:** Mr. Pell typed three copies of Vance's journals. Copy 1 is in Halloway's vault, and Krell has copy 2. Halloway sent copy 3 to Justicar Panhard in 2025 as a gesture of goodwill. Now Archon Duval, who knew Vance, has read it and wants to talk to David. He asks him to travel to Marseille. It isn't an order. Yet.

**What Could Happen:**

- **David travels.** Duval shows him copy 3. It has folios that aren't in the other two, including the ones about Krell. David earns an Archon's favor (temporary Mawla 2), but he's away from Seattle for one story, and the city keeps moving without him.
- **David refuses.** Duval doesn't press, but he takes note. Next time it will be an order. Celeste, if she's in play, receives a parallel invitation from the Justicar, whom Halloway told about her.
- **Panhard takes an interest in Seattle.** If the ending of @ID[AVE-01] was @ID[FIN-B] (a pact with Halloway), the Justicar sends Duval to the city to investigate. He arrives with one question: who's the Prince now?

**Focus:** Social and Mental.

## GAN-08 · The Viral Video {#GAN-08}

**Theme:** Masquerade

**Cast:** any PC, Ezra Lund (@ID[PJ-03]), Helen Ward (@ID[PNJ-04]), Nadia Ferro (@ID[PJ-04]).

**What Happened:** an eleven-second video recorded by a Gale Sentinel doorbell shows one of the PCs moving too fast: crossing a street in under a second, or clearing a ten-foot fence without touching it. Someone posted it on social media. It has 400,000 views in a day and is still climbing. A mystery podcast asks Helen to discuss it live.

**What Could Happen:**

- **Erase the trail.** Ezra can trace the source and take the video down with @tirada{attributes.intelligence+skills.technology|5|Intelligence + Technology (Difficulty 5)}. It can't be erased from the internet, but it can be buried. If they manage it within 48 hours, the Thermometer doesn't rise.
- **Tell a different story.** Nadia can fabricate a plausible explanation: a fake, a viral ad, an actor with effects. It takes @tirada{attributes.manipulation+skills.performance|4|Manipulation + Performance (Difficulty 4)} and media Contacts. The video becomes a joke.
- **Helen talks.** If Helen goes on the podcast, she says what she thinks. If the PCs haven't talked to her first, the Thermometer rises 2 and the video becomes “the case of the year.” @termometro{+2|GAN-08: Helen talks}

**Focus:** Mental and Social.

## GAN-09 · The Larchmont Survivor {#GAN-09}

**Theme:** Masquerade

**Cast:** the survivor (@ID[MEN-08]), David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), Ruth Abernathy (@ID[PJ-06]), Liam O'Connor (@ID[PNJ-17]), the Hecata family of Tacoma.

**What Happened:** in 2014, the third Kindred of the Larchmont pack escaped through the drain into the Sound. He's spent twelve years hiding in Tacoma, feeding carefully, never drawing attention. O'Connor watches him from a distance. Now people are disappearing in Tacoma again: two homeless people in a month, a night-shift nurse. Ruth gets the bodies. They're missing blood.

**What Could Happen:**

- **David finishes the job.** He finds him with O'Connor's help and destroys him. David gets back some of what he lost in 2014: he can make a Remorse test with +1 die. And before he dies, the survivor tells him something about the seven tenants that David didn't know.
- **Ruth covers it up.** Ruth signs off on the three deaths as overdoses. The Masquerade is safe for now, but FIRSTLIGHT already had “R. A.” on its list: the Thermometer rises 1 for every false report. @termometro{+1|GAN-09: false report from “R. A.”}
- **The Hecata family.** Tacoma's Hecata know where he is and want something in return: for someone to open the Larchmont cistern and hand over the seven bodies so they can be properly buried. That means telling Helen about Danny Ruiz, or at least risking that she finds out.

**Focus:** Physical and Mental.

## GAN-10 · Letters from Cluj {#GAN-10}

**Theme:** Blood

**Cast:** David Smith (@ID[PJ-01]), August Krell (@ID[PNJ-12]), and whoever signs the letters.

**What Happened:** weeks after the end of the adventure, an envelope with no return address arrives at the hangar, postmarked Cluj-Napoca, Romania. Inside is a single sheet of paper with a single line, handwritten in Marcus Vance's hand: *“There are two. You already know that. Finish the lesson.”* Signed: *“M. V.”*

**Who Wrote It?** The Storyteller decides based on what they want to do with the chronicle:

- **Vance is alive.** He survived in 1985, changed somehow, and he knows what happened in Seattle. This opens an international arc in which David will have to decide whether to look for his sire or leave him buried.
- **It's Krell.** If Krell survived the adventure, this is his last play: he wants David to believe their father is still alive so they'll go looking for him together.
- **It's the Justicar.** Panhard read copy 3 of the journals (@ID[GAN-07]) and wants to know what the Hound does when he thinks his sire is calling him.

**What Could Happen:**

- **David ignores the letter.** He burns it. He gains 1 point of temporary Willpower. The letters keep arriving every February 14.
- **David answers.** He sends a reply to the address on the postmark. What comes back depends on who wrote it.
- **David looks for Krell.** If Krell is alive, David finds him and shows him the letter. The conversation between Vance's two sons can turn Krell into an ally or a permanent enemy.

**Focus:** Mental and Social.
