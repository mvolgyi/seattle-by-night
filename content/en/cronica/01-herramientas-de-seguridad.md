# Safety Tools

This chronicle deals with hard subjects: the death of a daughter, gun violence, addiction, and the exploitation of young people. Before you begin, run a session zero with these tools.

## General Content Warnings

Death of a daughter and grief. Gun violence and hostages. Exploitation of at-risk teenagers and drugs. Addiction (Bear's dependence on Vitae). Post-traumatic stress and war guilt. Surveillance, paranoia, and loss of privacy. Blood drinking and mental coercion. No gratuitous gore.

## Content Warnings for ***The Ward File***

| Part | Sensitive content |
|---|---|
| Prologue and Part 1 | Breaking and entering, surveillance, violent death of a Kindred, overdose deaths (mentioned) |
| Part 2 | Memory of Emily Smith's murder, a fire with casualties, grief, mental coercion |
| Part 3 | Ambush, kidnapping or serious injury of a loved one, Vitae dependence |
| Part 4 | Armed raid, betrayal, possible loss of a Touchstone |

## Lines and Veils

**Lines** are content that never appears at the table. **Veils** are content that happens off-camera, with a fade to black. We recommend starting from this baseline and having each player add their own in private:

| Suggested lines | Suggested veils |
|---|---|
| Sexual violence · harm to minors · detailed torture | Emily's death (told in memories, never in detail) · prolonged feeding · Bear's withdrawal |

## The X-Card

Put a card with an X on it in the middle of the table, or an icon in Foundry. Anyone can tap it without explaining why, and the scene is cut or rewritten. No questions, no discussion.

## Session Zero Checklist

- Read @ID[CAP-00] aloud or send it out beforehand.
- Go over the content warnings and the Lines and Veils.
- Introduce “The Archived” (@ID[COT-00]) and create the characters from the concepts @ID[PJ-02] through @ID[PJ-06] or from scratch.
- Have each player choose what their folder in the File (@ID[AYU-09]) contains: a photo, a video, a pattern. The Storyteller writes it in Helen's voice.
- Define one Touchstone per character who could appear in Part 3.
- Explain FIRSTLIGHT's Thermometer to the players. They should know it exists, even if they don't see the number.
